# Source guide for the latest discussion

This is a guide to the public evidence used during the conversation through 22 September 2026. The archive operation did not perform a new full market study. External pages may change after this snapshot; old reports preserve their own citations and research dates.

## Manufacturing process

| Source | What it supports |
|---|---|

## India: operation, construction and policy

| Source and date | Evidence and limits |
|---|---|
| [MeitY, 17 September 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2311726&lang=1&reg=3) | Five commercial units within twelve approved programme projects; major Tata facilities still under construction. This is a programme count, not a count of all historical Indian semiconductor facilities. |
| [Micron Sanand, 28 February 2026](https://micron.gcs-web.com/node/50101) | Commercial assembly/test and first shipment; inputs include DRAM/NAND wafers from global factories. Does not establish Indian HBM or GPU wafer manufacturing. |
| [Tata foundry description](https://www.tataelectronics.com/semiconductor-foundry) | Planned Dholera fab, 28–110 nm analog/logic technologies and planned capacity. Capacity is not actual output. |
| [Government backgrounder, 17 September 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2311220&lang=1&reg=48) | Semicon 2.0 programme outlay and market forecasts. Programme allocations are not expenditure already disbursed; market projections are not buyer orders. |
| [Official Gazette, 31 August 2026](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/275860.pdf) | New-project support categories and conditions. The relevant provisions were read directly from the Gazette during research. Older 50% sanctions must not be substituted for the newer rates. |
| [NITI Trade Watch, February 2026](https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-02/Trade-Watch-Quarterly-July-September-Q2-FY-2025-26.pdf) | Calendar-2024 integrated-circuit imports under HS 8542. This is not total electronics imports or chips embedded in imported finished products. |
| [Lam India](https://www.lamresearch.com/careers/global-opportunities/india/) | Indian engineering and global procurement activity. Procurement administered in India is not equivalent to local manufacturing content. |

## Demand and supply pressure

| Source and date | Evidence and limits |
|---|---|
| [Micron prepared remarks, 24 June 2026](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q3/Q3-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf) | Reported DRAM/NAND demand exceeding supply; future duration of tightness is management's forecast. Revenue growth partly reflects prices and is not equivalent to physical volume growth. |
| [TSMC transcript, 16 July 2026](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/encrypt/files/encrypt_file/reports/2026-08/3e494f0c14dd0890f897aa044415e21d93486cc4/TSMC%202Q26%20Transcript.pdf) | Packaging constraint and shortages in selected AI power-management chips/sensors. Does not establish shortages in every mature-node or power-chip category. |
| [IBIDEN Q&A, 5 August 2026](https://www.ibiden.com/ir/items/en_QA_FY2026Q1.pdf) | Reported package-substrate shortage and strong demand for large, complex substrates. This does not prove the same shortage in ordinary PCBs. |
| [Infineon results, 5 August 2026](https://www.infineon.com/press-release/2026/infpr202608-125) | Strong AI power demand; some capacity agreements concluded and others still under negotiation. |
| [NVIDIA transcript, 26 August 2026](https://investor.nvidia.com/files/content_files/TRANSCRIPT_-NVIDIA-Corp-NVDA-US-Q2-2027-Earnings-Call-26-August-2026-5_00-PM-ET.pdf) | AI systems supply constraints and memory scarcity reported by management. Does not establish demand for a particular prospective Indian supplier's product. |
