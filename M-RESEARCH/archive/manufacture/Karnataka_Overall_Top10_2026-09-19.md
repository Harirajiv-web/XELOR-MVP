# Overall Karnataka starting-business ranking

Decision date: 19 September 2026. This combines the earlier industrial, EV and semiconductor screens for the user's new objective: a first manufacturing business based in Karnataka, with meaningful Indian manufacturing and reduced import dependence. The sector-specific lists remain useful comparisons, but this is the current overall starting order.

Assume the user's best-case access to financing, an experienced technical team and introductions. The historical INR 1–30 crore envelope is a planning reference, not reconfirmed capital. Rankings are qualitative judgments, not projected returns. No buyer interviews, RFQs, equipment quotations or customer commitments were obtained.

1. **Precision moulds and tooling for electrical/electronic components.** Start with demanding connector-housing moulds, relay-bobbin tooling and replacement inserts. This combines broad official import evidence with Karnataka connector manufacturing and a manageable customer qualification unit: one paid tool. Tool design, machining, inspection and repair are the local value. Avoid interpreting broad tooling imports as the exact market size of this niche.
2. **Specialised production and test machines.** Start with one battery-pack end-of-line station or one electrical-component assembly/test process, owning fixtures, controls, diagnostic software and integration. Karnataka engineering and service proximity are useful. Imported measurement instruments and motion components may remain. A general automation offering without a defined process/customer is weaker.
3. **Precision parts for semiconductor equipment.** Selected machined parts, brackets, housings and approved mechanical assemblies are the strongest direct semiconductor entry for this starting context. Bengaluru has equipment engineering and component qualification activity. Initial orders could be exports through global equipment supply chains; export growth is distinct from replacing current Indian fab imports. Contamination-sensitive chamber internals require additional processes and qualification.
4. **Custom power magnetics.** Make transformers, inductors and chokes for converters, chargers, UPS and industrial drives. Karnataka has relevant power-conversion manufacturers, creating a broader customer route than EV-only products. Target a specific imported finished component; specialised cores can remain imported. Existing domestic suppliers mean design, quality, delivery and support must differentiate the offer.
5. **Engineered high-voltage interconnects and PDU/BDU assemblies.** Manufacture current paths, insulation, enclosures and validated assemblies for nominated commercial-EV/battery platforms. Use approved purchased contactors/fuses initially. Local two-wheeler companies are not automatically buyers of high-voltage commercial-vehicle products. Vehicle safety and platform qualification require an experienced design authority.
6. **Semiconductor test-interface hardware.** Begin with selected socket housings, device-holding fixtures, handler conversion parts and test-board assemblies. Bengaluru's testing/validation ecosystem supports engineering access; it is not proof of high-volume local OSAT orders. Imported contacts/connectors and advanced boards may remain. Keep this product distinct from complete battery/electrical factory machines at rank 2.
7. **Application-specific BMS.** Develop/licence the electronics and software for a buyer's actual cell chemistry and duty cycle. Bengaluru's embedded engineering ecosystem is useful; ARAI's stated TRL8 platform is an exploratory technology route, subject to current terms and customer-specific validation. Compete against established domestic firms through proven application performance. Imported control/monitoring chips remain initially.
8. **Qualified cold plates and coolant manifolds.** Manufacture thermal hardware for locally assembled power electronics and nominated EV platforms. ABB's converter/drive operations and Schneider's Bengaluru cooling activity strengthen possible customer access. Neither establishes an unsupplied imported part. Validate joining, leak performance, cleanliness, corrosion and life against a nominated specification.
9. **Specialised medium-voltage cast-insulation components.** Select one customer-approved epoxy part and voltage family for switchgear. Karnataka provides CPRI qualification infrastructure, but the relevant buyer programme may be elsewhere in India. ABB's announced 33 kV GIS expansion is in Nashik, not Bengaluru. Ordinary supports and advanced GIS spacers are different technical scopes.
10. **High-purity gas-delivery assemblies for semiconductor equipment/plants.** Start only with an experienced technical partner and a customer-approved inert-gas tube/panel assembly. Local welding, cleaning, testing and documentation create value; valves, regulators and controllers may still be imported. Major fab/EPC buyers may be outside Karnataka. This remains a conditional first-business candidate because of qualification and customer-access demands.

## Karnataka location and network

Prefer a leased Bengaluru/Peenya unit for engineering, qualification and early production. Compare Dabaspet and Tumakuru for the production site once the anchor buyer, recruitment, utilities, logistics and process requirements are known. Bengaluru Urban and Nelamangala/Dabaspet are Zone 3; Tumakuru taluk is Zone 2; Gubbi is Zone 1. Classification follows the plot's actual taluk. Do not automatically stack Industrial, ESDM, Clean Mobility and central subsidies.

Use the stated H. D. Kumaraswamy connection for engineering/sourcing introductions and institutional access, including CMTI where relevant. Karnataka authorities separately handle state land, incentives and local approvals. The highest-value milestone is a paid tooling or machine-development order, or a formally sponsored semiconductor-part qualification programme. No outreach was conducted.
