# India manufacturing entry: pharma and adjacent alternatives

Research cut-off: 11 September 2026. Adapted to a first-time entrepreneur around Bengaluru with ₹1–30 crore available. All recommendations, capital bands and sequencing below are analyst judgments, not vendor quotations or promised returns. Source dates are distinguished from dates of underlying data. No company named below has been contacted; prospect lists are not confirmed orders.

## Recommendation

My strongest candidate within this assignment is **industrial filtration and hygienic fluid-handling equipment**, beginning with stainless-steel filter housings, replacement filter elements, dosing modules and narrowly specified clean-in-place (CIP) skids for food, non-sterile pharma and industrial water customers. Select one application and three to five products after customer interviews, rather than opening a general fabrication shop. A sanitary component/OEM subcontracting route can come before selling complete systems. It benefits from pharma growth without requiring the founder to discover a drug, win a drug dossier, or compete on commodity molecule cost.

For Bengaluru, this is credible but competitive: Sartorius already engineers and manufactures stainless components, fermentation/bioreactor equipment, filtration systems and syringe filters locally. That is evidence of an established ecosystem, skilled labour and demanding competitors—not an empty import-substitution market. Its October 2024 description is a useful benchmark for capabilities a newcomer must gradually build. [Sartorius, 1 October 2024](https://www.sartorius.com/en/company/newsroom/blog/this-is-sartorius-bangalore-1617624).

The likely durable advantage is documented quality plus local turnaround: material traceability, weld records, surface-finish verification, pressure/leak tests, replaceable parts, on-site commissioning and prompt service. Low-priced catalogue housings or generic RO assemblies alone have little protection. Higher-margin sterile filters, membrane chemistry and single-use bioprocess bags require much more materials science, validation and customer qualification; they are later possibilities, not the opening promise.

## APIs: large strategic need, difficult first-factory economics

India imported ₹36,124 crore of the listed APIs/KSMs/drug intermediates in FY2024–25, of which China supplied ₹27,032 crore—**74.8% by import value**, calculated from the official table. This does **not** mean 74.8% of every medicine ingredient consumed in India is imported. It does identify concentration risk. The same release names bulk-drug parks in Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat and Himachal Pradesh; none is Bengaluru. [Department of Pharmaceuticals, 10 March 2026; FY2024–25 trade data](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2237413&lang=1&reg=1).

More revealing than the subsidy headline is the gap between installing capacity and commercialising it. The August 2026 parliamentary reply reports 48 approved PLI projects, ₹5,070.45 crore actual investment through March 2026, and capacity for 28 APIs/KSMs/intermediates; ten products had not reached commercial production. Only 18 listed products had commenced production. This is not a profitability statistic, but it is strong evidence against equating factory completion with a functioning business. [Department of Pharmaceuticals, 7 August 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2295928&lang=1&reg=1).

Competition can hurt on both sides of the cycle. In June 2025, reporting described falling API prices amid Chinese overcapacity and aggressive pricing. By March 2026, reporting described disrupted solvent/feedstock supply and higher costs associated with West Asia conflict, including some production stoppages. These are dated examples of volatility; neither quote is a September 2026 supplier price. Build a business that survives lower selling prices *and* higher input prices. [Economic Times, 5 June 2025](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/healthcare/biotech/pharmaceuticals/api-prices-fall-sharply-easing-pressure-on-indias-pharmaceutical-industry/articleshow/121656709.cms); [Economic Times, 25 March 2026](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/healthcare/biotech/pharmaceuticals/rising-costs-of-key-chemicals-choke-production-at-drug-ingredient-makers/articleshow/129785958.cms).

DGFT's January 2026 measure introduced minimum import prices on Penicillin G-potassium, 6-APA and amoxicillin trihydrate for one year; below-threshold imports are restricted, with exemptions for specified export-linked routes subject to conditions. Such protection demonstrates policy concern, but a plant with a multi-year payback cannot assume a temporary price floor will persist or cover its products. [Government Trade Connect summary, 4 February 2026; notification dated 29 January 2026](https://content.trade.gov.in/TCP-CMS/15178725000930448/3080000217/DGFT%20Introduces%20Minimum%20Import%20Price%20Restrictions%20on%20Key%20Antibiotic%20Inputs.pdf).

At ₹30 crore, a focused synthetic intermediate operation or small specialised API facility may be technically possible under favourable site and process assumptions. That does not fund a broadly competitive integrated fermentation business. The correct diligence unit is a **specific molecule, route, scale and buyer**, including reaction yield, solvent recovery, impurity removal, batch failure rate, energy/water, containment, effluent disposal, analytical laboratory, quality staff and working capital. A low reactor quotation omits much of the project.

GMP requirements are operating systems, not certificates bought at launch. FDA's API guidance covers supplier qualification, independent quality responsibilities, equipment/process qualification, batch records, cleaning validation and controlled changes. Established customers may have to assess changes in source/process before buying. India conditionally extended revised Schedule M compliance for qualifying smaller existing manufacturers only to 31 December 2025; a new 2026 project should be designed to current requirements rather than depending on that expired transition. [FDA Q7, September 2016](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/q7-good-manufacturing-practice-guidance-active-pharmaceutical-ingredients-guidance-industry); [MoHFW, 12 February 2025](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2102291&lang=2&reg=48).

Environmental liabilities are product-specific. CPCB's monitoring guidance requires consented products and addresses pharmaceutical residues, contaminated discharges and waste; it also provides for greater effluent recycling. Obtain written site/process advice on environmental-clearance applicability, pollution-control consent, hazardous-waste permissions and disposal capacity before fixing the land or route. A permitted industrial estate does not automatically permit every reaction. [CPCB monitoring guidance, official document accessed 11 September 2026](https://www.cpcb.gov.in/uploads/Guidelines_monitoring_mechanism_API_residues.pdf).

**My API entry gate:** an experienced process/QA co-founder; a genuinely differentiated synthesis, impurity or delivery advantage; paid evaluation and a credible qualification plan with at least two customers; validated pilot data; reliable feedstocks; a fully costed environmental solution; and enough liquidity to survive qualification delays. Test the route at an existing licensed contract plant before building. Outsourcing R&D or toll manufacturing can lower initial asset exposure but does not remove quality accountability, IP risk, customer audits or dependence on the contractor. Buy a small compliant operation only after inspecting historic data integrity, environmental liabilities, customer retention and actual usable approvals.

## Export and international risk

Do not treat all US pharmaceuticals as one tariff category. The 2 April 2026 White House fact sheet imposed tariffs on patented medicines and associated ingredients with company/country exemptions, while generics, biosimilars and associated ingredients were then excluded. Subsequently, news reported planned generic tariffs beginning August 2028. Treat that later announcement as a policy risk rather than a verified current tariff payable today; exact product classification and implementation need checking when quoting. A first business dependent on one US generic buyer carries avoidable concentration risk. [White House, 2 April 2026](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/04/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-bolsters-national-security-and-strengthens-u-s-supply-chains-by-imposing-tariffs-on-patented-pharmaceutical-products/); [Axios, 28 July 2026](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/28/trump-generic-drug-tariffs-price-strategy).

China-plus-one interest can create customer conversations, but does not confer orders on an unqualified new supplier. Currency depreciation helps only the net export exposure: imported reagents, membranes, resins, instruments and machinery can offset it. Require currency/commodity adjustment clauses where negotiable, more than one approved source and a realistic inventory policy. AI assists engineering quotation, records retrieval, scheduling and inspection; it cannot replace chemical process validation or release a regulated batch without accountable controls. These are analytical implications, not market-size forecasts.

## Comparison of accessible alternatives

The bands below are **screening estimates for an initial leased-facility phase, including a working-capital provision, excluding purchased land; uncertainty can readily exceed ±50%**. They describe narrow versions of each business, not full sector entry.

| Candidate | Indicative initial funding | Strength | Principal reason it fails |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Industrial filter housings, selected filter elements and fluid-handling modules | ₹3–8 crore | Repeat replacement demand; several end markets; service proximity | Undifferentiated assemblies, poor field performance, no local selling capability |
| Documented sanitary vessels/CIP skids/OEM components | ₹5–12 crore | Engineering and qualification can support customer retention | Design error, rework, long project collection cycles, established competitors |
| Specialised non-sterile medical/lab consumables or contract moulding | ₹3–10 crore | Repeat demand and manageable initial scale in a narrow product family | Commodity prices, costly tooling, wrong regulatory classification, distributor margin |
| B2B processed food ingredients/co-packing | ₹2–8 crore | Domestic repeat consumption; possible local sourcing | No procurement advantage, poor quality consistency, spoilage, receivables |
| Printed secondary pharma packaging/traceable labels | ₹3–10 crore | Recurring orders and comparatively simpler process | Fierce local competition and purchasing concentration; mistaken assumption that every pack is high-margin |
| Specialised KSM/intermediate or small API route | ₹15–30 crore or more | Technical differentiation and export potential | Qualification delay, environmental costs, imported inputs, molecule price collapse |

**Water/filtration:** the February 2026 AMRUT release reports 583 sewerage projects approved under AMRUT 2.0 worth ₹66,117.69 crore, covering 6,649 MLD treatment capacity. That supports sector activity, not a newcomer's order book. Prefer private industrial retrofit, replacement and local OEM supply initially; municipal EPC adds tender qualifications, guarantees and collection risk. [MoHUA, 5 February 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2223878&lang=1&reg=3).

A particularly relevant Bengaluru proof point is Sartorius's May 2026 report on site water reuse, rainwater harvesting and reclaimed water for production/utilities. It demonstrates an industrial customer addressing local water stress. Your product must prove lower total operating cost or greater reliability against incumbent suppliers; selling a generic sustainability story is insufficient. [Sartorius, 13 May 2026](https://www.sartorius.com/en/company/newsroom/blog/every-drop-tells-a-story-water-sustainability-at-sartorius-bangalore-1825128).

**Pharma equipment:** Praj's published offering includes purified-water systems, process vessels, CIP/SIP, sterile filtration, automation and after-sales services. It establishes what buyers already expect. A newcomer should enter a limited specification or subcontracted component, not promise a turnkey sterile biologics plant. [Praj HiPurity product catalogue, undated; accessed 11 September 2026](https://www.prajhipurity.net/knowledge-centre/product-catalogue/).

**Medical consumables:** non-sterile does not mean unregulated or automatically Class A. Intended use and the exact product determine classification. CDSCO's framework exempts qualifying non-sterile, non-measuring Class A devices from the full licensing regime while retaining registration and quality obligations. Its 2025 FAQ addendum also clarifies that an ISO 13485 certificate is not itself a universal precondition for a manufacturing licence under MDR; customers/export markets may separately require certification. Avoid blanket claims such as “all disposables need only ISO” or “every medical device needs ISO certification.” [CDSCO DTAB minutes, 10 May 2023](https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/resources/UploadCDSCOWeb/2018/UploadCommitteeFiles/Minutes%20of%2089th%20DTAB%20held%20on%2010.05.2023.pdf); [CDSCO FAQ addendum, official PDF accessed 11 September 2026](https://www.cdsco.gov.in/opencms/export/sites/CDSCO_WEB/Pdf-documents/Addendum-0152.pdf).

**Food processing:** favour contracted B2B ingredients with verified local procurement access, e.g. a specific blended/dried ingredient for institutional or branded-food buyers, over launching a broad consumer food brand. MoFPI identifies seasonal perishability and weak supporting infrastructure as structural constraints. Commercial success requires dependable raw material and buyers, not merely rising national consumption. Export foods add destination-specific residue/microbiology and traceability demands. [MoFPI Annual Report 2024–25, published August 2025](https://www.mofpi.gov.in/sites/default/files/mofpi_annual_report_2024-25_english_21.08.2025.pdf).

## Bengaluru prospecting and decision gates

Anthem's official facilities page identifies Bommasandra and Harohalli manufacturing, including chemistry, fermentation and newer peptide/high-potency capacity. Its contact page provides a vendor-registration channel. Biocon publishes Bengaluru manufacturing locations. Syngene describes local R&D, biologics and small-molecule activities. These are **prospects and ecosystem evidence**, not assurances that a new supplier can win work. Start with procurement/maintenance interviews and the OEMs already serving such plants; direct sales into critical drug-contact applications may come later. [Anthem infrastructure, undated](https://anthembio.com/infrastructure/); [Anthem contact/vendor registration](https://anthembio.com/contact-us/); [Biocon manufacturing locations](https://www.biocon.com/news-biocon/image-gallery-biocon/manufacturing-locations/); [Syngene facilities](https://instavisit.syngeneintl.com/labs/), all accessed 11 September 2026.

Before major machinery: interview 20–30 buyers across two end markets; obtain actual drawings/specifications, annual usage, current landed price, rejection reasons and payment terms; commission independent sample tests; deliver three paid pilot orders through outsourced fabrication; and then decide which process deserves ownership. Do not count letters of interest as sales. Seek milestone advances for custom skids and recurring spare-element supply, while avoiding uncapped performance guarantees.

Model cash as carefully as gross margin. At ₹12 crore annual revenue, **90 days of receivables alone equals about ₹2.96 crore**, before inventory and after-sales obligations; supplier credit offsets some working capital. This is simple arithmetic, not an industry norm. Run downside cases with sales delayed six months, prices down 10%, input costs up 15%, collections slower by 60 days and rework above plan. Spend only the first tranche needed to validate demand; availability of ₹30 crore is not a reason to install ₹30 crore of capacity immediately.

The ranking changes if the founder brings deep chemistry expertise and committed buyers: a narrow API/KSM can then outrank equipment. With unknown technical advantage, Bengaluru proximity and a desire for durability, the more defensible opening is precision, documented equipment and replenishment consumables serving several growing industries.
