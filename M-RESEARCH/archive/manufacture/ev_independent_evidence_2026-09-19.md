# Independent EV and electrical manufacturing evidence ledger

Checked: 19 September 2026. Prepared from government notices, company product pages and company filings; this assessment does not use the attached deck or its ranking as evidence. Working assumption: Bengaluru, experienced people and funding accessible through the founder's network, initial deployment broadly within INR 1–30 crore. Introductions are an advantage in obtaining technical discussions; they are not purchase commitments.

## Decision

The strongest electrical finalist is **customer-specific production machinery for electrical components: winding, assembly, calibration and high-speed end-of-line testing**. Start with one contracted process cell for a relay, solenoid or magnetic-component manufacturer. Battery-pack leak/electrical testing is a second market, with more visible Indian competition. This is capital-equipment manufacturing with software and fixtures, not merely a testing service.

The second finalist is **industrialising an existing rare-earth-free motor design under a licensed/JV/contract-manufacturing arrangement**, with the IP owner and an OEM jointly releasing the application. It has more direct strategic import-reduction logic than another generic busbar shop, but materially greater product-adoption risk.

The third finalist is **custom power magnetics**, conditional on a buyer identifying an imported transformer/choke assembly that existing Indian suppliers cannot competitively supply. Local winding alone leaves the core-material dependency intact. A specialist core-material venture is a separate, more demanding project.

**Charger rectifier modules should not retain an unconditional first rank.** There are already Indian module suppliers and vertically integrated charger companies. Localisation policy creates a qualification requirement, not proof of an unmet supplier gap. Proceed only with an anchor OEM, proven design rights, qualification ownership and a costed comparison with incumbent modules.

Confidence below means confidence in the recommendation based on public evidence, not a probability of commercial success. No reviewed source establishes an unfilled purchase order for a new entrant.

## Primary evidence and limits

### Exact import evidence: electrical machinery and materials

The [CEA consultation dated 12 December 2025](https://cea.nic.in/wp-content/uploads/notification/2025/12/12_12_2025_identified_critical_items_currently_being_imported_and_support_required_for_their_localization.pdf), Annexure II, PDF page 6, identifies bobbin winding/testing/inspection and high-volume latching-relay assembly automation. It also identifies specialised magnetic cores, precision injection tools, contact materials, coil-winding equipment and relay calibration/high-speed testing. Another portion lists semiconductor devices and DC capacitors. This is official product/process-level evidence, stronger than a broad EV market forecast. It supplies neither a quantified addressable market for a new entrant nor an award mechanism. Its consultation deadline has passed; it is not an open tender.

### 1. Production automation/testing: suppliers and a real manufacturing customer type

- [PM Electronics](https://pmelectronics.in/) advertises relay, transformer and solenoid production lines with winding, assembly and testing. Accessed 19 September 2026; undated manufacturer claim, direct page fetch intermittently timed out. Equipment origin, installed base and exact local value addition need plant diligence.
- [Ador Digatron: module/pack EOL](https://adordigatron.com/eol-testing-modules-packs/) offers integrated battery electrical, communication and leak-test functions. This establishes an existing specialist competitor/technology-partnership route, not an empty domestic market.
- [Cybernetik, 10 September 2026](https://www.cybernetik.com/blog/cleantech/battery-pack-end-of-line-testing-in/) describes battery-line EOL electrical/leak/BMS and traceability operations. Battery EOL is an adjacent application; it is not the same item as CEA's relay-production test machinery.
- [OEN 2025 corporate presentation](https://oenindia.com/uploads/userfiles/OEN_Corporate_Presentation_2025.pdf) shows automated winding, assembly and EOL relay production; its [contract manufacturing page](https://oenindia.com/contract-manufacturing-services) lists in-house tooling, automation and test capability. This proves the buyer/process exists and also that an incumbent may build equipment internally. It does not prove an external RFQ.
- [ICAT September 2025 GeM bid](https://icat.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/GeM-Bid-Document.pdf) is an example of public purchasing of a multichannel cell tester. Historical procurement, not a live lead; laboratory cell cycling is different from production pack EOL.

Production-line opportunity: replace an imported cell with equivalent cycle time, gauge repeatability, measurement traceability, changeover and service response. Field protective-relay secondary-injection kits are not interchangeable evidence for high-speed electromechanical relay factory testing.

### 2. Rare-earth-free motor industrialisation

- [Chara technology](https://www.chara.co.in/technology) describes a synchronous-reluctance platform with no permanent magnets; [solutions](https://www.chara.co.in/solutions) covers mobility and industrial products. These are company claims and design descriptions, not independently verified fleet durability or an available licence.
- [Matel company announcement](https://www.matel.co.in/nextinmotion) reports INR130cr Series B and motor/controller production, including magnet-free technology. Page undated; accessed 19 September 2026. This is evidence of an existing competitor and substantial funding, not a comparable cost for one licensed production cell.
- [Ola investor announcements](https://www.olaelectric.com/investor-relations/announcements) include its October 2025 ferrite-motor announcement. Ferrite means rare-earth-free magnets, not a magnet-free motor. The two approaches must not be conflated.

The proposed entry is manufacturing/industrialisation for an existing design owner with performance responsibility clearly contracted. It is not a new motor-research company claiming to solve the entire powertrain within INR30cr.

### 3. Magnetics and film capacitors

- [Exxelia SVM](https://exxelia.com/en/list/svm-bus-bars) documents Indian transformer/choke design, winding and test capability as well as busbars. Existing Indian supply means a particular unmet specification is essential.
- [Würth custom transformers](https://www.we-online.com/en/products/components/service/custom-transformers) demonstrates global competition in custom power magnetics. Indian manufacture of the exact offered product has not been established here.
- [Cosmo Ferrites](https://www.cosmoferrites.com/investor-relations/annual-report-notice) lists Indian soft-ferrite product families and EV applications. This prevents claiming all ferrite cores are absent domestically. A gap needs a material grade, loss curve, temperature range and geometry.
- [Exxelia Alcon](https://alconelectronics.com/en/alcon) identifies Indian manufacturing of polypropylene film and aluminium electrolytic capacitors. Its [DCL-6 product page](https://alconelectronics.com/en/products/capacitors/film-mica-capacitors/dc-link/dcl-6) and DC-link range show existing converter applications. Do not substitute this evidence for automotive qualification of every product or domestic origin of its dielectric film.

### 4. HV interconnects and connectors

- [Tata AutoComp TEVCS](https://www.tataautocomp.com/tevcs/) lists busbars alongside motor controllers, BMS and chargers.
- [Exxelia SVM busbar products](https://exxelia.com/en/products/svm-bus-bars) include laminated, coated and formed interconnects; [its manufacturing description](https://exxelia.com/en/list/svm-bus-bars) identifies local forming, insulation and joining capabilities.
- [Excel Metal Engineering, Peenya infrastructure](https://excelmetalengineering.com/infrastructure.php) describes local busbar processing and inspection. These three suppliers disprove a generic claim that India cannot make busbars.
- [Amphenol Industrial India factory](https://amphenol-industrial.com/our-company/global-manufacturing-capabilities/aipi/) lists EV/ESS-related connectors and cable assemblies with moulding, stamping, machining, crimping and testing. This establishes local manufacturing capability, not complete domestic content of every HV connector SKU.

The opportunity is an approved interconnect design plus process capability and repeat production. Generic bending/assembly has less defensible import-reduction content.

### 5. Thermal assemblies

- [Alkraft battery thermal management](https://alkraft.com/btms/) offers Indian designed/manufactured BTMS systems.
- [Tata AutoComp Air International](https://www.tataautocomp.com/taco-air-international/technology/) offers EV battery thermal-management systems.
- [Tata AutoComp supply-chain products](https://www.tataautocomp.com/supply-chain-solutions/segments/) include cold plates, heat sinks and battery components.

These show domestic capability. They do not reveal whether a particular automotive cold plate, microchannel process or manifold is currently imported. A proposed factory needs drawings, annual volume, approved joining route, leak limits and nominated customer before its import-saving claim can be quantified.

### 6. Charger modules: attempted disproof of first rank

1. **Already local:** [Ador/Yonder 30kW converter](https://yonderh2.com/30kw-converter/) states development/manufacturing in Pune; [Bacancy modules](https://bacancysystems.com/power-module) markets 20/30/40kW local products. Manufacturer claims still require factory/BOM verification.
2. **Large customers can integrate:** [Exicom Hyderabad announcement, March 2026](https://www.exicom.com/insights/press-releases/hyderabad-manufacturing-facility-inauguration-2026) describes its INR216cr integrated facility and planned local production of Tritium liquid-cooled power modules. The whole-facility cost is not a minimum cost for a small standalone module factory.
3. **International alternatives remain:** [Infypower catalogue](https://www.infypower.com/products?cid=30) and [UUGreenPower](https://www.uugreenpower.com/) offer multiple module families; the latter lists its EV India 2026 presence. Strong price competition is a reasonable inference. No comparable verified landed-price quotations or price-decline percentage were obtained, so none is asserted.
4. **Policy is not unique access:** the [18 July 2025 charger PMP](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/docs/policy_document/264796.pdf) specifies local AC/DC-converter manufacturing operations from December 2025 for scheme eligibility. It does not require Indian semiconductor fabrication and is not a universal module-import ban.
5. **Serviceability alone is insufficient differentiation:** domestic incumbents already market modular/maintainable products. A new entrant needs demonstrated OEM qualification, thermals/EMI performance, lifetime field cost and a supply/service commitment.

Go only if an OEM provides a nominated application, sample acceptance programme, volume/price framework and access to failure data; independently cost domestic assembly plus imported chips/cores, warranty reserve and collection delay. Buy or license a proven design where feasible. Starting simultaneously with chargers, traction inverters and onboard chargers would dilute this first phase.

### 7. Motor cores and wound subassemblies

- [Pitti FY26 investor presentation, 14 May 2026](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/PITTIENG_14052026201542_INVESTORPPT.pdf) describes an established integrated electrical-lamination/assembly business.
- [EuroGroup H1 2025 report](https://eurogroup-static.discoveryreplymedia.com/assets/105/11/a366cb6b-e3a6-4d16-ac62-b970147f2ffe/eb526a4b-de69-4e06-a530-e2dbaf195fed.pdf) covers its Kumar Precision Stampings acquisition in India. This is domestic core capacity, not proof every EV-grade rotor/stator is local.
- [Tempel company footprint](https://www.tempel.com/en/resources/tempel-mexico-expansion?hs_amp=true), 5 October 2023, includes India. The linked announcement concerns Mexico; its expansion cost/capacity cannot be assigned to India.

Preferred limited entry: customer-released wound/assembled product using qualified bought-in laminations. Avoid a commodity high-speed lamination factory unless tooling, electrical-steel supply and customer volumes are already secured.

### 8. Contactors/fuses

- [Schaltbau India](https://schaltbau.com/en/company/locations/schaltbau-india/) describes local manufacture including contactors for rail applications. This is not proof that all automotive sealed-HV contactors are made in India.
- [Sensata GIGAVAC products](https://www.sensata.com/products/gigavac-power-contactors-fuses/gv-contactors) establish a global competing family; its [product brochure](https://www.sensata.com/sites/default/files/a/sensata-gigavac-power-products-brochure.pdf) identifies US manufacture for the described range. Catalogue origin does not quantify Indian demand.
- [Mersen India DC protection](https://www.mersen.in/products/solutions-power-management/dc-protection-electric-vehicles-battery) markets EV/battery protection. An India sales page does not establish domestic production for each fuse.

A new sealed contactor involves materials, ceramic/metal sealing, arc control and endurance qualification; a fuse involves interruption and time-current behaviour. A contact assembly or licensed manufacturing step is a different, narrower opportunity from inventing a safety-critical full product family.

## Financial reality from filed company information

These are observations about existing mixed businesses, not forecast margins for a startup or isolated product.

- [Exicom FY26 release, 19 May 2026](https://www.exicom.com/insights/press-releases/exicom-delivers-its-strongest-quarter-of-fy26-as-both-businesses-return-to-sharp-growth): standalone revenue about INR895cr and EBITDA about INR70cr; consolidated revenue INR1,152cr with EBITDA loss INR103cr. Different business mixes and overseas integration explain why headline charger growth is not proof of easy profits.
- [Exicom filed earnings transcript, May 2026](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/EXICOM_21052026160328_Intimation__TranscriptSigned.pdf): management described inventory growth while two facilities operated during transition, and increased receivables. It also described input-cost/forex pressure on fixed-price critical-power contracts. The latter is not direct evidence of charger-module pricing, but supports careful escalation clauses and working-capital planning.
- [Servotech FY26 filed transcript, May 2026](https://www.servotech.in/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/SERVOTECH_07052026175543_NSETRANS.pdf), pages 3–4: management reported borrowings increasing from INR75cr to INR196cr, receivables from INR155cr to INR243cr, and negative operating cash flow. It attributed about INR40cr to OMC infrastructure-related payment delays and discussed railway commissioning-linked payments. The business includes solar, chargers, storage and EPC. Its quoted 60–70-day working-capital objective was a target, not an achieved result. Government-related orders can still tie up cash.
- [Pitti FY26 presentation](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/PITTIENG_14052026201542_INVESTORPPT.pdf) reports consolidated operating cash flow INR204.9cr versus INR288.5cr a year earlier, with INR125.2cr absorbed by working-capital changes. Established component manufacturing still requires cash beyond machinery. Do not transfer its mature company's margin to a new winding line.

For project equipment, negotiate design/development payment, long-lead-material payment, factory acceptance and site-acceptance milestones. For serial components, model metal/FX pass-through, approved inventory, payment delay and warranty reserves. Validate contribution after scrap and rework, not merely selling price less nominal BOM.

## Buyer access through the network

Use the H D Kumaraswamy connection to request focused supplier-development introductions through MHI, ACMA/SIAM and appropriate public enterprises. The useful meeting includes the buyer's engineering owner, sourcing owner and supplier-quality owner together. FADA's dealer network can illuminate operating problems, but usually does not own component procurement for vehicle manufacture.

Existing entry routes, checked 19 September 2026:

- [BHEL supplier registration](https://www.bhel.com/online-supplier-registration-portal) and [PEM vendor development](https://pem.bhel.com/Bhel_vendor_section.aspx): registration, item-specific qualification and tender participation. Select the relevant division/product; generic registration does not qualify every item.
- [Tata Motors supplier self-registration](https://srm.inservices.tatamotors.com/sap%28bD1lbiZjPTU1Ng%3D%3D%29/bc/bsp/sap/zros_self_reg/main.htm): product categories and supplier details; no application submitted.
- [Mahindra MSETU](https://supplier.mahindra.com/msetuLanding): supplier workflow covering sourcing/quality/commercial transactions. Existing portal functionality does not imply a public open RFQ.
- [ACMA](https://www.acma.in/about-us.php): supplier/OEM engagement route. Ask for specific localisation engineering discussions, not broad investment endorsement.

No currently open component RFQ naming the proposed startup was verified. Historical bids and supplier portals are evidence of purchasing mechanisms, not promised demand. No company was contacted.

## Exclusions and investment gates

Exclude as initial INR1–30cr projects: an integrated traction-magnet materials factory; a battery-cell gigafactory; a broad semiconductor-device fab; and a from-scratch catalogue of safety-critical HV contactors/fuses. Exclude generic AC charger assembly or commodity busbars from the shortlist solely on import-substitution rhetoric; they need a separate commercial case.

Before committing major equipment spend, obtain: (1) a named imported item/process with drawings and present landed cost; (2) a buyer's qualification and test plan; (3) two independent equipment/process quotes; (4) an explicit imported-input schedule; (5) a funded pilot or conditional sourcing nomination; (6) cash-flow stress testing for delayed approval/payment. A technology partner must grant the required production rights and engineering support, not merely supply an imported kit.

Recommended first discussions: one relay/magnetics production-equipment replacement specification; one rare-earth-free motor industrialisation mandate; and one custom power-magnetics assembly. Select the first factory from those actual specifications and commitments, rather than from sector-level subsidies alone.
