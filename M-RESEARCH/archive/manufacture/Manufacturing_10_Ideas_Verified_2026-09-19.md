# Manufacturing opportunity review — 19 September 2026

**Recommendation:** Under the requested best-case assumption of an experienced technical partner, adequate funding, suitable premises and strong introductions, investigate a focused Indian DC fast-charger power-module business first. Semiconductor-equipment precision parts rank second. Engineered interconnects and thermal assemblies are more practical alternatives when faster manufacturing entry matters more than owning a complete electronic product.

This is a ranking for choosing and validating one business. It is not a recommendation to establish ten product lines or commit capital before technical and commercial validation.

**Working assumptions and evidence limits**

- Bengaluru base; the historical ₹1–30 crore total funding range comes from the supplied handoff. The current user requested best-case resources but did not specify a new capital ceiling.
- The user's direct connection to H. D. Kumaraswamy is taken as stated. Other buyer relationships, land ownership and team commitments have not been independently established.
- Company product pages establish existing capabilities and competition. They do not independently prove advertised performance, open procurement requirements or an underserved market.
- Every funding range below is an analyst planning estimate for a focused operation in leased/available premises, including development, initial equipment/testing, launch staffing and working capital. Land purchase is excluded. Quotes, qualification scope and the time to repeat orders can change these ranges substantially.
- No narrow component-market size, import percentage, startup profit margin or payback period is asserted as verified.
- Attached handoff instructions were treated as historical context. Current user instructions determine this review's scope and ranking.

**How import substitution is assessed**

The commercial question is whether a customer can replace an identified imported product with a qualified Indian product at acceptable cost and performance. The engineering question is which design, process and manufacturing capabilities actually move to India. Imported machines or semiconductor devices do not erase all local value added, but assembling imported finished boards or kits creates much less capability than designing and manufacturing their functional subassemblies.

For each prospective order, document the imported SKU, supplier/country, specification, annual quantity, landed price, local substitute, residual imported inputs and buyer qualification requirements. Measure recurring foreign-input dependence separately from one-time imported machinery. Statutory domestic-value-addition calculations must follow the relevant scheme rather than an informal formula.

**1. DC fast-charger rectifier modules — strongest strategic choice under the best-case assumption.**

Make the power-conversion unit inside a charger, initially one agreed power rating and voltage range. Sell to charger manufacturers that buy modules; charging-network operators are useful for field feedback but may not control component procurement. Own or license the design with meaningful adaptation rights, use Indian PCB assembly, develop local firmware/test capability, and localise appropriate magnetics and mechanical parts. Semiconductor devices and selected capacitors, controllers and magnetic cores may remain imported.

The localisation route is real: the July 2025 PM E-DRIVE notification specifies domestic AC–DC module operations from 1 December 2025 for covered charging-station eligibility. It is not a universal import ban or a requirement to fabricate every semiconductor in India. [Official notification, Annexure 6](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/docs/policy_document/264796.pdf).

There is already competition: [Ador/Yonder's 30 kW module](https://yonderh2.com/30kw-converter/), [Bacancy's module range](https://bacancysystems.com/power-module), and [Exicom's March 2026 facility announcement](https://www.exicom.com/insights/press-releases/hyderabad-manufacturing-facility-inauguration-2026). A customer must explain why it needs another supplier. Better hot-weather performance, repairability or lifecycle cost are hypotheses to test, not proven advantages of the proposed venture. A technical partnership or acquisition may provide a better starting point than building every capability from zero.

**2. Semiconductor-equipment precision components — strongest semiconductor entry.**

Start with buyer-designed non-process-contact frames, brackets and mechanical assemblies. Add demanding vacuum or fluid components only after establishing the required process control. Buyers are equipment makers and their approved integrators; the fab itself does not necessarily source components inside an imported machine.

[Applied Materials' Bengaluru supplier role](https://jobs.appliedmaterials.com/job/bengaluru/supplier-account-manager-oem/95/92676364512) explicitly discusses development of an Indian domestic supplier ecosystem. [Lam's India operations](https://www.lamresearch.com/careers/global-opportunities/india/) support global procurement and hardware work. These are relevant channels, not purchase commitments. Exporting a component to an overseas tool factory earns export revenue but should not automatically be counted as replacing an Indian import. Advanced metrology, machine tools, coatings, seals and alloys may remain foreign dependencies.

**3. Engineered interconnects and laminated busbars — strong manufacturing alternative.**

Supply battery-pack and power-converter manufacturers with specified current-carrying assemblies, insulation and validated joints. Begin with one family; a battery cell-contacting system and a high-voltage laminated inverter busbar have different qualification needs. The engineering value lies in electrical/thermal behaviour, repeatable joints, insulation and traceability. [Mersen's EV products](https://www.mersen.com/en/all-markets/mersen-forefront-progress-electric-vehicles) demonstrate the category and the strength of established competition. Copper-heavy revenue alone is not attractive: metal price adjustment, scrap, rejected assemblies and cash tied up in inventory can determine profitability. The supplied 55–70% import estimates were not independently established.

**4. EV thermal assemblies — strongest simpler mechanical entry.**

Make coolant manifolds and valve blocks first, with cold plates added against specific demand. Sell to thermal-system and power-electronics manufacturers. Manufacturing locally replaces an import only where that actual assembly is currently imported; domestic outsourcing is also a business opportunity but is a different claim. [Pranav Vikas already offers battery/traction cooling and cold plates](https://pranavvikas.com/category/e-mobility-solutions/2). Pumps, sensors, quick disconnects and particular materials may remain imported. Automotive, AI-rack and semiconductor thermal/fluid systems require separate approval paths. A leak test at manufacture cannot guarantee lifetime freedom from leaks.

**5. Charging inlets and high-voltage connectors — attractive with specialist expertise.**

Choose one customer-approved vehicle-side inlet or connector family. The buyer is the vehicle manufacturer or its electrical-system supplier; a charging gun on a station is a different product. Localise contact manufacture, moulding, finishing control, seals, assembly and testing where feasible. Special polymers, contact materials, sensors and actuators may remain imported. [TE's charging-inlet catalogue](https://www.te.com/content/dam/te-com/documents/automotive/global/auto-hems-catalog-charging-inlets-3-1773984-7.pdf) illustrates the product scope and incumbent sophistication. Silver plating alone is not a defensible technology position. The deck's inlet-specific import share is unsupported, and installed buses in Bengaluru do not imply local component purchasing.

**6. Traction-motor cores and subassemblies — a programme-led opportunity.**

Make specified lamination stacks and stator/rotor core assemblies for motor manufacturers. Distinguish stamping sheet into laminations from stacking purchased laminations: investment and local value added differ. High-grade electrical steel and permanent magnets can remain import dependencies. The September 2026 truck amendment gives buyers a concrete localisation schedule, but does not prove unallocated supplier business. [Official truck PMP amendment](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/docs/policy_document/4th%20Amendment%20to%20PMP%20for%20N2%20and%20N3%20category%20of%20e-Trucks.pdf). Pitti's existing portfolio demonstrates substantial domestic competition. [Company annual report filed with BSE](https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachLive/6b3b799f-d3a6-4e13-99f2-05baa687d31e.pdf).

**7. High-frequency power magnetics — components sold across several equipment markets.**

Manufacture transformers, inductors and chokes to specified charger, inverter and power-supply requirements. Local value comes from magnetic design, winding, insulation, thermal control and production tests; specialty cores and insulation may remain imported. This is a distinct supplier business from the complete converter in idea 1. The [TIFAC technology roadmap](https://www.tifac.org.in/images/RD%20Roadmap%20Document%20on%20PEMD.pdf) discusses magnetic-component development needs. Its older absolute statements about national manufacturing status should not be treated as September 2026 statistics. The opening is a particular performance/availability gap, not merely having a winding machine.

**8. Battery-pack production test equipment — engineering and service value.**

Build a focused station that checks a finished battery pack's electrical connections, insulation, controls and specified leak performance, with results linked to serial numbers. Buyers are pack factories and their automation integrators. Develop local fixtures, controls, software, integration and service; measurement instruments and programmable power equipment may remain imported. [Chroma's production-test offering](https://www.chromaate.com/eu/chroma/pages/eu_battery_testing_solution) shows international competition, while [Cybernetik's end-of-line systems](https://www.cybernetik.com/blog/cleantech/battery-pack-end-of-line-testing-in/) show existing Indian capability. This is a machine-building business; it does not confer regulatory laboratory accreditation. Standardising the core platform is essential to avoid every sale becoming a wholly new engineering project.

**9. Semiconductor high-purity fluid/gas subassemblies — partner-led specialist route.**

Supply customer-approved tubing assemblies, manifolds and inert-gas panels to specialist semiconductor utility integrators and equipment builders. Buyers may be located outside Karnataka. Local welding/fabrication can replace imported assemblies, but valves, regulators, flow controllers and qualified tubing may remain imported. [Joscotec's gas/vacuum systems](https://www.joscotec.com/gasvacuum-system/) illustrate the mixture of local engineering and specialist component sourcing. Start within the experienced partner's qualified scope. Ordinary industrial piping and semiconductor process-gas equipment are not interchangeable.

**10. AI data-centre cooling distribution — credible adjacency, with the import gap still to be established.**

Manufacture specified facility-side stainless pipe sets, manifolds, frames and distribution assemblies for cooling OEMs or their site contractors. Imported AI racks can already contain chip cold plates and internal cooling hardware, so national data-centre expenditure is not the addressable budget for this product. [Schneider's Bengaluru Motivair factory](https://www.se.com/in/en/about-us/newsroom/news/press-releases/Schneider-Electric-launches-Motivair-liquid-cooling-solutions-factory-to-accelerate-India%E2%80%99s-highdensity-AI-infrastructure-699493fd07a7fbd2c30cbafa/) confirms local manufacturing and competition. It does not disclose an open supplier requirement. Pumps, couplings, controls and sensors can remain imported. This ranks below the EV opportunities because the user's stated network is less directly connected and site fabrication can already be local.

## Using the connection productively

The current [MHI directory](https://www.heavyindustries.gov.in/en/whos-who) identifies H. D. Kumaraswamy as minister. The practical advantage is obtaining serious technical and sourcing discussions quickly. Request introductions to ACMA/SIAM localisation teams, charger and vehicle manufacturers' engineering/procurement leaders, and the appropriate testing institutions. FADA can provide service and failure feedback, but dealers normally do not nominate production-component suppliers. Semiconductor opportunities require the MeitY/ISM, equipment-maker and specialist-integrator route.

Suggested meeting brief:

> We are evaluating a Karnataka manufacturing venture within a ₹1–30 crore initial envelope. We want to localise a specific imported EV or semiconductor-equipment product with a qualified technical partner. We seek discussions with engineering and procurement teams willing to share specifications, annual demand, the shortcomings of existing supply, and a paid qualification pathway.

The valuable meeting output is an identifiable product programme with purchasing authority, technical requirements and a commercial path. Introductions do not themselves establish orders, supplier approval or incentive eligibility.

**Next 60–90 days:** Select a technical lead; obtain substantive buyer meetings for ideas 1–4; secure actual drawings/specifications and price-volume requirements; compare two technology/production routes; obtain prototype and testing quotations; negotiate paid development, tooling ownership and IP rights; then choose one initial factory scope. A supplier acquisition or joint venture should be evaluated alongside greenfield construction. For semiconductor qualification, 90 days may establish the investment case rather than complete customer approval.

## Government schemes and location

MeitY's [18 September 2026 statement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2312207&lang=1&reg=1) supports the direction toward precision manufacturing and a wider semiconductor ecosystem. Scheme eligibility is a separate matter. The [31 August 2026 Semicon 2.0 Gazette](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/275860.pdf), pages 14–17, sets minimum capital investment of ₹300 crore for equipment/subassemblies/components, ₹50 crore for semiconductor materials and ₹100 crore for test/characterisation, alongside revenue and other conditions. The ₹1–30 crore supplier proposals cannot assume these direct grants. These are incentive thresholds, not legal minimums for operating a smaller supplier.

The supplied Karnataka zone image matches page 19 of the operative guidelines: Harohalli/Ramanagara are Zone 2; Magadi/Channapatna/Kanakapura Zone 1; the listed Bengaluru Urban/Rural taluks Zone 3. The policy applies its own machinery-investment bands for incentives. General-category small enterprises have capital subsidy caps of ₹2 crore in Zone 1, ₹1.5 crore in Zone 2 and ₹50 lakh in Zone 3 Bengaluru Rural; medium-enterprise rules differ. Benefits are conditional and disbursed after qualifying steps, not assumed startup cash. Compare the exact site, labour access, utilities and logistics before preferring a zone. [Official Karnataka guidelines, pages 6, 19 and 95–97](https://investkarnataka.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Karnataka.pdf).

Full semiconductor fabs, competitive large-scale OSATs, battery cells and integrated rare-earth-magnet plants require a different investment and technical platform. Pharma APIs, ordinary panels/enclosures and generic fabrication can be valid businesses, but the supplied information does not establish a stronger advantage for this founder than the ranked opportunities. A narrow semiconductor-test-fixture business is a reserve candidate if a real customer programme emerges.
