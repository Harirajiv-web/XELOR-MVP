# EV manufacturing: current policy, demand and a five-product shortlist

Checked 19 September 2026. Independent policy and demand research for the user's new EV-only assessment. Assumptions: Bengaluru base, access to an experienced team and introductions through H. D. Kumaraswamy; an initial ₹1–30 crore working envelope. That envelope is an assumption, not a confirmed budget. No outreach, applications or changes to the user's deck were made.

The scheme and its subcomponents remain limited by funds/vehicle ceilings and may close earlier. The L5 e-3W category already closed on **26 December 2025**. **Paid demand:** The 28 July parliamentary reply reports, as of 22 July 2026, reimbursement for 20,56,831 e-2W, 5,082 e-rickshaw/e-cart and 2,60,965 L5 vehicles: 23,22,878 vehicles and ₹2,281.94 crore in total. **Buses:** 14,000 allocated, with quantity-confirmation letters issued to successful bidders for 13,800; Surat and Hyderabad had signed concession agreements for 600 and 915 respectively. **Commercial inference:** Serve established 2W/3W platforms and nominated bus programmes where possible; treat trucks as an additional future application. OEM nomination and dated production schedules are better capacity-planning evidence than the scheme's headline ceiling.

## PLI: incentive recipient and supplier are different roles

PLI-Auto requires at least **50% domestic value addition** for incentivised products. As of 16 July 2026, 18 applicants had DVA certification for 154 products/variants; cumulative incentive disbursement to March 2026 was ₹2,386.36 crore. This shows real implementation, but does not establish that new applications are open or a new ₹1–30 crore firm will qualify. The practical route is to become an approved supplier to an eligible manufacturer and contribute documented domestic content. [21 July 2026 official status](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2287124&lang=1&reg=48).

For context, the March 2026 parliamentary committee report records existing automotive-component applicant thresholds of ₹500 crore global group automotive/component revenue and ₹150 crore global fixed assets. These are that applicant route's thresholds, not a complete account of every scheme category. It also describes legacy ACC terms of a 5 GWh minimum and substantial investment/DVA requirements. A recommendation to relax thresholds is not an implemented relaxation. [Standing Committee report, March 2026, PDF pp.31–32](https://sansad.in/getFile/rsnew/Committee_site/Committee_File/ReportFile/17/215/332_2026_3_14.pdf?source=rajyasabha).

**Important new ACC distinction:** The MHI RFP released **15 July 2026** concerns ACC for **grid-scale stationary storage**. It provides 1 GWh blocks, a maximum 4 GWh per bidder, ₹150 crore/GWh net-worth eligibility for the ordinary bidder route, and 25% value addition within two years rising to 40% within five years. Its stated bid deadline is 13 October 2026. These terms differ from legacy ACC tranches. It is neither an EV-cell startup grant nor within the assumed initial envelope as a standalone applicant. Component, machine and process suppliers may pursue commercial orders from actual cell manufacturers without being the PLI beneficiary. [Current RFP, pp.7, 9, 12, 32](https://heavyindustries.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-07/rfp_2.pdf).

## What current localisation rules really create

PMP rules specify domestic processes for vehicles/chargers seeking benefits under the covered programme. They do not ban every imported EV component across India, require Indian ownership, guarantee orders to new suppliers, or imply that all upstream materials/chips must be Indian.

| Rule checked | Domestic activity or deadline relevant to a manufacturer | Opportunity and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Bus base PMP, 3 March 2025 | CCS2 inlet contact-pin fitment, cable integration and housing fitment after six months. Battery-pack assembly includes cell interconnections, BMS, thermal and enclosure integration; imported finished modules are restricted as specified. | Manufacturing and test operations create supplier demand; the inlet requirement does not by itself require domestic manufacture of every pin, resin or plating input. |
| Truck base PMP, 10 July 2025 | CCS2 inlet's same three operations from 1 September 2025; domestic pack operations also prescribed. | Scope applies to qualifying N2/N3 programme vehicles, not every OEM purchase in India. |
| Truck amendment, 29 April 2026 | Local BMS, DC/DC and VCU PCB component assembly requirements from 1 September 2026; read against earlier local integration stages. | Opportunity includes locally engineered PCB assemblies, firmware and test; imported semiconductor devices remain possible. |
| Bus amendment, 3 September 2026 | Motor fitment of rotor/stator assemblies, bearings, enclosure, connectors/cables from 1 September 2026; magnet and shaft fitment from 1 April 2027. | This wording is not proof of a requirement to make magnet material or perform every upstream stator process in India. |
| Truck amendment, 3 September 2026 | Defined motor and inverter manufacturing stages extended to 1 April 2027, with earlier integration obligations retained where specified. | A localisation window for qualified suppliers, not evidence that incumbents lack compliant supply. |
| Charger PMP, 18 July 2025 | ACDC converter local PCB component assembly, wiring/crimping, heatsink/housing and firmware operations from 1 December 2025. | Makes local power-electronics manufacturing relevant. Imported chips and some cores remain; existing local module makers already compete. |

Official documents: [bus base PMP](https://www.heavyindustries.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-07/261454.pdf), [truck base PMP](https://www.heavyindustries.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-07/gazette_264519-e-trucks_dated_10.07.2025_0.pdf), [truck electronics amendment](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/docs/policy_document/272155.pdf), [September bus amendment](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/docs/policy_document/4th%20Amendment%20to%20PMP%20for%20e-buses%20M2%20M3.pdf), [September truck amendment](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/docs/policy_document/4th%20Amendment%20to%20PMP%20for%20N2%20and%20N3%20category%20of%20e-Trucks.pdf), [charger PMP](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/docs/policy_document/264796.pdf).

## Five specific opportunities justified by this policy/demand screen

This is a shortlist for customer qualification, not a claim that the government has identified five unsupplied markets. Ranking reflects breadth of existing demand, depth of locally controlled work, and ability to start with an anchor customer. All capex numbers below are **analyst planning estimates, not vendor quotations**, excluding land and a separately funded working-capital reserve.

| Rank and initial product | Why it belongs in the EV shortlist | Real Indian work / remaining imports | Buyer and entry condition | Initial scope estimate / confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Battery-pack assembly and EOL test stations**: leak, insulation/hipot, contact resistance, BMS/CAN and traceability in one production cell | Domestic pack operations serve current 2W/3W and bus plants. Can sell a customer-funded station before building a volume component factory. | Own fixtures, handling, electrical integration, test software and validation. Precision measurement instruments, PLCs/servo drives and selected sensors may remain imported. | Pack manufacturer's manufacturing engineering, plant quality and capex procurement. Obtain a paid station specification with cycle time, false-reject limit and acceptance protocol. | ₹3–8 crore for engineering/integration and demonstration capacity; medium-high on entry practicality, medium on verified EV-specific import substitution. |
| **2. Engineered HV power-distribution/interconnect assemblies** for pack-to-inverter and charging paths | Local packs and bus platforms need validated current paths. Broader platform applicability than a truck-only or single-inlet factory. | Busbar design, cutting/forming, insulation/overmoulding, joints, cable termination and electrical/thermal test. HV contactors, fuses, specialist connector inserts may initially be imported. | Battery Tier-1 or vehicle electrical architecture team owns design; sourcing and supplier quality approve nomination. Start one design-controlled PDU/interconnect family. | ₹5–12 crore with selected processes outsourced; medium confidence. Existing Indian supply means exact imported incumbent part must be identified. |
| **3. Battery/inverter cold plates and coolant manifolds**, initially one bus/LCV platform | Bus procurement is tangible, though deliveries lag allocations. Locally controlled thermal hardware can also serve existing passenger and commercial EV programmes. | Machining/forming, joining/brazing, cleaning, leak/pressure/flow testing and design validation. Pumps, control valves or imported alloy/specification inputs may remain. | Pack/thermal Tier-1 and vehicle thermal engineering; nominated sourcing team. Require drawing, coolant specification, corrosion criteria and lifetime validation. | ₹7–18 crore depending on joining process and outsourcing; medium confidence. No verified national import percentage. |
| **4. Industrialise a licensed rare-earth-free traction motor** for one 3W/LCV duty cycle | Genuine way to remove a vulnerable material from the design, supported by MHI's alternative-motor direction. Start with already proven IP and co-developed controls. | Winding, impregnation, rotor/stator mechanical work, assembly, balancing and dyno test. Electrical steel and inverter devices may still be imported. Avoid merely assembling an imported complete motor. | OEM powertrain engineering, technology owner and sourcing. Gate investment on matched motor/inverter efficiency maps, NVH, cooling and durability results in the chosen vehicle. | ₹10–25 crore for a narrowly scoped pilot/industrialisation line; medium-low until OEM and technology partner commit. |
| **5. Automotive power magnetics**: one qualified high-frequency transformer/choke family for onboard/DC/DC/charging conversion | Local electronics assembly still needs engineered passives. One component family can reach several power-electronics buyers. | Winding, insulation system, bobbins/fixtures, potting, thermal design and dielectric/partial-discharge testing. High-performance magnetic cores may initially be imported. | Power-electronics manufacturer's magnetics design engineer and sourcing team. Win a design-in against a named imported BOM line; general charger growth alone is insufficient. | ₹3–8 crore for scoped winding/assembly/test; medium confidence, lower on exact EV-specific import-gap proof. |

Evidence that these are contested markets: [Ador Digatron's battery EOL offering](https://adordigatron.com/eol-testing-modules-packs/), [Cybernetik battery EOL, 10 September 2026](https://www.cybernetik.com/blog/cleantech/battery-pack-end-of-line-testing-in/), [Tata AutoComp EV component portfolio](https://www.tataautocomp.com/tevcs/), [Exxelia SVM busbars](https://exxelia.com/en/list/svm-bus-bars), [Alkraft battery thermal systems](https://alkraft.com/btms/), [TACO Air International technology](https://www.tataautocomp.com/taco-air-international/technology/), [Chara motor technology](https://www.chara.co.in/technology), [Matel technology](https://www.matel.co.in/nextinmotion). These pages establish offerings, not a promise that those firms will partner or buy.

MHI's **5 December 2025** alternative-motor workshop specifically covered induction, switched-reluctance, wound-field, reluctance-assisted and non-rare-earth magnetic options across vehicle segments. This supports the strategic rationale for rank 4; it is not validation of a particular startup motor. [Official workshop release](https://heavyindustries.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-12/press_release_press_information_bureau.pdf).

CEA's **12 December 2025** critical-import consultation names specialised cores, winding/assembly automation and high-speed testing among broader electrical-industry needs. It supplies adjacent import evidence for ranks 1 and 5, **not proof of the size of an EV-only import market**. Its consultation deadline has passed; it is not an open procurement order. ## Two ideas policy enthusiasm alone does not justify

**A general-purpose 30/40 kW charger rectifier factory is not automatically the best first investment.** Local offers already include [Yonder/Ador 30 kW](https://yonderh2.com/30kw-converter/) and [Bacancy modules](https://bacancysystems.com/power-module); Exicom announced its Hyderabad facility and local Tritium manufacturing plans in March 2026. A differentiated module can still qualify with an anchor buyer, repairability or a demanding operating envelope, but an assembly deadline does not prove a shortage. Imported semiconductor devices remain. [Exicom facility announcement](https://www.exicom.com/insights/press-releases/hyderabad-manufacturing-facility-inauguration-2026).

**An ACC cell gigafactory or integrated rare-earth magnet plant is outside the assumed initial scope.** The new grid-storage RFP alone requires eligibility at a much larger scale. Partnership or supplying tools, machines and validated subassemblies is a different proposition from owning the subsidised cell project.

For cash planning, Servotech's May 2026 filed call described increased receivables/borrowings and commissioning-linked payment delays across its mixed business. That is a practical warning to negotiate advances, factory acceptance payments and customer credit; it is not a startup margin benchmark or evidence that every charger business loses money. [Filed earnings transcript](https://www.servotech.in/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/SERVOTECH_07052026175543_NSETRANS.pdf).

## The useful request through the user's network

Ask for a technical sourcing workshop with **three vehicle OEMs, two battery/powertrain Tier-1s, one test agency and relevant ACMA members**, with permission to discuss specific imported BOM lines under NDA. Each firm should bring annual quantity, drawings/specifications, incumbent origin, landed price, qualification cycle and a responsible sourcing engineer. FADA can supply operating/service feedback; it ordinarily does not nominate production components. SIAM/ACMA can convene buyers; MHI can explain scheme interpretation and introduce testing institutions. Private engineering, procurement and supplier-quality teams still decide nominations.

Before committing factory capacity, require a customer-funded prototype or development order, an agreed validation plan, a credible nominated-volume schedule and a working-capital model. A domestic-process rule plus a ministerial introduction is a useful opening, not an order book.
