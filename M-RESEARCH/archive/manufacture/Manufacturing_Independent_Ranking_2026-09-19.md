# Independent manufacturing opportunity ranking — 19 September 2026

This decision record follows the user's request to broaden the research beyond the supplied HTML and prior reports. It supersedes the ordering in `Manufacturing_10_Ideas_Verified_2026-09-19.md` for the current import-reduction brief. Original evidence and supplied files were preserved.

## Scope and assumptions

Fresh public-source research covered industrial machinery/tooling, EV and electrical components, and semiconductor/electronics suppliers. Three parallel evidence ledgers record the individual comparisons. Their candidate lists overlap; they should not be added together as a count of unique ideas.

The user requested a best-case scenario. Assume access to an experienced technical team, financing, industrial premises and useful industry introductions. The INR 1–30 crore initial total-investment range in the supplied context remains a planning reference, not newly confirmed funding. Orders, licences, eligibility for incentives and all-in factory costs are not established. The ranking is analytical judgment, not a government priority order or a quantified investment return forecast.

## Final ordering

1. Precision injection-mould tooling for demanding electrical/electronic parts: focus on connector housings, relay bobbins and replacement inserts. Strongest combination of official import evidence, identifiable manufacturing customers, repeat tooling/maintenance requirements and scoped entry.
2. Specialised electrical-component production and testing machines: begin with one relay/solenoid winding, assembly or test-cell family. Avoid an undefined automation integrator or a complete battery production line.
3. Specialised medium-voltage insulation components: select one customer-approved cast part family and voltage range. Do not combine ordinary support insulators and advanced high-voltage GIS components into one assumed capability.
4. Custom high-frequency transformers and inductors: replace a buyer's imported finished magnetic assembly. Domestic winding does not by itself localise specialised core materials.
5. Rare-earth-free motor industrialisation through an existing technology owner: a licensed or joint manufacturing arrangement for one application. A partner's willingness, licence price and application approval remain unknown.
6. Precision parts for semiconductor manufacturing equipment: customer-approved mechanical parts and selected clean assemblies. Initial export sales are a distinct business case from replacing current Indian imports.
7. High-purity gas-delivery subassemblies for semiconductor plants: start with selected inert-gas panels/tube assemblies through an approved technical partner. Imported valves, regulators and controllers may retain substantial product value.
8. Specialised DC-link/snubber film capacitors: wind, terminate, encapsulate and test one industrial product family using purchased qualified film. Existing Indian producers mean the business needs an unmet specification or service advantage.
9. Qualified thermal-management components: custom cold plates and coolant manifolds for industrial power electronics or EV systems assembled locally. Exact current imported part demand remains to be established; imported complete AI racks are not assumed to generate local cold-plate purchases.
10. Precision-ground ball screws, conditional on a specialist partnership: strongest evidence of a narrow technological import gap among the long-term candidates, but a competitive commercial factory within INR 30 crore is unproven. Limit the initial proposal to a quoted part family/pilot and explicit buyer qualification programme. An independent CMTI licence is not assumed available.

The three most useful first investigations are tooling, specialised production equipment, and medium-voltage insulation. The number 10 position is conditional and should not be interpreted as an equipment-purchase recommendation. Semiconductor test-interface fixtures were a close alternative; their partial imported content, established domestic jig suppliers and overlap with the tooling/testing capability kept them outside the final ten.

## Findings that changed the previous ranking

- Official MHI figures report INR 9,400 crore of FY2024–25 imports in the broad dies/moulds/press-tools sector. That is not the market size for connector tooling or attainable startup sales. [MHI parliamentary answer, 10 February 2026](https://www.heavyindustries.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-02/lsauq_1737_0.pdf).
- CEA identifies particular imported manufacturing tools, relay-production equipment and switchgear components. This supplies a more specific starting point than broad EV sales forecasts. The December 2025 consultation is not a live procurement notice. [CEA import-localisation list](https://cea.nic.in/wp-content/uploads/notification/2025/12/12_12_2025_identified_critical_items_currently_being_imported_and_support_required_for_their_localization.pdf).
- Charger modules already face local suppliers and vertically integrated OEMs. The previous unconditional first rank is withdrawn. A module venture requires its own anchor buyer, design rights, cost advantage and field-service economics; localisation rules alone do not prove an unserved market. See the EV evidence ledger.
- CMTI's February 2026 newsletter confirms ball-screw technology transfer to Jyoti CNC and explicitly discusses investment and competitive barriers. Its combined ball-screw/guideway market estimate is not measured customs imports. [CMTI newsletter](https://cmti.res.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/SciMakIssue2Feb2026.pdf).
- The 18 September 2026 semiconductor announcement records Tata Electronics/Kelington cooperation on gas systems, high-purity piping and local-partner training. It also records established partners for chemicals, gases and lead frames. These announcements show concrete industry relationships, not an open subcontract award. [MeitY/PIB update](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2312234&lang=1&reg=1).

## Supplemental root research

- [Epoxy House](https://www.epoxyhouse.com/) and [System Control](https://systemcontrol.in/) establish existing Indian insulation/switchgear capability. Qualification and product-level imported origin must be demonstrated; the category is not empty.
- [BHEL vendor development](https://pem.bhel.com/Bhel_vendor_section.aspx) establishes formal package-specific registration and qualification. Supplier registration is not a purchase commitment.
- BHEL public notices for [epoxy/SMC items](https://tenders.bhel.com/supply-epoxy-smc-items-14-items) and [heat sinks](https://tenders.bhel.com/supply-heat-sink-2-types-0), dated 19 September 2026 and checked by direct HTTP retrieval, illustrate adjacent buying activity. They do not establish that those items are imported, that the startup qualifies, or that the tender quantities define a market.
- [Syrma's magnetics sourcing disclosure](https://syrmasgs.com/magnetics/) distinguishes domestic ferrite options and overseas specialised core sourcing.
- [Deki](https://www.dekielectronics.com/product/metallized-pp-dc-link-capacitors-customized-product) and [Exxelia Alcon](https://alconelectronics.com/en/alcon) show established Indian customised capacitor supply.

## How the network is relevant

Request engineering and sourcing discussions through CMTI, relevant MHI-linked supplier-development channels, ACMA and appropriate private manufacturers. Obtain currently imported part numbers, specifications, annual purchases, landed costs and qualification plans. For tooling and factory equipment, a paid development/trial programme is a useful first commercial milestone. Semiconductor work also needs equipment OEMs, fab/EPC contractors and the MeitY/ISM ecosystem; a connection to one ministry does not control those purchasing decisions.

## Research limits and working files

No company or official was contacted. No factory visit, independent equipment quotation, item-level customs extract, executed technology licence, buyer order or audited startup financial model was obtained. Manufacturer claims establish marketed capability, not independently verified product performance. This is a detailed desk-research opportunity screen, not completed investment due diligence.
