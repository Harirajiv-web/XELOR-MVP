## Conclusion

The pasted ranking identifies genuine manufacturing opportunities, but its precise market numbers should not determine the final order. It mixes broad import baskets, future industry forecasts, cumulative construction spending and narrow product estimates. These measure different things. Its assertion that the semiconductor supply chain has no orders and only MoUs is contradicted by primary company announcements. Conversely, physical installation in India does not make all equipment Indian-made or all project spending accessible to a new supplier.

Keep AI cooling, semiconductor equipment assemblies and semiconductor testing in the serious shortlist. Include advanced packaging and HDI PCB fabrication as ambitious partner-led projects. Rank a selected connector family, a selected chemical or a defined utility assembly—not an entire electrical or chemical import basket—as the business opportunity.

## Mature Indian supply changes the question, not the opportunity

- **High-speed connectors:** Amphenol's own October 2025 certificate confirms manufacture/assembly of connectors and cages at Electronic City, Bengaluru. This supports Karnataka execution potential and the need to choose an unserved or second-source product specification. It does not establish that every high-speed connector is already local. https://www.amphenol-cs.com/media/wysiwyg/files/documentation/certification/ahst-india-iso9001.pdf
- **HDI PCBs:** AT&S Nanjangud explicitly manufactures high-frequency/HDI boards. Its stated Indian applications include automotive, industrial and infrastructure; do not infer from its global portfolio that this plant already makes every advanced AI-server board or IC substrate. https://ats.net/en/campaign/nanjangud/
- **New PCB supply:** the first ECMS approvals name Kaynes HDI/multilayer/laminate projects, Syrma multilayer PCBs and Ascent multilayer PCBs. Competitors are also proof of industrial momentum; new capacity must nevertheless enter the demand/supply assessment. https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/oct/doc20251027678301.pdf
- **Semiconductor equipment parts:** TEAM D and Millworks demonstrate existing Bengaluru-area production capabilities. The opportunity is to win specific qualified assemblies and grow the local value chain, not claim no Indian supplier exists. https://www.teamd.in/ ; https://millworksindia.com/semiconductor/
- **Test engineering:** Tessolve provides established test/productisation capability; STPI's Bengaluru SMART Lab supplies a real local validation ecosystem. These are potential partners and commercial benchmarks, not reasons to abandon testing. https://www.tessolve.com/ ; https://stpi.in/en/smart-lab-services
- **Gas supply:** INOX's own news page lists operational ultra-high-purity electronic-grade nitrous-oxide production and solar-sector gas contracts. This disproves an indiscriminate claim that India makes no electronic-grade gases, but solar contracts do not automatically prove an IC-fab-qualified product at the needed purity/specification. https://inoxairproducts.com/news-center/

## Semiconductor demand: use the right scale

SEMI's 14 July 2026 global forecast projects equipment sales of USD 165.9 billion in 2026, up 23.2%; test equipment USD 15.3 billion, up 31.0%; and assembly/packaging equipment USD 6.7 billion, up 9.6%. These support growth exposure for qualified suppliers, but are global equipment categories, not Indian machined-component or socket markets. China, Taiwan and Korea remain major demand centres, so initial Indian supplier revenue may be exports. https://www.semi.org/en/semi-press-release/global-semiconductor-equipment-sales-forecast-to-reach-a-record-229-billion-dollars-in-2028-semi-reports

The dated original WSTS Spring 2026 forecast demonstrates highly uneven growth between memory, logic, analog and discrete categories. Do not transfer semiconductor revenue growth mechanically to leadframes, utility pipes, tooling or a specific Indian production line. Revenue growth also differs from physical production growth. https://www.wsts.org/esraCMS/extension/media/f/WST/7618/WSTS_FC-Release-2026-May.pdf

India's May 2026 NITI roadmap highlights advanced packaging, compound semiconductors and specialised design as strategic opportunities. It labels its own data as drawn from secondary sources, and its ambitions are not supplier orders or an independently measured accessible market. https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-05/Future-of-India-Semiconductor-Industry.pdf

## Recommended shortlist contribution to the final ten

For a growth-oriented Karnataka venture with strong connections and access to partners, my semiconductor/electronics priorities are: **equipment assemblies; test/burn-in interfaces; advanced HDI/high-layer-count PCBs; a differentiated connector family; and advanced packaging JV.** Treat UHP and selected chemicals as credible alternatives where the user can obtain a strong technology/EPC relationship and actual buyer programme. Keep leadframes and substrates as distinct partner-led manufacturing candidates, not a combined catch-all category. Fabless chips are a strategic product company option, with economics and team requirements unlike a workshop.

In the wider ranking, retain AI cooling and power-delivery equipment for direct infrastructure demand. Do not let the pasted ranking's unverified pipework market estimate or absence of a named subsidy push cooling out automatically. Consider combining broad cleanroom/facility EPC and UHP as one platform with clearly differentiated initial scope if otherwise they duplicate the same investment pool; alternatively keep both only when one is a product manufacturing business and the other a distinct process-systems business.

Political/industry access can improve introductions, consortium formation and anchor purchase commitments. That is a real advantage. Strong competition is not disqualifying. The deciding evidence remains: a defined article or service, a buyer with authority to approve it, transferable technology, accepted samples, manufacturing value added and sustainable margin. No ranking here assumes that an introduction itself is an order.

## Scheme interpretation limits

The parent policy audit separately verifies current ECMS/ISM2 windows and thresholds. Even where a cleanroom or utility asset is an eligible part of an approved customer's project, do not say government pays a fixed fraction of every supplier invoice or mandates procurement from this new entrant. Supplier-side eligibility and customer-side eligible capex are different. An advertised open scheme window is not sufficient to establish a viable business; lack of subsidy is not sufficient to reject one.

No product-level verified market size, success probability, margin or capital quote is invented in this audit. Exact unverified import totals should remain labelled until their original tables are produced.
