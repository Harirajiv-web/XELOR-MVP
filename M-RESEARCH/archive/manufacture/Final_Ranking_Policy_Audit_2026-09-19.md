## Semicon funding and cleanroom/hook-up

The actual **31 August2026 Gazette** is accessible at [eGazette275860](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/275860.pdf). Manufacturing categories specify approved applicants, financial/technology thresholds and eligible capital expenditure; disbursement follows project approval conditions. The official pages confirm **40% silicon fabs**, **35% compound/photonics/MEMS/discrete fabs**, **35% advanced ATMP**, **25% legacy ATMP**, and **30% eligible machinery/materials/test-facility capex**. [Fab terms](https://ism.gov.in/schemes/semicon2.0/fabs), [ATMP terms](https://ism.gov.in/schemes/semicon2.0/atmp-osat), [machines/materials terms](https://ism.gov.in/schemes/semicon2.0/machines-and-materials)

The ISM homepage lists manufacturing guidelines dated16September2026. The linked PDF hosting returned403 during this audit, so the assertion that cleanrooms and hook-up are specifically named in those new guidelines was **not independently verified**, and is not labelled false. Even if those costs are eligible, that establishes support to the approved customer on qualifying expenditure. It does not give every contractor a30–40% subsidy, force purchase from an Indian-owned supplier, or establish an unawarded market of the stated size. [Official guideline notice](https://ism.gov.in/)

The12 approved plants belong to **ISM1.0**, five already commercially operational according to the18September2026 official release. Different project sanctions and remaining procurement schedules must govern their supplier opportunity. [Current official status](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2312288&lang=2&reg=48)

The pasted claim correctly distinguishes multiyear programme authorisation from an annual budget. However, February's ₹1,000 crore ISM2.0 allocation does not mean all support for the existing pipeline is limited to₹1,000 crore: the modified legacy semiconductor programme separately received₹8,000 crore in the FY2026–27 budget. A current cash or remaining-appropriation claim also needs subsequent budget/reappropriation records. [Budget explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2224839&lang=1&reg=1)

**Commercial implication:** retain cleanroom/process-utility engineering as a credible opportunity under best-case expertise and finance. Rank the specific package a new company can qualify for, including recurring retrofits/maintenance and its own manufactured modules. Do not rank by applying one generic percentage to every approved project's full value. UHP gas/chemical delivery is frequently a subset of this facility scope, so its demand cannot be added again without separating scopes.

| Category | Investment threshold | Six-year turnover incentive | Capex incentive |

[Official PIB launch table](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2124620&lang=2&reg=48). The live portal states that A/B/C/E closed30September2025 and D remains open until30April2027. The connector route is therefore **not presently an open fresh application simply because its headline terms are attractive**. [Live portal](https://ecms.meity.gov.in/)

The [original official guidelines](https://ecms.meity.gov.in/documents/ECMS%20Guidelines_26.04.2025.pdf) were downloaded and visually read; references below use printed page numbers:

- **p30, Annexure1:** specified electro-mechanicals expressly include connectors, relays and switches for electronic applications. Broader power/automotive categories should not be assumed eligible without classification.
- **p16, §8.2–8.3:** additional1% of incremental multilayer-PCB sales for domestically sourced/manufactured laminate, with proportionate treatment for partial domestic sourcing. **p17, §10.2.6:** buying imported material through an Indian supplier does not make it domestic.
- **p14, §6.1–6.2:** one percentage point of turnover incentive and five points of capex incentive depend on employment thresholds. Headline rates are conditional.
- **p28, §22.9:** a company manufacturing multilayer and HDI but approved only under multilayer can include HDI sales at the multilayer rate; avoid an absolute statement that HDI can never use that schedule.
- **p9, §5.1.5:** the same investments/sales already incentivised by another Government of Bharat scheme cannot be incentivised under ECMS. This is an explicit central same-base restriction, stronger evidence than the previous ledger's caution about uncertain overlap.
- **p8, §4.2.1/4.3.1; p24, §19.2:** nine-month deadline after relevant FY-end, while claims may be filed during the year once eligibility is met.

**Disbursement verdict:** neither a claim-filing deadline nor silence in an approvals release proves zero cash disbursement. No current primary record substantiating zero as of19September2026 was located. Use “current cumulative disbursement not verified” rather than inventing zero. Likewise, an approved investment number measures project investment, not incentives paid.

**Tooling verdict:** an applicant's eligible expenditure can include tools, dies and jigs. That does not automatically turn every toolmaker into an eligible CategoryD manufacturer. A covered capital-good or supply-chain product and its intended electronics use must be established in the application. Precision moulds remain a sound industrial candidate on customer economics; scheme eligibility is product-specific.

## CEA import-gap claims

The **12 December2025 CEA letter** identifies73 items, prioritises16 and requests further information for57. AnnexureI, printedp3, includes specialised Ethernet switches conforming to communication requirements such as IEC61850, plus PLC controllers. These are not interchangeable with generic electromechanical switches. AnnexureII, p6, lists specialised contacts, magnetic cores, moulds, coil-winding automation and relay testing; p8 lists relay categories. The response deadline was29December2025. It is evidence of identified industrial gaps, **not an open order or approved incentive scheme**. [Official CEA document](https://cea.nic.in/wp-content/uploads/notification/2025/12/12_12_2025_identified_critical_items_currently_being_imported_and_support_required_for_their_localization.pdf)

This supports choosing a specialised contact, mould, winding or test-system product. It does not justify transferring a broad import total or prioritisation label to every connector, relay and switch variant.

## Karnataka: do not confuse one exclusion with no support

[Invest Karnataka's official ESDM sector page](https://investkarnataka.co.in/key-industrie/electronic-system-design-and-manufacturing/) separately describes land subsidy, plant/machinery subsidy and turnover support. Its specific land footnote excludes **both Bengaluru Urban and Bengaluru Rural**. The page still labels an older policy period, so it is not sufficient by itself for a new2026 sanction or an entitlement calculation.

Actual continuation is supported by [Aequs's2June2026 regulatory disclosure](https://www.aequs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Intimation-on-ACPPL-Incentive-Order.pdf): its Dharwad subsidiary received a1June2026 sanction under **Special Incentives Scheme for ESDM Sector2020–2030**. This is real state support; it does not establish that every startup receives the same package or that central benefits stack on identical costs.

The [industrial policy operative guidelines](https://investkarnataka.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Karnataka.pdf) require selection of the relevant state policy (printedp12) and contain route-specific treatment of other assistance (MSME capital subsidy,p97). Bengaluru Urban/Rural are Zone3; some base industrial incentives apply only to the Bengaluru Rural portion of that zone. That is **not a blanket legal exclusion of Bengaluru Urban from all ESDM support**. Dabaspet is not a way to claim the ESDM land subsidy described on the sector page, since it lies in Bengaluru Rural.

No primary basis was found for declaring Karnataka “weakest of six” across unmatched project types, site categories, caps, tax reimbursements and benefit timing. Nor was a current universal automatic ISM/ECMS top-up established. Correct formulation: **Karnataka package and central interaction require project-specific verification; a blanket zero-support claim is unsupported.** Compare locations using the same project model and sanctioned present value, alongside technical labour, buyer proximity, utilities and logistics.

## EV localisation and magnets

The **3 September2026 truck PMP**, S.O.4871(E), pp4–5, requires domestic defined motor/controller operations from1April2027. It specifies **high-voltage connector fitment**, local assembly of components/semiconductors/connectors on PCB, heatsink/enclosure fitment and software flashing. It applies to the covered N2/N3 scheme vehicles; it does not mandate that every HV connector or upstream input be domestically manufactured. The opportunity is in qualified local subsystem production and integration, not a blanket ban on imported connectors. [Official amendment](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/docs/policy_document/4th%20Amendment%20to%20PMP%20for%20N2%20and%20N3%20category%20of%20e-Trucks.pdf)

The **₹7,280 crore REPM scheme** and five-beneficiary design are real, but the initial global tender closed on **12August2026**, and **20 bids were received/opened on13August2026**. No current final award list was verified in this audit. A new company may consider a technology/supply partnership with a qualified bidder or independently viable manufacturing, but should not assume a presently open application. Full oxide-to-metal-to-alloy-to-magnet capability is different from machining imported magnet blocks. [MHI13August2026 announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2299078&lang=1&reg=3)

The quoted exact “100% imported” and EV interconnect “55–70% imported” were not independently substantiated here at the specified product scope. Do not transfer broad rare-earth magnet or tariff-code data to each specific magnet technology or busbar product.

## Orders exist: distinguish public proof from an exhaustive procurement ledger

Tata Projects' **23September2023 announcement** explicitly identifies an awarded contract to construct Micron's Sanand assembly/test facility, including a500,000-square-foot cleanroom in phase1. This directly contradicts the sector-wide assertion that all activity remains at MoU stage. It is a facility contract, not proof of every semiconductor process component's domestic origin. [Tata Projects primary announcement](https://www.tataprojects.com/web/node/1793)

An inability to find public component POs should be labelled a research limit. Private qualification lists, EPC subcontracts and OEM purchase orders are often not public. Conversely, the existence of a contract with an incumbent does not make the sector unavailable: it identifies the buyer chain and the need to locate future phases, second-source programmes, local subcontract packages or differentiated products.

## Implications for the final top10

Rank by growth, defensible capability and the scale of a specific attainable product family under best-case funding/team/network. Do not make either a competitor's presence or a missing subsidy an automatic veto.

- Keep process utilities/UHP systems and precision semiconductor/electronics equipment serious candidates, while separating their scopes and approval routes.
- Treat connectors/relays, tooling and PCBs as specific product/process businesses; the headline import basket is not the obtainable market.
- Distinguish direct subsidy eligibility, subsidised-customer demand and ordinary commercial demand. All three can support a good business.
- Treat closed-window PCB/connector and magnet schemes as beneficiary-partnership or independent-investment cases, not fresh grants already available.
- Keep AI cooling/power and fabless design in contention when their growth and IP/product economics justify it; absence from the pasted ranking or lack of a particular factory grant does not weaken commercial demand by itself.
- Use the user's network to obtain an actual buyer's product specification, qualification sponsor, indicative demand and technology partner. Introductions are useful; they do not change scheme definitions or substitute for nomination.

Unresolved: exact new-guideline cleanroom/hook-up eligible-cost wording, current ECMS cumulative disbursement, a matched six-state incentive comparison, and project-specific state/central sanction interactions. These uncertainties are explicitly preserved rather than converted into negative facts.
