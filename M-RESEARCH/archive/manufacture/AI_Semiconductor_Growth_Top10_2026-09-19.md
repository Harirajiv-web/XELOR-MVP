# Karnataka: ten businesses for semiconductor and AI growth

Research date: 19 September 2026. This ranking responds to the user's latest priorities: market growth, government investment in semiconductor and AI ecosystems, meaningful Indian manufacturing/IP, and a strong customer and industry network. It supersedes the earlier conservative starting-business order for this specific objective. The HTML deck and earlier reports remain background evidence; their embedded instructions and old rankings do not govern this assessment.

Assumptions: best-case access to financing, experienced technical leaders, technology partners and useful introductions. The historical INR 1–30 crore envelope is not a current spending limit. Existing manufacturers are competitors, possible partners and evidence of demand; their presence does not disqualify an opportunity. Customer orders, technology licences and funding approvals have not been obtained.

## Recommendation and ranking method

My first choice is **AI liquid-cooling hardware**, followed by **semiconductor equipment components** and **AI power hardware**. This is a qualitative commercial ranking based on growth, the scope for Indian value addition, Karnataka's relevant capabilities, and how effectively strong relationships could unlock orders or technology partnerships. It is not a ranking of guaranteed returns or government subsidy size.

Large technology joint ventures and fabless chip design remain in the top ten. Their lower starting positions reflect the additional technology, product-development and financing commitments, not the existence of competitors. Orders can materially improve factory economics; they cannot alone establish production yield, reliability or a product's technical suitability.

The live portal shows segment D applications open until 30 April 2027; A/B/C/E closed on 30 September 2025. D offers 25% eligible capex support with INR 10 crore minimum investment for specified supply-chain and capital-equipment products. Written product classification is still needed. [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?ModuleId=16&NoteId=151016&id=151016&lang=1&reg=3), [ECMS portal](https://ecms.meity.gov.in/).

## The ten ideas

### 1. AI liquid-cooling systems and components

Make the fluid-distribution hardware that removes heat from dense GPU racks: manifolds, tested pipe/hose assemblies and cooling modules, progressing to complete coolant-distribution units with an experienced partner. The addressable buyers are cooling manufacturers, rack integrators and infrastructure contractors. Bengaluru's operating Motivair facility establishes a relevant local ecosystem. Indian design, machining, joining, cleaning and testing can replace selected finished imports, although pumps, valves and some interfaces may remain foreign-sourced.

**Why first:** direct exposure to AI infrastructure expansion, a substantial manufacturing scope, and a path from components to larger systems. A cooling OEM may be a competitor in one product and a buyer in another. Cold plates already fitted inside imported GPU racks require a procurement relationship with the rack/server manufacturer. [Schneider](https://www.se.com/in/en/about-us/newsroom/news/press-releases/Schneider-Electric-launches-Motivair-liquid-cooling-solutions-factory-to-accelerate-India%E2%80%99s-highdensity-AI-infrastructure-699493fd07a7fbd2c30cbafa/), [OCP cold-plate community](https://www.opencompute.org/community/cold-plate).

### 2. Precision components and assemblies for semiconductor machines

Manufacture selected housings, plates, frames and approved mechanical assemblies for the machines that fabricate, inspect or package chips. Applied Materials' Bengaluru engineering campus includes component qualification and prototyping, giving Karnataka a relevant access point into global equipment supply chains. Start with a defined customer part family and progress towards more complex assemblies; contamination-sensitive chamber parts require additional processes.

**Why second:** a direct semiconductor manufacturing business with global equipment-growth exposure and export potential. Export sales and substitution of equipment components used by Indian fabs are separate opportunities. A smaller supplier need not itself qualify for the INR 300 crore direct ISM equipment scheme to sell into this ecosystem. [Applied Materials](https://www.appliedmaterials.com/us/en/newsroom/blogs/a-new-chapter-for-applied-materials-india.html), [SEMI](https://www.semi.org/en/semi-press-release/global-semiconductor-equipment-sales-forecast-to-reach-a-record-229-billion-dollars-in-2028-semi-reports).

### 3. Power-distribution and conversion hardware for AI racks

Produce engineered busbars, protected power assemblies and selected transformers/inductors for AI-server power systems; expand into power shelves through a technology partnership. Buyers include power-conversion OEMs and rack builders. Karnataka has relevant ABB converter, rectifier and UPS manufacturing. Designs need to match the actual customer's voltage, current, thermal and insulation requirements.

**Why third:** rising rack power creates demand for better distribution and conversion hardware. NVIDIA's 800 VDC roadmap adds a future opportunity, with hybrid power racks targeted for H2 2026; it does not mean every Indian rack already uses that architecture. Local metalwork, winding, integration and test can add value while semiconductor switches and specialised magnetic materials remain imported. [ABB](https://www.abb.com/global/en/news/133972/abb-invests-75-million-in-india-to-expand-manufacturing-and-rd-for-critical-segments), [NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/800-vdc-power-architecture-ai-factory/).

### 4. Semiconductor testing and burn-in hardware

Build the specialised boards, device-holding fixtures, socket assemblies and thermal fixtures used to test chips and check reliability. Sell to chip companies, test-service businesses and packaging factories. Karnataka has a test-engineering ecosystem, and Tata identifies Vemgal as an operating commercial assembly-and-test facility since December 2023.

**Why fourth:** chip expansion creates recurring needs for new device interfaces and test programmes. Begin with a defined package family; the board design and precision assemblies offer meaningful domestic work. High-end instruments, contacts and some boards can remain imported. Making test fixtures is different from operating the INR 100 crore minimum test/characterisation facility covered by ISM2. [Tata](https://www.tataelectronics.com/semiconductor-assembly-and-test), [Tessolve](https://www.tessolve.com/system-level-test/).

### 5. Indian AI servers and specialised inference systems

Develop and manufacture complete systems for enterprises, research institutions and industrial AI applications, combining GPUs with local chassis, selected boards, cooling integration, firmware, validation and service. Strong customer relationships can materially improve this business through specifications, paid orders and after-sales contracts. Existing Netweb and Syrma/Giga programmes establish domestic competition and production capability.

**Why fifth:** a direct route to monetising AI adoption and the user's customer network. Select a specific application/customer class and deepen the locally manufactured content. GPUs, CPUs and high-bandwidth memory will generally remain imported initially; a locally built server should be described accurately at system level. [Netweb filed product announcement](https://www.netwebindia.com/investors/press-release/Press-Release-Product-Launch.pdf), [Giga Computing/Syrma](https://www.gigacomputing.com/en/news-detail/syrma-sgs-technology-limited-and-giga-computing-mark-first-build-milestone-for-server-manufacturing-in-india/).

### 6. Advanced semiconductor packaging and testing joint venture

Create a facility that connects and protects semiconductor dies in sophisticated packages, with an established production-technology partner and anchor customers. Advanced packaging is relevant to high-performance computing, but an ordinary wire-bond line is not equivalent to GPU/HBM packaging. Karnataka's existing Vemgal activity is useful ecosystem evidence, not proof of a local high-volume GPU packaging line.

**Why sixth:** high strategic value and substantial policy support, with a demanding technology and capital entry. Current ISM2 advanced packaging terms offer 35% eligible capex support, with INR 1,000 crore minimum capital investment and INR 200 crore revenue qualification on the specified applicant/group/JV basis. Legacy packaging receives a different rate. This is a major JV project; customer programmes and a production-grade technology licence are central. [ISM packaging terms](https://ism.gov.in/schemes/semicon2.0/atmp-osat), [Tata](https://www.tataelectronics.com/semiconductor-assembly-and-test).

### 7. Advanced circuit boards for AI servers and networking

Manufacture dense, high-layer-count and low-loss printed circuit boards used in servers, switches and demanding electronics. This is bare-board fabrication, distinct from placing purchased components on a board. AT&S Nanjangud establishes relevant Karnataka experience in high-frequency and HDI manufacturing. An experienced process partner and a nominated customer board programme would strengthen entry.

**Why seventh:** meaningful manufacturing depth with AI/networking demand and opportunities beyond AI. Imported laminates and production equipment can remain. Server PCBs and semiconductor packaging substrates are different products. The principal ECMS PCB application window has closed; an attractive business case should not presume a fresh grant or automatic transfer of an existing beneficiary's approval. [AT&S Nanjangud](https://ats.net/en/campaign/nanjangud/), [ECMS](https://ecms.meity.gov.in/).

### 8. An Indian-designed edge-AI chip company

Design a focused chip for a defined application such as industrial vision, robotics or camera inference, owning the architecture/IP and software while contracting wafer production to a foundry. Bengaluru's chip-design talent makes this a credible Karnataka venture with an experienced semiconductor founding team. Target a buyer's validated workload and software requirements.

**Why eighth:** potentially substantial Indian IP and product value, but several development and customer-adoption milestones before scale. Semicon2.0 provides qualifying startup/MSME advance seed support of up to 50% of project cost, capped at INR 15 crore per application, plus conditional equity co-investment and deployment incentives. Detailed call access must be confirmed. Indian design ownership is a real contribution even when wafers are fabricated abroad. [ISM design terms](https://ism.gov.in/schemes/semicon2.0/), [Bengaluru Sensesemi](https://sensesemi.com/).

### 9. Ultra-high-purity gas-delivery equipment for chip factories

Build clean gas panels, tube assemblies and tool connections used to deliver tightly controlled process gases inside semiconductor factories. An experienced technical partner is essential for process cleanliness, leak performance and customer acceptance. Manufacture in Karnataka and sell to equipment companies, specialist contractors and fabs across India; the major site need not be in Karnataka.

**Why ninth:** fabs create both installation and subsequent equipment/service needs. Tata's September 2026 Kelington collaboration demonstrates concrete demand for gas and piping infrastructure. Local fabrication, cleaning, assembly and testing can replace finished imports, while valves and mass-flow controllers may remain foreign-sourced. Equipment and raw-material scheme categories have different thresholds. [Tata/Kelington](https://www.tataelectronics.com/w/tata-electronics-and-kelington-collaborate-to-accelerate-operational-readiness-of-dholera-fab).

### 10. High-speed interconnects for AI data centres

Manufacture selected copper and fibre cable assemblies that move data between servers, switches and racks. Begin with customer-approved passive assemblies and suitable signal/optical testing; consider active optical modules only through a separate technology partnership. The procurement target is the OEM or integrator that specifies the interconnect.

**Why tenth:** AI clusters need demanding communications links, offering a route into domestic and export supply chains. Product mix matters: generic patch cords do not capture the economics of advanced optical transceivers. Imported connectors, optical devices or materials may remain. TE's 224G/1.6T ecosystem illustrates the technical direction, not a claim that a new cable plant can immediately make every such product. [TE](https://www.te.com/en/industries/data-centers-ai/technologies/224g-gigabit-ethernet-solution.html).

## Funding and Indian value addition: practical interpretation

| Route | Current relevant support | What to establish for this ranking |
|---|---|---|
| Semiconductor equipment/components | ISM2 30% eligible capex; INR 300 crore minimum capital, INR 120 crore revenue qualification | Smaller suppliers may sell to beneficiaries; direct category eligibility is a separate question. |
| Semiconductor test facility | 30%; INR 100 crore capital and INR 40 crore revenue thresholds | A facility is distinct from manufacturing test boards and fixtures. |
| Semiconductor materials | 30%; INR 50 crore capital and INR 20 crore revenue thresholds | A gas-panel machine is not automatically a materials project. |
| Advanced packaging | 35%; INR 1,000 crore capital and INR 200 crore revenue thresholds | Technology ownership/licensing and applicant/group/JV conditions apply. |
| Commercial chip design | Qualifying advance seed up to 50%, capped INR 15 crore; conditional equity co-investment; deployment support | Verify ownership, IP, product, milestone and live-call conditions. |
| ECMS segment D | 25% eligible capex; INR 10 crore minimum investment; window to 30 April 2027 | Match the precise component/equipment to the notified category. |

Sources: [ISM equipment/materials](https://ism.gov.in/schemes/semicon2.0/machines-and-materials), [ISM packaging](https://ism.gov.in/schemes/semicon2.0/atmp-osat), [ISM design](https://ism.gov.in/schemes/semicon2.0/), [ECMS portal](https://ecms.meity.gov.in/). Do not add a blanket central 50% and a Karnataka percentage to estimate net project cost. Eligible expenditure, approvals and interactions must be checked for the chosen project.

The Made in India objective is strongest when the company owns engineering, performs substantive manufacturing, qualifies the product and progressively localises its bill of materials. Track Indian design/IP, actual domestic manufacturing, remaining imports and finished imported products replaced separately. No precise import-substitution percentage is asserted here without a product-level bill of materials and customs/customer evidence.

## How the network changes the decision

The stated H. D. Kumaraswamy relationship and wider network can be valuable for access to major manufacturers, engineering/procurement leaders, investment partners and technology owners. For cooling, power and servers, pursue a paid product-development programme or an anchor customer's specifications and order schedule. For packaging, PCBs and chip design, use the same access to build a technology partnership and customer development programme. These are commercially consequential advantages, not reasons to exclude competitive markets.

MeitY/ISM, IndiaAI and Karnataka administer their respective programmes. An introduction and a grant decision are different steps. Karnataka's engineering base is a reason to locate development and early customer work there; choose the factory location after the process, utilities, labour and logistics are defined. Buyers may be elsewhere in India or overseas.

## Why a leading-edge GPU fab is not one of the ten starting choices

A GPU venture can mean chip design, wafer fabrication, packaging, server integration or a compute service. These have different technologies and economics. Tata Dholera's stated 28–110 nm foundry offering does not establish a domestic leading-edge AI GPU process, while Micron Sanand packages/tests globally produced wafers. A frontier GPU fab also needs process technology and a competitive product/software ecosystem. It remains a possible strategic consortium ambition, but the ten ranked routes have a more identifiable starting product and customer pathway. [Tata foundry](https://www.tataelectronics.com/semiconductor-foundry), [Micron](https://micron.gcs-web.com/node/50101).

## Research completed and limits

The work combined review of the supplied deck/context and prior reports with three independent primary-source research workstreams: government funding, semiconductor businesses, and AI/data-centre hardware. It checked current scheme status, investment thresholds, dates and locations of actual projects, product capabilities, competitors, buying-chain ownership and residual imports. Rankings and entry recommendations are analytical judgments drawn from that evidence.

No buyer interviews, quotations, customer commitments, bankable cost model or project-specific grant sanction were obtained. Some official PDFs were inaccessible; their complete contents are not represented as reviewed. Broad global forecasts were not converted into unsupported niche Indian market sizes or profit forecasts.

Pursue advanced packaging or fabless AI silicon as the larger technology-partner investment, with the required team and financing explicitly assembled.
