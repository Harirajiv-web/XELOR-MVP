# Separate EV manufacturing ranking

Research date: 19 September 2026. This ranking is separate from the earlier general-manufacturing and semiconductor lists. It answers the user's new question about the EV ecosystem described in H. D. Kumaraswamy's Vigyan Bhawan post.

## Assumptions and decision standard

Assume a capable technical team, access to finance for a focused manufacturing operation, and useful introductions through the user's stated connection. The historical INR 1–30 crore initial envelope is a planning reference, not a newly confirmed budget. Do not assume unlimited capital, government orders, exclusive technology access or subsidy eligibility.

The order is a qualitative business-screening judgment combining achievable import replacement, customer demand, access to technology, manufacturing scope and qualification risk. It is not an audited market-share ranking or financial return forecast. Public sources do not establish which imported part a particular OEM will nominate to a new supplier. Each opportunity therefore needs a customer specification, incumbent origin, landed cost and qualification plan before investment.

## Ranked opportunities

1. **Specialised battery-pack production and end-of-line test machines.** Start with electrical, insulation, leakage, BMS communication and traceability stations for a defined pack line. Local fixtures, controls and software can replace an imported complete station where the buyer confirms one is used. Precision instruments or power stages may initially remain imported. This ranks first for a bounded development project and ability to earn a paid customer acceptance before committing to a high-volume component line. Ador Digatron and Jendamark already compete; generic automation is insufficient differentiation. Do not combine this launch with a full motor-dynamometer business.

2. **Engineered high-voltage power-distribution and battery-disconnect assemblies.** Make the current-carrying interconnects, insulation, housings, joints and tested assembly that routes or disconnects battery power. Begin with one nominated commercial-vehicle or battery platform and approved purchased protection components. A specific imported complete PDU/BDU is the replacement target; a box of imported internals alone delivers limited localisation. Trinity Touch, Minda and Tata AutoComp demonstrate competition.

3. **Application-specific battery-management systems.** Develop or license the battery's sensing, protection and control electronics, then validate hardware and firmware on the buyer's actual cells and duty cycle. ARAI publishes a BMS platform at stated TRL8, making a technical-transfer discussion concrete, though current licence terms and vehicle-specific approvals remain unverified. Maxwell/Endurance, Vecmocon and Napino already operate here. Imported monitoring and control chips will initially remain; local intellectual property and validated performance are the proposed value.

4. **Automotive power magnetics.** Manufacture a selected family of high-frequency transformers or chokes for charger and converter manufacturers. Own electromagnetic/thermal design, winding, insulation, assembly and testing. This is a focused component business with several possible downstream buyers. Specialised cores may remain imported, and Exxelia SVM already manufactures magnetics in India: win against a named imported component, not a claimed blanket domestic shortage.

5. **Auxiliary DC-DC converters.** Make one converter family that supplies a vehicle's low-voltage electronics from its traction battery. Use an experienced design team or proven licensed technology and one OEM programme. The scope is narrower than a complete integrated passenger-car charging/powertrain platform. Valeo and other suppliers already manufacture conversion electronics locally. A C-DAC prototype is evidence of Indian R&D, not a turnkey certified product; its examined invitation's January 2026 deadline has passed.

6. **Motor controllers for a defined electric 2W/3W drivetrain.** Design the electronics and control software that regulate motor torque, speed and regenerative braking, matched to a customer's motor. Target an independent customer base and a demonstrable performance, reliability or service advantage. Sterling, Vecmocon and Napino make this a contested category. Imported automotive chips remain. The dedicated controller for rank 9 belongs inside that motor programme, rather than counting as another sale to the same buyer.

7. **Power-conversion modules for DC fast chargers.** Supply a charger manufacturer with the replaceable AC-to-DC power unit inside the charger, with local design, firmware, magnetics and production test. A particular imported module and a buyer willing to qualify a local second source are essential. Exicom, Electrowaves, Bacancy and Ador/Yonder contradict an empty-market claim. High-power semiconductor devices may remain imported; this is not semiconductor fabrication.

8. **Battery/inverter cold plates and coolant manifolds.** Manufacture and validate the metal parts that carry coolant and remove heat. The entry is one imported design requiring reliable joining, cleanliness, leak testing and corrosion durability. India's mechanical thermal-management supply base is already substantial, including Tata AutoComp and Alkraft; ordinary cooling hardware is therefore a weaker import-gap claim. This remains a practical manufacturing option if a specific buyer confirms the need.

9. **Rare-earth-free traction motors, with a technology partner.** Industrialise a proven motor and its matched controller for one vehicle duty cycle, using a design that avoids rare-earth permanent magnets. This directly addresses a vulnerable material dependency but ranks lower for immediate execution because accessible production-ready IP and vehicle qualification have not been established. Greaves' disclosed Chara arrangement specifies exclusive manufacturing for covered products. The examined C-DAC motor uses reduced rare earths, not zero rare earths. Do not present either as an available turnkey licence.

10. **Chemistry-specific battery-material recovery.** Recover useful lithium and other materials in India from contracted scrap or spent batteries, with buyers accepting the output specification. C-MET has a rolling 2026 transfer offer, but its published pyro route yields lithium carbonate and cobalt-nickel alloy and includes small-scale demonstration stages. Battery-grade salts and profitable commercial scale have not been established for the proposed business. Feedstock chemistry, collection costs, processing performance, permits and offtake determine feasibility; LFP feedstock must not be valued as cobalt/nickel-rich material.

## Policy interpretation and use of the connection

The 10 August 2026 Gazette raises PM E-DRIVE's outlay to INR 11,900 crore and changes the e-2W timetable. Read current notifications, not superseded INR 10,900 crore summaries. Vehicle/charger localisation requirements are relevant to covered scheme products; they do not prove all inputs must be domestic or that a new supplier receives a grant. PLI-Auto applies to eligible applicants/products. The July 2026 ACC tender examined is for grid-scale stationary storage, not an EV-cell startup subsidy window.

The minister's post supports the direction of local manufacturing and adoption. The useful network action is a technical sourcing workshop with vehicle OEMs, battery/powertrain suppliers and ARAI/ICAT. Ask buyers for a small number of actual imported parts or machines they want to replace, annual volumes, specification, incumbent landed cost and the engineer responsible for approval. SIAM/ACMA can help convene manufacturers; FADA can add operating/service feedback. Supplier nominations still belong to customer engineering, quality and procurement teams.

The immediate commercial comparison should be one battery test-station requirement, one PDU/BDU sourcing request and one BMS development programme. Select the business after obtaining a paid development order or comparable written customer commitment and a funded qualification plan. No outreach was conducted in this research.
