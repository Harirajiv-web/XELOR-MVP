# EV electronics and battery manufacturing: independent opportunity screen

Date: 19 September 2026. Separate EV-only research for the user's top-ten decision. No outreach, applications or changes to attached documents were made.

## Decision

Within the historical initial INR 1–30 crore envelope and assuming access to strong technical partners, the four finalists from this electronics/battery comparison are:

1. **Application-specific EV power-distribution and battery-disconnect assemblies.** Manufacture engineered busbars/interconnects, insulation, housings and assembled/tested PDUs/BDUs for a nominated commercial-vehicle or battery platform. Do not begin by developing every contactor, fuse and sensor internally.
2. **EV battery and power-electronics end-of-line test equipment.** Build the fixtures, switching, communications, measurement integration, controls and traceability that validate packs/controllers/chargers on production lines. Develop a product family around a defined test process rather than a general automation shop.
3. **A differentiated battery-management-system platform.** Use owned or licensed hardware/firmware and actual cell/vehicle validation; target one difficult application and buyer. ARAI's published BMS technology provides a concrete route to investigate, but substantial Indian competitors already exist.
4. **A narrow licensed power-conversion product.** Prefer an isolated auxiliary DC-DC converter or application-specific light-EV charger with a nominated buyer and demonstrated technology. A 30 kW fast-charger rectifier module is a more demanding conditional variant; it is not an empty domestic category.

Traction controllers remain a credible alternate when paired with a motor technology partner and OEM program. HV charging inlets/connectors remain a conditional mechanical/electrical option, but should not be sold as a competition-free gap. Generic pack assembly and a full cell factory rank below these for the stated import-reduction objective.

These are business-screening judgments, not proven purchase orders or forecasts. The public record does not establish a current, accessible import TAM, a generic margin, or a quoted factory cost for these niches. A strong government/industry connection is useful for introductions to development heads, testing institutions, technology-transfer teams and formal vendor-development programs. It does not replace engineering approval or procurement selection.

## What is already localized, and what remains foreign

The useful distinction is **finished subsystem versus its upstream components**. An Indian-designed and manufactured charger/controller/BMS can still depend on imported automotive semiconductors, sensor ICs, specialty cores, substrates, connectors or cells. Localizing a complete subsystem creates manufacturing and design value without making every upstream input Indian.

The [NITI/ADB/BCG localization study](https://www.niti.gov.in/node/889), dated May 2022 inside the document and published on NITI's portal in May 2023, identified rectifiers, controllers, connectors and battery subcomponents as localization gaps. Its historic percentages cannot describe September 2026 unchanged. India's supplier base has evolved: Exicom now reports Indian power-module manufacturing; Valeo manufactures integrated charging electronics in Pune; Maxwell, Vecmocon, Napino and Sterling have local electronics capabilities.

NITI's [August 2025 EV report](https://www.niti.gov.in/node/1772) also records manufacturers' requests to localize charging components. That is stakeholder evidence of an issue, not audited product-level import statistics. The June 2026 IEEFA/JMK report supplied by the research team is useful corroborating secondary analysis; its localization estimates are not customs measurements and are not used here as precise market shares.

The practical proof of import substitution is a buyer's existing imported part number and country of manufacture, followed by an approved local equivalent and a bill of materials separating residual imports. Foreign branding alone is insufficient: a foreign-owned supplier may manufacture in India, while an Indian-branded product may import major internals.

## 1. Traction controllers and inverters

[Sterling Tools' official history](https://stlfasteners.com/about-us) records a Faridabad MCU manufacturing operation, expansion to a stated 600,000-unit annual capacity, and multiple OEM programs. Its current product direction includes OBC/DC-DC licensing. These are company disclosures; they prove a substantial incumbent, not spare capacity or an unserved market. [Napino's e-mobility range](https://www.napino.com/e-mobility) includes motors, controllers and DC-DC conversion. [Vecmocon](https://vecmocon.com/) also markets motor controllers and an integrated EV electronics platform.

**What a new entrant must own:** motor-control algorithms and calibration, protection, diagnostics, gate-drive/layout/thermal design, configuration management and production test. Rebadging an imported board inside an Indian enclosure delivers little independence. A motor manufacturer alone cannot necessarily provide production-ready controller firmware; contracts must identify source-code access, modification rights and validation responsibility.

**Best wedge:** a matched controller for a specific drivetrain that needs better torque control, thermal derating, regeneration, lower system cost or a new motor topology. If a rare-earth-free motor business is already a separate recommendation, count its co-designed controller as part of that platform unless there is a genuinely independent controller customer base.

**Residual imports:** automotive MCU, gate-driver and power-switch devices; some sensors, capacitors and specialty board materials may remain imported. Their exact share requires an actual bill of materials. No current audited controller import percentage was established.

**Why not higher:** established high-volume domestic suppliers, OEM reluctance to reopen a validated drivetrain, software ownership and vehicle-level fault behaviour. Large-car traction inverters are a different technical and commercial scale from 48–96 V light-EV controllers.

## 2. Battery-management systems: reconsider positively, but do not overlook incumbents

[Maxwell Energy](https://www.maxwellenergy.co/), an Endurance company, states that its electronics are manufactured on its own Aurangabad SMT line. [Vecmocon's BMS](https://vecmocon.com/battery-management-system/) includes configurable CAN communications, balancing and state estimation; its [company history](https://vecmocon.com/about-us/) identifies BGauss as an OEM adoption example and describes a multi-month onboarding process. [Napino's R&D page](https://www.napino.com/research-%26-development) also describes hardware/software-customizable smart BMS products.

There is a particularly relevant government-network connection: Vecmocon's [MHI/ISB-supported project page](https://vecmocon.com/vecmocon-mhi/) describes BMS and controller development. That establishes activity in this area; it does not mean another applicant will receive support.

**Concrete technology starting point:** [ARAI's EV-BMS page](https://www.araiindia.com/electric-vehicle-battery-management-system) describes a hardware/software platform at stated TRL8, model-based estimation, passive balancing and master/slave scalability up to 800 V. It reports EMI/EMC and environmental validation and lists battery-pack manufacturers among intended beneficiaries. A proposed licensee must confirm current transfer terms, exact deliverables, production maturity and what safety/certification evidence applies to its intended pack. TRL8 is not automatic approval for every vehicle or cell chemistry.

**Differentiation:** prove better application-specific performance using the buyer's real cells and duty cycles—accurate usable-energy estimation, diagnostics, fault handling, thermal control and service support. An app or a claim of AI by itself is not differentiation. BMS software creates a stronger potential moat than generic PCB assembly, but field data and calibration require time and an engaged customer.

**Residual imports:** monitoring/front-end ICs, processors, isolation devices and switching devices may remain foreign. The local value lies in hardware design, algorithms, configuration, manufacturing and validation. Large OEMs may own their control logic and source manufacturing separately; the decision owner must be identified before offering a complete platform.

## 3. OBC and auxiliary DC-DC conversion

[Valeo's Pune manufacturing account](https://www.valeo.com/en/valeo-pune-electrifying-mobility-in-india/) describes production of a 3-in-1 OBC/DC-DC/PDU unit and a 7,000-square-metre expansion. Its [Mahindra program release](https://www.valeo.com/en/valeo-to-supply-electric-powertrain-for-mahindra-mahindras-electric-vehicle-program/) establishes a named vehicle customer. [Tata AutoComp's electronics division](https://www.tataautocomp.com/tevcs/electronics-division/products/) markets integrated charging/power-conversion products. [IPEC India](https://i-pec.in/) offers portable/onboard chargers and identifies major Indian OEM customers; the source is the supplier's own customer list, not customer-specific order values.

[Uno Minda's 30 April 2025 announcement](https://www.unominda.com/uploads/investor/invites-and-announcements/2026/Press%20Release%20-%20April%2030%2C%202025.pdf) describes a proposed INR 423 crore multi-product high-voltage powertrain factory with Inovance. This demonstrates the scale of a broad integrated portfolio. It is **not** the minimum cost to manufacture a single auxiliary converter, nor evidence that the project has achieved every announced production milestone.

**Preferred entry:** one DC-DC voltage/power family for auxiliaries, or one rugged light-EV charger, backed by a real process/design licence and nominated OEM. Required competencies include isolation design, magnetics, EMC, switching control, thermal management, protection and automated production test. Vehicle-installed OBC products have different environmental and architecture constraints from external charging modules.

**Why this can outrank a speculative 30 kW module:** a narrower product and a defined vehicle program permit more bounded engineering and qualification. It still needs an actual advantage over IPEC, Napino, Tata AutoComp, Sterling and other established suppliers.

## 4. Fast-charger rectifier modules: useful but no longer a blank localization map

[Servotech's 19 February 2025 release](https://www.servotech.in/press-releases/servotech-wattandwell-to-develop-and-manufacture-ev-charger-components-in-india) identifies a Watt & Well partnership for an initial 30 kW module and local manufacture, with exclusive Indian marketing rights for those components. This establishes a specific licensed-localization model and an incumbent arrangement, not technology freely available to this user. Its earlier capacity announcements should not be presented as independently verified 2026 output.

More decisive current evidence: [Exicom's 19 May 2026 results release](https://www.exicom.com/insights/press-releases/exicom-delivers-its-strongest-quarter-of-fy26-as-both-businesses-return-to-sharp-growth) reports its Hyderabad plant's March inauguration and local manufacture of Tritium liquid-cooled modules. Its [17 April 2026 manufacturing article](https://www.exicom.com/insights/blogs/precision-manufacturing-in-ev-chargers) states that it designs and makes system PCBAs and power-conversion modules internally. The INR 216 crore integrated-plant figure covers a broader facility and must not be used as a single module line quotation.

**New-entrant route:** identify a charger OEM still importing a particular module and willing to approve a second source. Secure topology/firmware rights, component sourcing, test procedures and serviceability. Thermal derating, dust/moisture performance, efficiency over the operating range, input quality, module interchangeability and field repair costs matter. A charge-point operator can validate the uptime problem, but the charger OEM normally controls module selection.

**Residual imports:** high-power semiconductor devices, control ICs and selected magnetic/capacitor inputs. Own power-conversion design and local magnetics/PCB/thermal production can create real value; an imported finished module installed in a domestic cabinet cannot be described as localized module manufacture. Do not confuse a packaged SiC semiconductor module with the complete charger AC-DC power-conversion module.

## 5. EV PDU/BDU: strong fit if the assembly contains real engineering

A PDU distributes high-voltage power across loads. A BDU connects/disconnects the battery and incorporates relevant protection/pre-charge/measurement functions. A product can combine these functions, but they are not interchangeable labels for any electrical box.

[Trinity Touch](https://www.trinitytouch.com/ev-charging-solutions/) explicitly describes design/manufacture of EV PDUs with customer-specific engineering. [Tata AutoComp](https://www.tataautocomp.com/tevcs/electronics-division/products/) includes PDU functionality in integrated electronics. Minda Corporation's [4 September 2024 stock-exchange disclosure](https://bsmedia.business-standard.com/_media/bs/data/announcements/bse/04092024/c86f17d7-458c-4f6f-8697-853a82f7c0d5.pdf) describes a Sanco licence covering connecting systems, PDUs and BDUs; this is a primary company disclosure hosted by a news-distribution mirror.

**Entry scope:** co-design or build-to-print a family of busbar/harness/insulation/enclosure assemblies using approved purchased contactors, fuses and sensors, then own documented assembly and electrical verification. It is possible to deepen localization progressively without pretending to manufacture every switching component.

**Technical gate:** fault-current interruption/protection coordination, creepage/clearance, pre-charge sequencing, insulation, contact resistance, temperature rise, sealing, vibration and traceability to the buyer's requirements. Product approval cannot be inferred merely because all purchased parts carry certificates.

**Important contrary evidence:** [Sterling's official history](https://stlfasteners.com/about-us) describes a GLVAC-linked Bengaluru HV contactor manufacturing initiative. Therefore the claim that all relevant contactors are necessarily imported is already too broad; assess the exact rating, qualification and origin of an approved device.

The buyer is the battery-system/HV electrical design authority, not the person buying commodity busbars. A low-volume commercial-vehicle variant with specific packaging requirements is a more coherent first target than a universal catalogue PDU for every EV.

## 6. Charging inlets and HV connectors

[INGAR](https://ingar.in/pages/charging-inlets) states that it manufactures/supplies charging inlets and inlet harnesses to Indian OEMs. [Elcom's catalogue](https://elcom-in.com/products/ev-inlet-and-connectors/ccs2_connectors/charging-inlet) includes CCS2 inlets. IPEC and Minda's connecting-system initiative provide further competition. These are manufacturer/corporate statements and do not establish identical qualification levels or full local content for every model.

[Phoenix Contact's group description](https://www.phoenixcontact.com/en-de/company/phoenix-contact-group/companies-in-the-group) places its e-mobility development/production business in Germany, China and Poland, while separately describing broader production in India. An Indian office or general Indian factory should not be used to infer the manufacturing origin of a specific inlet.

**Entry logic:** target a buyer-specific inlet/terminal/harness configuration with a tooling/process partner. Own contact geometry, plating-process control, mould tolerances, joining and sealing, plus application-specific temperature-rise and durability testing. The standards interface does not erase product-design IP or the need for vehicle homologation compatibility.

No verified current CCS2 inlet import percentage, unique coating bottleneck or nationwide absence of competing capability was found. This can be a viable product business; it is not automatically superior to a broader interconnect/PDU platform. Generic imported contacts inside a domestic shell represent only partial localization.

## 7. Cell-to-pack assembly versus cell manufacture

[Tata AutoComp Gotion](https://www.tataautocomp.com/taco-gotion/technology/) already describes lithium-ion pack technology for mobility/storage. [Napino](https://www.napino.com/e-mobility) markets packs across cell chemistries/formats. These examples are enough to reject the claim that domestic pack assembly is an overlooked empty category.

A specialized pack can create value through vehicle packaging, thermal performance, weld quality, serviceability, abuse protection, diagnostics and warranted operating life. The commercial risk is cell consistency, traceability, inventory funding, degradation guarantees, field failures and mismatch between supplier and customer warranties. Replacing an imported complete pack with a locally engineered pack is meaningful, but assembling imported cells does not localize the electrochemical cell.

Cell manufacture is a separate materials/process/yield business involving electrode production, controlled environments, formation and qualification. A small pilot facility is not equivalent to an economically competitive volume plant. Keep a full EV-cell factory outside the INR 1–30 crore first-business shortlist. ACC policy backing for cells should not be misrepresented as an automatic subsidy for pack assembly.

## 8. EV test equipment: a useful route into both electronics and batteries

Start with production tests that have a measurable factory problem: pack electrical/communications checks, BMS flashing and fault simulation, weld continuity/resistance, isolation tests, controller/charger functional tests and complete result traceability. Do not promise certification services without the necessary laboratory status; making production test equipment is a different business from issuing type approvals.

**Incumbents:** [Ador Digatron's EOL offering](https://adordigatron.com/eol-testing-modules-packs/) combines battery testing and diagnostic integration and explicitly describes manufacturing presence in India. [Jendamark](https://jendamark.io/component-assembly-solutions/electric-vehicles/) provides pack assembly/testing integration. [Chroma](https://www.chromaate.com/eu/products_list/battery_pack_manufacture_test_solution) offers advanced test systems, while [Arbin](https://www.arbin.com/battery-test-equipment.html) describes US production and Indian support. The latter is a foreign-equipment supply route, not proof of an Indian import market size.

**What to localize:** fixtures, contact/switching systems, control software, safety interlocks, data acquisition integration, mechanical handling, test sequencing and engineering. Initially purchased precision instruments, regenerative power stages, PLCs or safety components may still be foreign. Over time, a proprietary validated measurement/power subsystem creates deeper import substitution than assembling a trolley around imported instruments.

**Differentiation:** shorter station changeover, quicker fault localization, better false-reject control, calibration support, locally available spares and direct compatibility with the buyer's pack/BMS architecture. The buyer's manufacturing engineer and quality manager jointly define acceptance; sourcing and capex committees then approve procurement. Recurring calibration/service helps, but machine orders can be irregular.

## Concrete technology-transfer routes and their limits

The ARAI route above is specific enough for an exploratory technical meeting: review the exact BMS hardware, firmware/source-model deliverables, validation dossier, production support, licence scope and economics. ARAI's [iVCON page](https://www.araiindia.com/intelligent-vehicle-controller-platform) instead describes supervisory vehicle-control software at stated TRL7; it is not a ready-made traction inverter. The [EV charger transfer page](https://www.araiindia.com/ev-charger-technology-know-how-available-for-transfer) currently exposes a title and equipment photograph but no readable power rating or detailed terms. Do not infer a 30 kW rectifier licence from it.

[C-DAC's EVSS technology-transfer invitation](https://www.cdac.in/index.aspx?id=lu_EVSS01_EOI_5ProjectsV1) is a concrete published route, but its stated submission deadline was **15 January 2026**. No current extension was verified. The committee determines transfer terms/pricing. Examples include a Kalyani 3 kW HV auxiliary converter at TRL7, an IIT Madras light-EV charger family, BMS technologies and motor controllers. Read individual entries: the reconfigurable OBC is TRL6 with no certification listed; several other entries also have certification pending. The reduced-rare-earth motor uses both NdFeB and ferrite and is not rare-earth-free. This evidence establishes technologies to investigate, not an open, free, exclusive or universally vehicle-qualified licence.

For any licensed route, access to process drawings/source code does not by itself establish manufacturability at target yield. Ask for build files, component alternatives, test fixtures, software tool licences, change-control responsibility, warranty allocation and an actual pilot build with the intended customer.

## How the user's network should be used

MHI-linked introductions can focus on three concrete workstreams: (1) identify nominated imported subassemblies from an OEM/Tier-1's current localization plan; (2) obtain a technical discussion with ARAI or the appropriate government technology-transfer team; (3) reach the relevant testing and vendor-development desks. C-DAC/EVSS is under MeitY, so do not imply MHI alone controls its licensing.

The [19 December 2025 parliamentary reply from MHI](https://www.heavyindustries.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-12/rsasq_217.pdf) lists approved PLI-Auto JV applicants and proposed products, including Napino controllers/DC-DC, Tata Ficosa PDU/DC-DC and Motherson HV harness/connectors. This is a useful map of incumbent activity and partnership channels. It is not proof that the user is eligible for PLI, that every listed product has achieved production/certification, or that a supplier receives an OEM's incentive directly.

**Immediate decision gate:** compare one PDU/BDU RFQ, one EOL-test-station requirement and one licensed BMS/converter program. Establish who owns the design, what finished imported article is replaced, how much of the bill of materials stays foreign, what the buyer must validate, and whether the complete launch plus working capital fits the available funds. Select one platform once those records exist. No capital allocation or profit forecast is justified from a sector growth story alone.
