# Final Karnataka manufacturing ranking: AI, semiconductors and EVs

Research cut-off: 19 September 2026. Prepared for the user's best-case funding, technical-team and network scenario, including the stated connection to H. D. Kumaraswamy. This is a comparative business-selection assessment, not a completed plant feasibility study or a claim that customers have committed orders.

## Recommendation

My first choice is **AI liquid-cooling hardware and systems**, followed by **precision semiconductor-equipment components and assemblies**, then **critical power-distribution equipment for AI facilities**. Together these offer the strongest combination of expanding demand, identifiable manufactured products, Karnataka engineering access and the ability to turn customer introductions into qualification programmes.

If direct participation in semiconductor manufacturing and import replacement is given greater weight than near-term AI infrastructure demand, move semiconductor-equipment components to first place. This is a deliberate change of priority, not a claim that either sector has a guaranteed return.

The consolidated ten are:

1. AI liquid-cooling hardware and systems.
2. Precision components and selected assemblies for semiconductor equipment.
3. Critical power-distribution equipment for AI data centres and advanced factories.
4. Specialised high-voltage connectors and engineered power interconnects.
5. Specialised electronics/EV production and test machines.
6. Advanced HDI and high-layer-count printed circuit boards.
7. Silicon-carbide power-semiconductor modules through a technology partnership.
8. Ultra-high-purity gas and chemical-delivery systems.
9. Modular cleanroom and mini-environment systems.
10. Advanced semiconductor packaging and testing through a technology JV.

These are alternatives for selecting a business, not a recommendation to build ten factories simultaneously. Each entry defines a first product scope; expanding into adjacent products requires separate engineering and customer acceptance.

## What this research did

Ran separate policy, semiconductor-market and AI/EV challenge workstreams, then reconciled them against primary government notifications, scheme terms, company announcements, exchange disclosures and industry research. Particular attention went to whether a source describes an operating facility, a purchase order, an approval, an MoU or a forecast.

The analysis compares actual products and buyer programmes. It does not rank a small cooling-pipe category against all connector imports, add overlapping cleanroom and process-utility spending, or equate government project support with a supplier's subsidy eligibility.

## Ranking basis and best-case assumptions

The order is my judgment using: strength and breadth of demand; meaningful Indian manufacturing and capability development; usefulness of the user's customer/industry network; scope for product differentiation and exports; and the path from a first customer programme to repeat production. Government support improves an otherwise sound business but does not establish demand by itself.

Assume access to adequate project finance, experienced management and a credible technology partner where needed. Do not assume that financing automatically supplies process IP, production yield, buyer certification, licence rights, critical materials or an available grant. Existing competitors are evidence of demand and benchmarks for differentiation. None of the ten is disqualified because an incumbent already exists.

Domestic value should be tracked through the product bill of materials and manufacturing processes. Local installation, local assembly of imported kits, Indian ownership and actual replacement of imported components are different achievements. Imported inputs can be part of a credible localisation plan; they should be disclosed and reduced where commercially and technically feasible.

## Why demand is credible

- **Semiconductors:** the government's 17 September 2026 account reports Semicon 2.0 approval with a ₹1,27,500 crore outlay, extending support across the ecosystem. The earlier programme has 12 approved manufacturing projects representing over ₹1.64 lakh crore of investment, five already in commercial production. Programme outlay, project investment and cash disbursement are different figures. [PIB status](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2311220&lang=1&reg=48).
- **AI infrastructure:** IEA projects global data-centre electricity demand rising from about 485 TWh in 2025 to 950 TWh in 2030, with AI-focused demand increasing particularly quickly. This supports the direction of cooling and power demand, not a specific Indian supplier revenue forecast. [IEA Energy and AI](https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai/executive-summary).
- **Actual Indian investment:** Google's April 2026 announcement describes groundbreaking for its India AI hub within a $15 billion investment programme over 2026–30. Such announcements support customer-pipeline research; their entire value is not a hardware supplier's addressable market. [Google announcement](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2026-04-28-Google-Breaks-Ground-on-India-AI-Hub,-Launching-a-National-Industrial-Ecosystem-Alongside-Indias-Digital-Infrastructure-Milestone).
- **EVs:** IEA reports that Indian electric two- and three-wheeler sales grew by more than 30% year on year in Q1 2026. EV growth can support connector, power-module and production-equipment programmes, but different vehicle segments use different components and qualification requirements. [IEA Global EV Outlook 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/executive-summary).
- **Semiconductor equipment:** SEMI's July 2026 forecast places global equipment sales at $165.9 billion in 2026 and $229.5 billion in 2028. This is a global opportunity indicator; it cannot be presented as demand available to an Indian startup. [SEMI forecast](https://www.semi.org/en/semi-press-release/global-semiconductor-equipment-sales-forecast-to-reach-a-record-229-billion-dollars-in-2028-semi-reports).

## The final ten in detail

### 1. AI liquid-cooling hardware and systems

**Make:** a defined family of coolant-distribution assemblies, manifolds or cold plates for high-density servers; expand into complete coolant distribution units after the required design and validation capability is in place. Different products require different manufacturing and reliability processes.

**Buyers and advantage:** cooling OEMs, server/rack integrators and data-centre developers. Network access can secure a funded pilot and access to the customer's actual rack architecture. Schneider's February 2026 launch of its Motivair liquid-cooling factory in Bengaluru provides concrete local ecosystem and competition evidence. [Company announcement](https://www.se.com/in/en/about-us/newsroom/news/press-releases/Schneider-Electric-launches-Motivair-liquid-cooling-solutions-factory-to-accelerate-India%E2%80%99s-highdensity-AI-infrastructure-699493fd07a7fbd2c30cbafa/).

**Why first:** direct exposure to AI deployment, a product-led route into repeat orders, and the ability to build domestic thermal/mechanical engineering capability without fabricating the processor itself. Manufacturing must extend beyond reselling an imported unit. Pumps, valves, sensors or controls may initially remain imported.

**Main condition:** independently validated leakage, corrosion, coolant compatibility and thermal performance. A licensed or jointly developed product with an anchor customer is stronger than a factory built around a generic cooling-market forecast. No blanket semiconductor subsidy is assumed.

### 2. Precision semiconductor-equipment components and assemblies

**Make:** selected vacuum-chamber components, machined housings, frames, motion assemblies or other buyer-designed parts and subsystems used in chipmaking equipment. Start with a qualified part family instead of promising an entire lithography, deposition or etch machine.

**Buyers and advantage:** global equipment manufacturers and their qualified tier-one suppliers, as well as selected Indian projects. Applied Materials' Bengaluru campus includes engineering and qualification activity relevant to this ecosystem. [Applied Materials](https://www.appliedmaterials.com/us/en/newsroom/blogs/a-new-chapter-for-applied-materials-india.html).

**Why second:** strong semiconductor exposure, export potential and substantial manufacturing capability. It is my first choice if the primary goal is to become a semiconductor industrial supplier. Exporting a component and replacing an Indian fab's import are separate outcomes and should be measured separately.

**Main condition:** dimensional consistency, surface finish, cleaning, traceability and formal buyer qualification. A network can open a supplier-development programme; production capability determines whether it becomes repeat orders. Semicon 2.0's equipment category has substantial financial thresholds; a smaller component shop is not automatically eligible.

### 3. Critical power-distribution equipment

**Make:** a nominated busway, switchboard or monitored distribution-panel family for data centres and advanced industrial facilities. Start with one product architecture and extend from there. Manufacturing busbars/enclosures and integrating switchgear do not automatically confer the ability to design a complete high-voltage DC conversion system.

**Buyers and advantage:** data-centre operators, electrical EPC companies and fab contractors. This is a particularly useful category for a network that reaches developers and procurement teams. Marine Electricals disclosed ₹115 crore of orders from L&T in April 2026 for electrical boards/panels at Tata's Dholera semiconductor facility. [Exchange disclosure](https://marineelectricals.com/images/disclosure-reports/Regulation-30-Bagging-Receiving-of-Order-of-contract-Rs.115.0.Cr.06.04.26.pdf).

**Why third:** demand spans AI and semiconductor facilities, with meaningful local fabrication, testing and integration. Existing Indian production means it should not be sold as a wholly unserved import market.

**Main condition:** technical approvals, reliability and the ability to compete on design, delivery and lifecycle support. NVIDIA's 800 VDC roadmap is a future architecture signal, not proof that every current Indian project requires that design. [NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/800-vdc-power-architecture-ai-factory/).

### 4. Specialised high-voltage connectors and power interconnects

**Make:** a defined EV high-voltage connector/contact family, or a nominated engineered power-interconnect assembly. Stamping/machining, moulding, plating, sealing and electrical testing create a deeper manufacturing proposition than importing finished connectors and attaching cables.

**Buyers and advantage:** EV manufacturers, battery-pack companies, charger manufacturers and their tier-one suppliers. These are among the businesses where the user's EV-industry connections could have the greatest direct relevance. Amphenol's Bengaluru manufacturing certification demonstrates an established local connector ecosystem. [Manufacturer certificate](https://www.amphenol-cs.com/media/wysiwyg/files/documentation/certification/ahst-india-iso9001.pdf).

**Why fourth:** repeat part demand and a credible localisation opportunity. A successful automotive connector family is not automatically qualified for high-speed AI-server interconnects; expand only through separate product programmes.

**Main condition:** electrical/thermal safety, tooling ownership, reliability qualification and competitive cost. The April 2027 truck localisation requirement specifies HV-connector fitment in the relevant assembly process; it does not establish a universal requirement that every connector be manufactured domestically. [Official PMP amendment](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/docs/policy_document/4th%20Amendment%20to%20PMP%20for%20N2%20and%20N3%20category%20of%20e-Trucks.pdf).

### 5. Specialised electronics/EV production and test machines

**Make:** a repeatable station family such as end-of-line functional/electrical testing for EV power electronics, battery-pack test equipment, or precision electronics assembly/inspection equipment. Pick one process and customer specification first.

**Buyers and advantage:** electronics manufacturers, automotive suppliers and battery/charger plants. A network can secure paid development and an acceptance-test agreement before a large manufacturing commitment. ECMS approvals for electronics capital-goods suppliers demonstrate policy interest in this part of the ecosystem. [Official first approvals](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/oct/doc20251027678301.pdf).

**Why fifth:** sales grow as domestic factories add capacity, while software, controls and service can build recurring customer relationships. It can be more defensible than a business that only makes custom general-purpose fixtures.

**Main condition:** prove cycle time, measurement accuracy, uptime and customer acceptance. An EV test station is not a semiconductor automated test system. Category-D ECMS eligibility must be matched to the actual equipment/product; generic factory automation is not automatically covered.

### 6. Advanced HDI and high-layer-count printed circuit boards

**Make:** complex bare circuit boards for a nominated industrial, automotive, communications or computing application. Build capability progressively; an HDI board, a leading-edge AI-server board and an IC package substrate are distinct products.

**Buyers and advantage:** electronics manufacturers and product OEMs. AT&S's Nanjangud operation provides direct Karnataka evidence of an established HDI manufacturing ecosystem. [AT&S Nanjangud](https://ats.net/en/campaign/nanjangud/).

**Why sixth:** strong overlap with electronics growth and genuine process manufacturing in India. Domestic board manufacture still may depend on imported laminates, chemicals and production tools; those should be tracked in the localisation plan.

**Main condition:** process yield, signal integrity, customer qualification and process/effluent infrastructure. This requires a capable process partner and significant industrialisation funding. The relevant ECMS application windows have closed for fresh applications; commercial attractiveness should not depend on assuming they remain open. Multilayer and HDI incentive categories have different thresholds and terms. [Official category table](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2124620&lang=2&reg=48).

### 7. Silicon-carbide power-semiconductor modules

**Make:** qualified power modules that package and interconnect semiconductor dies with the required thermal structure, for EV chargers, industrial converters and subsequently selected automotive applications. Begin with sourced dies and develop domestic module packaging and reliability capability.

**Buyers and advantage:** converter, charging, energy-storage and EV power-electronics manufacturers. L&T Semiconductor Technologies and Newen disclosed an April 2026 agreement involving SiC power-module development and supply for renewable-energy inverters, demonstrating a concrete Indian programme. It is evidence of a programme, not proof of completed mass production. [Company agreement](https://www.ltsct.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Press-Release_LTSCT-X-Newen-Systems-Private-Limited_21st-April.pdf).

**Why seventh:** a strong bridge between EV growth and semiconductor manufacturing. With a capable technology partner, it can accumulate valuable packaging, thermal-design and reliability expertise.

**Main condition:** die sourcing, process transfer, thermal cycling, reliability and buyer qualification. This is neither SiC wafer fabrication nor merely mounting already-packaged devices on a converter PCB. It remains separate from the advanced IC-packaging business at number ten. Scheme classification must be established for the proposed module process.

### 8. Ultra-high-purity gas and chemical-delivery systems

**Make:** a qualified family of gas cabinets, valve boxes, chemical-distribution assemblies or welded delivery systems serving semiconductor production. Work to an OEM/EPC-approved design and materials specification.

**Buyers and advantage:** fab owners, specialist equipment companies and process-utility contractors. Tata Electronics' September 2026 collaboration with Kelington illustrates the relevance of this specialist work to Dholera. [Tata–Kelington announcement](https://www.tataelectronics.com/w/tata-electronics-and-kelington-collaborate-to-accelerate-operational-readiness-of-dholera-fab).

**Why eighth:** specialised local manufacturing, installation, maintenance and modification demand. Customer access and a qualified international partner could materially strengthen an entrant's position.

**Main condition:** contamination control, compatible materials, cleaning, welding quality, leak testing and safety. Some critical valves/controllers may remain imported. The market cannot be sized using a general chemical-import percentage, and this scope must not be added again to a cleanroom/EPC total containing the same utilities.

### 9. Modular cleanroom and mini-environment systems

**Make:** engineered modular enclosures, panels, air-distribution/filtration modules and control/integration packages for selected semiconductor, electronics or related controlled-environment applications. Define which hardware is manufactured in India.

**Buyers and advantage:** fab/packaging developers, electronics factories and their EPC partners. Tata Projects' awarded Micron Sanand contract included a large cleanroom facility, establishing real contracted construction demand. [Contract announcement](https://www.tataprojects.com/web/node/1793).

**Why ninth:** useful local manufacturing and project-delivery business, with a route to retrofits and support. Strong developer/EPC access matters. It remains in the ten despite established competitors.

**Main condition:** the business must have a differentiated manufactured package and qualification capability. Full turnkey fab EPC is a different undertaking. The pasted ₹41,000–57,000 crore estimate is not verified unawarded demand available to a new company. Keep UHP fluid delivery separate; a shared site does not make it the same manufacturing process.

### 10. Advanced semiconductor packaging and testing JV

**Make:** a defined advanced IC package and test service using a partner's production-grade technology. Start with a nominated package family and anchor customers, then develop more complex products. Do not assume that ordinary chip packaging enables leading-edge GPU/HBM integration.

**Buyers and advantage:** chip designers and semiconductor product companies. Tata Electronics describes commercial semiconductor assembly/test activity at Vemgal, Karnataka, since December 2023; this establishes local ecosystem evidence, not confirmed frontier-GPU packaging capability. [Tata semiconductor assembly and test](https://www.tataelectronics.com/semiconductor-assembly-and-test).

**Why tenth:** high strategic value, meaningful semiconductor processing and substantial long-term potential. Strong finance and a serious technology JV keep it in contention.

**Main condition:** exact technology access, package yield, substrates/materials, customer commitments and capacity utilisation. The current advanced-ATMP scheme category has a ₹1,000 crore minimum capital threshold and 35% support on eligible capex, alongside revenue, technology and approval conditions. The threshold is a scheme condition, not a universal legal minimum for operating a packaging business. [Semicon 2.0 Gazette](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/275860.pdf).

## Funding that should actually affect the decision

**Semicon 2.0:** ₹1,27,500 crore is the approved programme outlay, not cash already disbursed or money available to any supplier. Eligible equipment/subassembly/component manufacturing has a ₹300 crore capital threshold and ₹120 crore revenue criterion; materials have ₹50 crore/₹20 crore thresholds; test/characterisation facilities have ₹100 crore/₹40 crore thresholds. These categories specify 30% eligible-capex support with additional conditions. Advanced and legacy ATMP have ₹1,000 crore capital and ₹200 crore revenue thresholds, with 35% and 25% support respectively. Applicant/group/JV qualification, licensed or owned technology and the applicable approval terms matter. [Gazette](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/275860.pdf), [official equipment/materials page](https://ism.gov.in/schemes/semicon2.0/machines-and-materials), [official ATMP page](https://ism.gov.in/schemes/semicon2.0/atmp-osat).

**ECMS:** the government reports an expanded ₹40,000 crore outlay. The portal reviewed for this research states that A/B/C/E application windows closed on 30 September 2025, while covered Category D remains open through 30 April 2027. Category D specifies ₹10 crore minimum investment and 25% eligible-capex support. This makes exact product classification particularly relevant to number five, and potentially some supply-chain products. It is not a blanket tooling or automation grant. Investments/sales already incentivised under another central scheme cannot simply be claimed again under ECMS. [Current government status](https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?ModuleId=16&NoteId=151016&id=151016&lang=1&reg=3), [portal](https://ecms.meity.gov.in/), [guidelines](https://ecms.meity.gov.in/documents/ECMS%20Guidelines_26.04.2025.pdf).

**IndiaAI:** the ₹10,371.92 crore mission supports the AI ecosystem; it is not a general GPU-manufacturing grant. Procurement and compute-capacity expansion can create downstream opportunities for approved products without entitling their suppliers to mission funding. [Government AI/semiconductor factsheet](https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?Id=150860&lang=1&reg=1).

**Karnataka:** assess the specific project and site under the applicable current policy and sanction terms. The existence of an ESDM incentive framework and an actual 2026 sanction disproves a blanket no-support claim; it does not establish identical eligibility for every new applicant. Bengaluru Urban/Rural land-benefit exclusions should not be confused with every other benefit. [Invest Karnataka ESDM](https://investkarnataka.co.in/key-industrie/electronic-system-design-and-manufacturing/), [June 2026 sanction](https://www.aequs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Intimation-on-ACPPL-Incentive-Order.pdf).

The latest linked manufacturing-guideline PDF could not be independently retrieved during the audit because its host returned 403. The report therefore does not claim to have verified the supplied chat's exact cleanroom/hook-up wording in those September guidelines. The Gazette, policy pages and separately retrieved ECMS guidelines support the broader distinctions above. Some official sites also returned intermittent access errors on repeat checks.

## Why other attractive ideas are outside this ten

- **AI-server and inference-appliance manufacturing/integration:** strongest commercial substitute if the network brings funded orders and component supply access. Imported GPUs do not make it a bad business. It ranks outside this manufacturing-depth shortlist because an initial integrator can retain limited domestic component value; PCBA, board design and service depth can improve that. A real anchor order can move it into the top five. [Indian manufacturing milestone](https://www.gigacomputing.com/en/news-detail/syrma-sgs-technology-limited-and-giga-computing-mark-first-build-milestone-for-server-manufacturing-in-india/).
- **Semiconductor test-interface hardware:** strongest more focused alternative: load/burn-in boards, contactor/socket mechanics and handler kits. It could replace a large JV project if faster entry or smaller initial investment becomes the priority. It was not rejected because of incumbents. [STPI Smart Lab services](https://stpi.in/en/smart-lab-services).
- **Electronic-grade chemicals:** recurring demand is attractive, but select the exact chemical, purity grade, technology owner and buyer qualification before ranking a plant. The Tata–Sumitomo September 2026 announcement is an MoU to explore supply/local manufacturing; it is not evidence that every listed chemical is already qualified and being made locally. A nominated chemical programme with credible offtake could displace number nine. [Company announcement](https://www.tataelectronics.com/w/tata-electronics-and-sumitomo-chemical-sign-mou-for-high-quality-semiconductor-materials).
- **Rare-earth permanent magnets:** strategically relevant, but the current incentive tender is closed and feedstock, alloy/process technology and customer qualification remain fundamental. Partnership or standalone investment remains possible. Competitors and scheme closure do not prohibit the business; they change the entry plan. [Tender status](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2299078&lang=1&reg=3).
- **Generic precision tooling:** can be a strong industrial business, but a broad tooling basket is less targeted to this user's AI/semiconductor/EV priority. Tooling dedicated to a nominated connector, electronics or semiconductor-equipment programme can still form part of the chosen business.
- **Fabless AI chips, a GPU competitor or a wafer fab:** potentially very large opportunities under a genuinely capable technical consortium. They require a specific workload/product, software and IP ecosystem or fab technology, plus customer design wins. They are not automatically the best first business merely because the sector's total market and subsidies are large.

## How to use the network and start in Karnataka

Use the stated connection and wider industry relationships to obtain introductions to actual procurement and engineering decision-makers, technology owners and credible project sponsors. For each shortlisted product, seek a written specification, target annual volume, qualification process, nominated commercial contact and a paid pilot or conditional order. Government and industry access can accelerate this work; it does not substitute for the buyer's technical approval or the scheme authority's decision.

For location, keep access to Bengaluru engineering and customer teams while comparing actual industrial sites for power, water, environmental requirements, logistics, labour and the applicable incentive zone. Do not infer that Dabaspet/Nelamangala, Tumakuru and Gubbi share one incentive classification. Site choice should follow the selected process, not precede it.

The immediate selection exercise should compare the top three through real customer programmes: one cooling assembly, one semiconductor-equipment part family and one power-distribution product. Obtain technology-transfer scope, an indicative manufacturing bill of materials, sample/qualification requirements and customer-backed demand. Commit the main factory investment to the strongest validated programme. The ranking should change if another category produces materially better evidence, such as funded server orders or a licensed module process with an anchor buyer.
