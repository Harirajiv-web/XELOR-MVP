# Independent semiconductor and electronics manufacturing comparison

Research cut-off: 19 September 2026. Prepared for the user's manufacturing-opportunity comparison; no outreach, quotations, commitments or applications were made.

## Recommendation and assumptions

For an initial Bengaluru-area project within the historical INR 1–30 crore envelope, the strongest independently researched starting point is **precision electronics tooling: connector mould inserts, precision stamping dies and assembly/test tooling**. It combines demonstrated Indian tooling imports, existing electronics customers, repair/replacement revenue, and a practical route from tooling into higher-value components. It does not require a wafer fab or semiconductor subsidy.

Beyond that tooling business, the four distinct semifinalists are **custom power magnetics, specialized DC-link/snubber film capacitors, semiconductor test-interface hardware, and UHP gas-delivery subassemblies**. These are alternatives to validate, not four businesses to start together. Precision semiconductor-equipment parts are also credible, but the strongest verified near-term route is supplying global equipment makers; that is an export/local-supplier-development case until an Indian buyer demonstrates an imported part it can actually replace.

Best case means access to a senior process/technical leader, an experienced quality team, funding, suitable leased industrial space, and introductions to qualified buyers. It does not mean assumed purchase orders, waived qualification, or certain subsidies. The connection to H. D. Kumaraswamy can be used for introductions to MHI-linked engineering and procurement teams, testing institutes, associations and formal vendor-development programs. Individual buyer engineering teams still decide specifications, qualification and orders. Semiconductor policy decisions belong to MeitY/ISM rather than MHI alone.

All investment ranges below are **analyst screening estimates**, not supplier quotations or verified project costs. They include a narrow manufacturing line, basic metrology/testing, development costs and initial working capital in leased premises, exclude buying land, and assume selected outsourced specialist processes. Treat the ranges as potentially varying by 50% or more until a process flow, capacity, machine quotations, staffing plan and working-capital cycle are established. A INR 10 crore total project is not automatically INR 10 crore of eligible scheme investment.

## Evidence rules

- **Measured import evidence:** official trade/industry-association data reporting imports. Broad categories cannot be treated as the startup's addressable market.
- **Procurement evidence:** a public Indian tender proves a requested article and an actual procurement route. It does not prove contract award, import origin, continuing demand, or an open tender today.
- **Supply-chain evidence:** a manufacturer identifies foreign sourcing, or an Indian distributor identifies its foreign principal. This supports a product-level dependence hypothesis but gives no audited import volume.
- **Inferred opportunity:** an adjacent factory needs a class of product. This is not evidence that the factory imports that product or will buy from a new supplier.

There is no independently verified, accessible startup TAM or margin estimate for any niche below. The decision should be made against buyer part numbers, current origin, annual usage, approved specifications and qualification budgets.

## 1. Precision electronics tooling: strongest overall evidence

The Ministry of Heavy Industries' Lok Sabha reply dated **10 February 2026** reports FY2024–25 dies, moulds and press-tool production of INR 18,400 crore, imports of INR 9,400 crore and exports of INR 2,300 crore, based on industry-association data. This is direct broad-category evidence, **not the import market for connector moulds alone**. The same reply lists machine-tool imports separately; those must not be added to the toolroom's potential sales.

Source: [MHI, Lok Sabha Question 1737, 10 February 2026](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/187/AU1737_kG5Sde.pdf?source=pqals).

**Domestic competitors/benchmarks:** [Edaran Precision India](https://edaran-precision.com/products.html) markets precision connector mould inserts; [Applied Precision Tooling India](https://www.aptindia.in/about) offers mould/press-tool inserts, jigs and fixtures; [Prime Components](https://primecomponents.net/) describes its own connector manufacturing and toolroom. These show a real Indian capability base, not an empty market. Their tolerance claims are manufacturer statements rather than independent audit results.

**Buyer route:** seek tooling-engineering and strategic-sourcing introductions at connector/electronics factories, including the relevant Indian operations of global connector companies. Molex explicitly requires its buyer or sourcing team to initiate supplier onboarding; registration alone is insufficient. Its global platform offers a real qualification process, but does not prove that a specific Indian plant currently seeks these tools. [Molex onboarding](https://supplychain.molex.com/content/molex/supplier-site/language-masters/en/supplier-resources/molex-supplier-onboarding.html). [TE's supplier portal](https://supplier.te.com/web/supplier-portal/home) publishes purchasing and supplier-quality requirements.

**Qualification risk:** tool life, repeatability over many cavities, steel/carbide quality, heat-treatment distortion, surface finishing, dimensional capability and delivery reliability. A five-axis CNC machine alone does not create an approved toolroom. Start with replacement inserts and repeatable component families before complete high-cavity moulds. Machine imports are acceptable startup inputs, but should be included when calculating net foreign-exchange displacement.

**First evidence required:** two factories' imported tool/insert purchase histories and drawings, with redesign rights, expected annual replacements, acceptance criteria and paid trial milestones. A request for introductions is useful; a subsidy presentation without these records is premature.

## 2. Custom power magnetics: strongest manufacturing adjacency

Choose a particular power range and switching-frequency family for chargers, industrial converters or renewable-energy electronics. Manufacture the magnetic assembly: electromagnetic/thermal design, core selection, bobbin/tooling, winding, insulation, termination, impregnation/potting and electrical testing. Avoid calling simple winding an indigenous core-material technology.

**Direct upstream sourcing evidence:** [Syrma SGS' magnetics page](https://syrmasgs.com/magnetics/) names Indian and Chinese ferrite sources, US/Chinese iron-powder, sendust and nanocrystalline cores, and Indian/Chinese/Taiwanese wire sources. Therefore some upstream imports are real, while other inputs already have Indian options. This evidence does not quantify imports of complete finished transformers or inductors. Do not confuse soft-magnetic ferrites with rare-earth permanent magnets.

**Domestic competitors/benchmarks:** Syrma SGS; [SVM](https://www.svmpl.in/index.html), which describes custom high-frequency/high-voltage magnetics and in-house supporting capabilities; and [MagTech & Power Conversion](https://www.magtechpower.com/product-hi.html), which markets specialized high-voltage products. These firms constrain any claim that India lacks the category.

**Buyer route:** converter and charger OEMs' power-electronics design heads nominate a magnetic part; sourcing then qualifies its cost, process and capacity. EMS firms may build the product but cannot always change a customer-nominated transformer. Obtain the design owner's approval, not only an EMS purchasing contact. In the absence of a public buyer RFQ for this exact niche, this route is an industry-process inference, not verified available demand.

**Residual imports and risks:** specialty cores, insulation systems or certain wire grades may remain foreign. Inductance alone is insufficient: leakage, insulation, partial discharge where applicable, core loss, temperature rise and long-term reliability need validation. A useful wedge is lower loss/size plus fast design changes and short lead times. Generic catalogue chokes compete directly with mature domestic suppliers.

**Go/no-go gate:** three recurring part numbers where current imported complete assemblies, not just imported cores, can be documented; the buyer must fund or co-fund qualification and accept the redesigned source. A future specialty-core project is a different, more demanding materials business.

## 3. Film capacitors: actual Indian procurement, substantial incumbency

An actual BHEL Electronics Division RFQ, **AKSPROP103 dated 6 January 2025**, requested **4,500 units of a 750 microfarad, 2,800 V DC-link capacitor**. It named ICAR SPA and Vishay Components India product alternatives. The tender requires previous relevant supply evidence, manufacturing quality controls and ISO 9001; for partially imported components, its PQR requires minimum 20% Indian value addition. The document proves procurement specifications and quantity, not the final award or each unit's manufacturing origin. Vishay's Indian entity name especially must not be mistaken for proof of imported manufacture.

Source: [BHEL capacitor RFQ and prequalification requirements](https://tenders.bhel.com/sites/default/files/Tender-2025-01-08-04%3A20%3A29.pdf). BHEL's tender listing also records **2026_BHEL_58032_1 / AKSPROP141**, dated **5 February 2026**, for the same broad 750 microfarad class; that tender closed in February and is evidence of procurement recurrence, not an open opportunity. [BHEL listing](https://tenders.bhel.com/tenders?field=&field_brief_work_description=&field_email_value=&field_nit_no__value=&field_notification_date_value=&field_notification_no__value=&field_publish_in_newspaper_value=&field_reference_number_value=&field_tender_types_value=Rate+Contract&field_tender_unit_name_value=All&page=2&title=).

**Domestic competitors/benchmarks:** [Deki's custom DC-link catalogue](https://www.dekielectronics.com/storage/products/398-Metallized-PP-DC-Link-Capacitors-Customized-pr-210225121800.pdf); [Alcon's DC-link catalogue hosted by its distribution partner](https://www.rellpower.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Alcon-DC-link-Cobrand.pdf); [Neotroniks](https://neotroniks.com/). [Meher Advanced Materials](https://meher-am.com/products/) supplies metallized film in India, so importing every film input is not an inevitable assumption either.

**Entry scope:** specialized industrial DC-link/snubber products with unusual form factor, ripple current or thermal requirements, based on experienced process engineering or a technology licence. Do not start by assuming approval for a traction application on day one. A local factory importing finished capacitors and fitting terminals is a much shallower substitute than winding, metallization sourcing, impregnation and testing locally.

**Qualification risk:** moisture, self-healing performance, ESR/ESL, ripple heating, termination integrity and life prediction. Railway tenders' prior-supply requirements make an established technical partner useful. The MHI network has a concrete use here: engineering-led vendor-development discussions at BHEL, followed by the published qualification process.

## 4. Semiconductor test-interface hardware: real buyers, controlled scope

SCL has publicly tendered a **test/burn-in socket for an 8-pin CDFP device with unformed leads**, reference SCL/PS/45061/ETQD/2024. The global tender allowed foreign-currency bids. This proves an Indian buyer for the exact article but does not establish who won, import value or current annual volume. [Government eProcurement tender](https://eprocure.gov.in/eprocure/app?component=%24DirectLink&page=FrontEndViewTender&service=direct&sp=SSO2ReGq%2BdVAaTx1zOKGRoA%3D%3D).

An ISRO procurement specification for test/burn-in jigs explicitly lists 3M and Yamaichi sockets and describes board/thermal requirements. It is direct evidence of foreign-brand content specified in an Indian procurement chain, while also demonstrating that complete jigs are already procured as customized assemblies. [ISRO technical specification, IS2025001106](https://eproc.isro.gov.in/common/viewDocument?id=ff808181986467c90198a190cadd629a&indentId=IS2025001106). ISRO's live portal also displayed IISU enquiry **IU202600278201**, PGA ZIF test/burn-in socket, due 1 October 2026, during the research; portal listings change and the buyer's complete tender controls eligibility. [ISRO procurement portal](https://eproc.isro.gov.in/home.html?sessionExpired=true).

**Competitors/benchmarks:** [Tessolve system-level test](https://www.tessolve.com/system-level-test/); [ChipTest/Wisteria's manufacturer presentation, 25 July 2025](https://chiptest.chiptest.in/downloads/ChipTest%20Corporate%20Presentation%20-%20Jul%2025%2C%202025%20-%20Net.pdf), covering load/burn-in boards and handler hardware; and [TPST's own SEMICON exhibitor description](https://expo.semi.org/india2025/Public/eBooth.aspx?BoothID=644539&FromPage=Exhibitors.aspx&IndexInList=1&ListByBooth=true&ParentBoothID=), which describes tooling/spares and semiconductor test support. These sources show overlapping offerings, not that every advertised component is fabricated in India. Global socket competition includes [Enplas](https://ic-socket-solutions.com/en/test-sockets/).

**Buyer route:** device/test engineers at SCL, Indian packaging/test plants, chip design firms and test-service companies; qualify one device family and its approved tester. [Micron's supplier page](https://www.micron.com/about/company/suppliers) is a genuine formal procurement route, but is global and supplies no public promise of Indian orders. Public [STPI SMART Lab](https://stpi.in/en/smart-lab-services) and [STQC component laboratories](https://www.stqc.gov.in/en/component-testing) already exist; the business should supply useful hardware or use shared validation rather than duplicate an entire general test laboratory.

**Entry scope:** socket housings, contactor mechanical parts, replacement handler kits and fixtures, combined with electrical design and assembly. Advanced spring contacts, fine-pitch probe technology, multilayer low-loss boards and high-bandwidth connectors can remain imported initially. Their value must be separated from local engineering and machining.

**Qualification risk:** contact resistance and repeatability, insertion life, coplanarity, thermal expansion, signal/power integrity and device damage. Mature test and burn-in fixtures are a more credible first step than a MEMS probe-card platform or full high-end automatic test equipment.

## 5. UHP subassemblies: technical-partner condition

The intended imported article is a qualified gas stick, welded tube assembly, manifold or complete inert-gas panel purchased as part of equipment/facilities construction. The proposed process localizes fabrication, welding, cleaning, assembly, leak testing and documentation. It does not initially replace the physics/control IP of a mass-flow controller or all specialty valve internals.

**Competitors and upstream evidence:** [Joscotec](https://www.joscotec.com/gasvacuum-system/) describes gas-box/stick work using brands including MKS, Brooks, Horiba, APTech, Parker and Swagelok. [AMS Worldwide](https://amsworldwide.in/gas-product-delivery-system/) identifies its relationship with PNC Systems for gas/chemical systems. [Techsol's SEMICON 2026 exhibitor material](https://expo.semi.org/india2026/Public/eBooth.aspx?BoothID=696078&SSID=13236&Task=PressReleases), dated 14 July 2026, describes UHP products and component offerings. These are direct evidence of active suppliers and foreign-product integration, not measured Indian UHP import share.

**Buyer route:** approved semiconductor equipment OEMs and EPC/facility contractors, followed by site owner acceptance. The equipment or fab owner's approved component list often controls purchasing. There is now a concrete development channel: the official **18 September 2026** SEMICON release describes Tata Electronics' agreement with Kelington Engineering for gas distribution, high-purity piping and tool hook-up, including training of local partners. This supports approaching the program's formal supplier-development chain; it is not a published open RFQ or a guaranteed subcontract. No public Indian RFQ establishing accessible annual volumes for this proposed startup was verified. [PIB, 18 September 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2312234&lang=1&reg=1), independently retrieved through a direct HTTP client after the web reader failed.

**Qualification risk:** surface condition, weld quality, particles, moisture, traceability, helium leak performance and approved cleaning. Start with specified inert-gas assemblies and a qualified experienced technical leader. Toxic/pyrophoric process-gas systems are a separate engineering and qualification expansion. Imported regulators, valves, MFCs and some electropolished tubing may remain, so the initial substitution is partial.

## 6. Precision semiconductor-equipment parts: separate export and import narratives

[TEAM D](https://www.teamd.in/), [Millworks](https://millworksindia.com/semiconductor/) and [Microtech](https://www.microtechblr.com/) describe Indian precision manufacturing relevant to semiconductor equipment. [Applied Materials India](https://www.appliedmaterials.com/in/en/about/india-overview.html) describes its local engineering/lab ecosystem. An official [Applied supplier-account-manager role](https://jobs.appliedmaterials.com/job/bengaluru/supplier-account-manager-oem/95/92676364512), posted in June 2026, specifically referred to domestic-market supply-chain development. These are useful signs, but a job advertisement is not a purchase order.

[Lam's India page](https://www.lamresearch.com/careers/global-opportunities/india/) describes substantial global procurement activity managed from India; this must not be translated into the same amount of locally consumed imported components. Lam also publishes [supplier onboarding/responsible supply-chain expectations](https://www.lamresearch.com/company/environmental-social-and-governance/responsible-supply-chain/).

**Route A — export:** machine an approved part in Bengaluru, ship to overseas equipment assembly, earn export revenue. This can be commercially excellent but does not directly replace a current import by an Indian fab. **Route B — domestic import replacement:** an Indian plant/equipment builder approves a locally made replacement for a currently imported part. This requires part-specific evidence, warranty rights and qualification. Never add Route A and B together as one Indian import-substitution market.

The practical entry excludes the hardest plasma-facing ceramics/coatings and IP-sensitive chamber internals until a technical partnership is established. Local machining may still rely on imported machines, special alloys, seals and qualified coatings. Customer-approved drawings and process qualification, not a general CNC capability, determine sales.

## 7–9. Why three attractive-sounding ideas do not make the first shortlist

**Packaging consumables:** [Dharitree Polychem](https://dharitreepolychem.in/) advertises Indian carrier-tape manufacture, while [Abhikush Fusion](https://abhikushfusion.com/) combines packaging supply with import-management services. A genuine niche may exist in a difficult pocket geometry, tray bake temperature or approved conductive material. Generic carrier tape is already served; a new company's moat must be established against warpage, ESD, contamination and volume economics. Thermoformed reel tape is not equivalent to a semiconductor-qualified high-temperature JEDEC tray.

**Thermal materials:** [Nolato Bangalore](https://www.nolato.com/group-companies/nolato-bangalore/) describes local TIM/potting capability; [Electo](https://www.electo.co.in/thermal-interface-material-silicone-potting-compound-manufacturer-in-india-electo/) markets domestic products; [Pantronics' Shin-Etsu principal page](https://pantronicsindia.com/our-principals/shinetsu/) demonstrates an established foreign-material channel. These sources contradict a blanket claim that India imports everything in this category. Real formulation, filler dispersion and long-life qualification are valuable; die-cutting imported pads offers limited independence. Specialty fillers and polymers may remain foreign.

**Bare-die power-module packaging:** [Kaynes' official release, 15 October 2025](https://www.kaynestechnology.co.in/doc/Stock-Exchange-Disclosures/Press%20release-%20Kaynes%20Semicon%2C%20AOS%20and%20Mitsui.pdf) documents a shipment of intelligent power modules in a partnership with AOS/Mitsui. This is a real incumbent/technology-partner model. A small laboratory can develop a module, but production yield, die sourcing, ceramic substrates, bonding, moulding and automotive reliability need a separately costed plan. Do not confuse assembling a converter PCB from packaged chips with manufacturing the semiconductor package itself.

The [18 September 2026 official SEMICON release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2312234&lang=1&reg=1) also identifies SiC module initiatives involving POWERGRID and L&T Semiconductor Technologies. It announces Tata partnerships for lead-frame localization, wet chemicals and high-purity gases. These are evidence of organized localization programs with technology partners, not vacant market categories accessible without equivalent know-how.

**If choosing the final top-ten slot between test interfaces and ball screws:** the present evidence favors narrowly defined semiconductor contactor/handler-interface hardware. Specific Indian procurement is verified, and one device family can establish qualification before large expansion. Ball screws require a separately validated partner, thread-grinding and heat-treatment route, preload/accuracy/life capability and production economics; none was independently costed here. This is an evidence-based preference, not a finding that ball screws cannot be viable. If production/test machines already occupy another slot, keep the categories distinct: complete automotive/electrical end-of-line equipment versus replaceable semiconductor device-contact and handler-interface hardware. Do not count generic test jigs twice.

## Scheme fit: ECMS is closer in scale, but product eligibility is not automatic

The [official ECMS portal](https://ecms.meity.gov.in/) states that applications for segments A, B, C and E closed on **30 September 2025**, while segment D remains open through **30 April 2027**. Therefore the fact that capacitors or electromechanical components are named somewhere in ECMS does not make their closed application window available to a new applicant now.

The [ECMS guidelines dated 26 April 2025](https://ecms.meity.gov.in/documents/ECMS%20Guidelines_26.04.2025.pdf), read as a scanned official PDF, provide:

- D(10), supply chain for specified subassemblies/bare components, and D(11), capital goods for electronics manufacturing including their subassemblies/components: minimum cumulative incremental investment **INR 10 crore**; capex incentive **25%** subject to conditions.
- Of that headline incentive, **5 percentage points depend on the employment condition**; the guidelines describe **20%** when the investment/commercial-production conditions are met but the employment condition is not.
- Annexure 3 lists indicative employment per crore of investment: D(10) 10 and D(11) 20. Thus a INR 10 crore D(11) project could imply 200 employees for the extra 5 percentage points under the stated proportional rule. Confirm the precise calculation with the scheme's project management agency; do not assume a highly automated small toolroom obtains the full 25%.
- The equipment-investment definition includes tools, dies, moulds, jigs and fixtures used to manufacture target goods. This is **not proof that every standalone toolroom is itself an eligible target-goods manufacturer**. D(11) classification of an electronics-specific tool or test-hardware business needs a written product-specific determination.
- Contract manufacturing of the target goods does not qualify as the applicant's own manufacturing merely because it owns the brand; limited domestic job work is treated differently. Investments or sales already incentivized under another central-government scheme cannot simply be counted again.

Relevant guideline locations: printed pp.8–9 for eligibility/manufacturing; p.14 for capex/employment incentive split; pp.31 and 33 for D categories and thresholds. There are two cover pages before printed page 1. Official [8 April 2025 notification](https://ecms.meity.gov.in/documents/ECMS%20Notification%20notified%20on%2008.04.2025.pdf).

**Semicon India 2.0 is materially larger in scale.** The original Gazette notification dated **31 August 2026** was downloaded from [the Government of India Gazette](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/275860.pdf), and its English eligibility table was visually inspected. It specifies minimum capital investment/prior revenue of INR 300/120 crore for the semiconductor equipment/refurbishment/subassembly/components category, INR 50/20 crore for semiconductor raw materials, INR 100/40 crore for test/characterization, and INR 1,000/200 crore for OSAT categories. These are scheme eligibility thresholds, not the minimum lawful cost of operating any small supplier business. The linked official [PIB notification release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2304963&lang=1&reg=3) corroborates the new scheme's notification.

The original Gazette downloaded successfully through a direct HTTP client even though the web reader could not parse it. SHA-256: E318148E06B898FD3FA48BAAC4F08A996AE8FD4FB859E84FFA66C80B8EA5D51A. It is byte-identical to the [Taiwan government-hosted copy](https://www.trade.gov.tw/App_Ashx/File.ashx?FileID=DAE734E10C974745). Primary citation should remain the Indian Gazette. Supplier businesses should be assessed on unsubsidized economics; possible ECMS support is an upside conditional on classification, application and approval.

## What to establish before choosing the factory

1. Select two candidate processes, led by tooling plus one distinct alternative. Obtain at least three actual imported part numbers from each potential anchor customer; document current origin, annual consumption, landed cost, lead time and failure/qualification requirements.
2. Secure a technical leader or licensed process owner who has already delivered the relevant quality level. Seek paid sample/development orders and a written qualification schedule; an introduction or nonbinding expression of interest is not an order.
3. Build a bill of materials and process map separating domestic value addition from imported machines, cores, films, contacts, chips, valves and chemical inputs. Measure net import displacement at the exact product level.
4. Obtain machine/testing quotations and working-capital terms. Compare operating economics with zero subsidy and with a conservative approved-incentive scenario. Use the political/industry network to reach the right engineering and vendor-development desks, then follow their documented process.

This research supports a practical manufacturing shortlist and identifies what is still unverified. It does not support a blanket assertion that any of these categories is 90–100% imported, competition-free, immediately grant-eligible, or assured an order by political access.
