# Current position — 22 September 2026

## What the founder wants

Start a meaningful manufacturing business in Karnataka that can sell across India and eventually internationally. The central aim is to build useful Indian manufacturing capability and reduce dependence on imported products as AI, semiconductors and electric vehicles grow.

The founder asked us to assume a best-case ability to arrange capital, technical partners and introductions. The founder reports a direct connection to H. D. Kumaraswamy and a strong network. These are user-provided context, not independently verified purchase commitments or government approvals.

Competition is part of the market. Existing manufacturers are not, by themselves, a reason to reject an opportunity. The founder wants simple explanations and clear distinctions between what is known, what is projected and what needs buyer confirmation.

## How the scope changed

The discussion began with broad manufacturing and EV opportunities, then concentrated on AI infrastructure, semiconductor equipment and components, and government incentives. It produced several dated top-ten rankings and two HTML field guides.

The founder explored liquid cooling for AI servers, its customers and technology risks, and compared it with semiconductor-equipment components. A Fujifilm materials announcement prompted a wider look at the suppliers that grow around a semiconductor factory.

The founder then asked for something larger than a basic machine-parts workshop. Large wafer fabs and advanced packaging projects were discussed, but the founder preferred a substantial ecosystem business rather than a national-scale megaproject. Chemicals and gas-delivery systems were considered, followed by a request for non-chemical alternatives.

The latest focus is therefore a **specialised product or equipment business in the semiconductor ecosystem**, with the exact product still to be selected through technical and customer evidence.

## The latest non-chemical shortlist

| Product family | Meaning in simple words | What is still needed |
|---|---|---|
| Chip-testing sockets and boards | Hold a chip and connect it to a tester. | A particular chip/package, test platform, electrical requirements and buyer qualification plan. |
| Automated chip-handling machines | Move chips into testing or packaging stations and sort them. | A defined production task, performance targets, integration requirements and customer trial. |
| Chip-inspection machines | Use precise cameras and software to identify defined package defects. | A specific defect-detection task, acceptable error rates, throughput and validation samples. |
| Leadframes | Small metal frames that support and connect certain packaged chips. | Package drawings, process partner, volume commitment and reliability approval. These are not GPU/HBM substrates. |
| Protective chip trays and carriers | Protect chips from damage, contamination and static during handling. | Approved materials, dimensions, cleanliness and compatibility with the buyer's machines. |
| Vacuum chambers and assemblies | Controlled, low-pressure chambers used inside manufacturing equipment. | Equipment-maker requirements, cleanliness and leak testing, and process qualification. |
| Advanced chip-package substrates | Complex platforms beneath powerful chips that carry their connections. | Specialist process technology, much more demanding manufacturing capability and a committed package customer. These are not ordinary circuit boards. |

A testing-and-automation company was suggested as one possible direction: start with a selected interface product, then expand into handling and inspection after customer validation. This is a provisional business concept, not a final investment decision or a commitment to launch three product lines together.

## What the research supports

- AI-related demand is placing pressure on selected memory, advanced packaging, package substrates and power-management chips.
- India has operating packaging/testing and discrete-device projects, while major additional fabrication and packaging facilities are still being built.
- Public announcements identify actual test-interface contracts, exploratory Indian leadframe localisation, equipment spending and formal supplier qualification processes.
- Karnataka can be an engineering/manufacturing base serving national projects and global equipment supply chains. Managing procurement from India does not mean all procured items are currently made in India.

Sources and the exact limitations of these findings are in [document 03](03-semiconductor-explained.md), [document 04](04-network-questions-and-findings.md) and the [source guide](06-source-guide.md).

## What the research does not yet establish

There is no selected customer-approved drawing, buyer-specific annual quantity, negotiated selling price, verified project margin, paid trial commitment, production order, available technology licence or approved subsidy for this proposed company.

No buyer was contacted or asked to place an order during the research recorded here. No particular shortage should be converted into an assumption that any newly formed Indian company can sell into it.

The old capex tables and rankings remain available as research history. They must be rebuilt around the selected product and current supplier quotations before an investment decision. A budget for support brackets is not a budget for qualified process chambers; a PCB plant is not automatically a package-substrate plant; conventional packaging is not automatically GPU/HBM packaging.

## The next information to obtain

1. Meet a buyer's purchasing, technical/quality and factory operations teams.
2. Obtain a specific product requirement, current purchases, target price and reason to add an Indian supplier.
3. Agree the sample tests, approval authority, timetable and cost responsibilities.
4. Identify a partner with proven production technology, and establish what know-how and rights can actually transfer.
5. Build the factory, cash-flow and incentive case around those requirements.
6. Seek a paid pilot, pass the tests and obtain a repeat production order on workable terms.

The government's policy objective, the size of a global market and the founder's network help explain why to investigate. The buyer's product, price and approval requirements determine which factory to build.
