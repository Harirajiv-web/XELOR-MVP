The problem

A factory’s work is one chain. Its software is a set of islands.

3–4×

is how often each number gets typed: Tally for billing, a register for stock, WhatsApp for suppliers, a diary for the floor.

The order that arrives on Monday touches sales, planning, stores, the floor, quality, dispatch and accounts before it leaves the gate. Each keeps its own record, and a person carries the number between them by hand every evening.

On an illustrative ₹20 crore plant that adds up to about ₹1.7 crore a year in stops, scrap, idle stock and retyping, built from published benchmarks. A pilot measures the real figure.

Competitive landscape

ERP features are table stakes. The gap is the record a factory leaves behind.

  • If Zoho ERP, Odoo or TranZact go deeper

    They already have planning, RFQs and receipts, and they cost less per user. We do not win on features or sticker price. We win on doing the setup ourselves, pricing per plant, and the record.

  • If Tally adds manufacturing

    Tally shipped document AI for accounting in June 2026. Production, purchase and quality beside Tally are still open, and XELOR runs beside Tally from day one.

  • If an order platform builds the record

    Zetwerk and Wootz hold supplier data for their own orders. IndiaMART has reach but no receipts. None of them sits inside the factory where a delivery is counted.

  • Why the record is the moat

    Dated deliveries and inspections cannot be scraped or back-filled. They accumulate with every receipt. They also need many plants in one cluster, which we have not built yet.

Risks, the ones we would ask about

Four things could go wrong. Each has a mitigation and an early signal.

Our price is several times the alternatives

TranZact lists from ₹12,500 a month and Tally costs ₹67,500 once. We ask ₹18 lakh a year. A pilot converts only when it shows five times the fee in measured value, in writing.

SignalFewer than half of completed pilots convert at the listed price

The product cannot host a real factory yet

Demo data ships inside the database and nothing backs it up. Ten named fixes come before the first factory’s data goes in, and every later customer needs the same ten.

SignalThe first factory is not live inside its agreed pilot window

Setup is where ERP projects fail

We configure one tested core and do the migration ourselves. That is people work, and it can eat the margin if each plant becomes a custom job.

SignalAn install runs past its time box, or recurring margin stays under 65%

The supplier record needs density

A record across many factories is worth far more than one inside a factory. It needs plants concentrated in one cluster and their consent to share.

SignalEarly customers are spread across clusters instead of packed into Peenya

What we will not do: show a number we cannot measure, claim a certification, call a simulation live, or promise predictive maintenance before there is failure history to learn from.
