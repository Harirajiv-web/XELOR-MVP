09 / Business model

Factories pay per plant. Suppliers join free.

Paid pilot₹3 L

Six weeks, one workflow, a signed baseline. Credited in full on conversion.

One plant₹50k / month

₹6 lakh a year. The whole plant, the agent, two standard connectors, every user included.

Suppliers₹0

A free record, quotes from a link and a capacity calendar. Every supplier is a future plant.

Later · plannedEarly pay

A share of supplier financing through TReDS partners, priced on the earned record. Not live.

5× rule. A pilot converts at full price only after it shows ₹30 lakh of measurable yearly value against a signed baseline.

Launch prices under test. No customer has accepted them yet; they are fixed after five paid pilots in each segment. Capacity bookings carry no fee.
