The problem

Finding a good supplier is still guesswork.

Behind almost every product made in India is a chain of small factories. They still find each other the old way.

WhatsApp, calls and word of mouth

Owners ask around, chase replies and make long site visits just to trust one new supplier.

Listing sites cost too much

A paid listing is ₹32,000 or more a year. Most small businesses can’t afford it.

No way to know who’s reliable

So owners take risks, or pay more just to be safe. Good suppliers stay invisible.

Paperwork everywhere

Orders in WhatsApp, stock in Excel, accounts in Tally, quality on paper. The same details get typed again and again.
