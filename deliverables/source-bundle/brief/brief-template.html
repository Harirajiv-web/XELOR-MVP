06 / The record that compounds

Earned at the gate.
Never typed in.

A supplier’s record comes from what actually arrives. Nobody types a score.

01

Counted, not typed

On-time and reject rates come from goods receipts and incoming inspections.

Reads “unknown” until a delivery is counted.
02

Unproven means unproven

A supplier that has never delivered scores nothing for track record.

It never borrows anyone’s average.
03

A one-minute quote

The request arrives as a message. The supplier answers from a private link.

No account, no app, one required field.

Ranked with reasons.
A person decides.

07 / Business model

Per plant.
Never per seat.

A factory decision crosses departments, so every user is included. The first payment is the pilot.

Paid pilot₹3 L

Six weeks, one workflow, a signed baseline. Credited in full on conversion.

InstallationQuotedper plant

Data migration from Tally and spreadsheets, and commissioning. We do the setup ourselves.

One plant₹50k/ month

₹6 lakh a year. The whole plant, two standard connectors, every user included.

More plantsGroup rate

Each extra factory joins at a group discount, with one view across every plant.

5×

A pilot converts at full price only after it shows ₹30 lakh of measurable yearly value, five times the yearly fee, against a signed baseline.Launch prices under test. No customer has accepted them yet; they are fixed after five paid pilots in each segment.

09 / What must become defensible

Features are table stakes.
The record is the moat.

We do not win on features or sticker price. Here is what happens if others move.

If Zoho, Odoo or TranZact go deeper

We win on setup and pricing

We do the setup ourselves, price per plant, and keep the record.

If Tally adds manufacturing

We already run beside Tally

Production, purchase and quality beside Tally are still open. XELOR works with it from day one.

If an order platform builds the record

They are outside the factory

Zetwerk and Wootz see their own orders. None of them sits where a delivery is counted.

Why the record wins

It cannot be back-filled

Dated deliveries and inspections build up with every receipt. It needs many plants in one cluster, which we have not built yet.
