01 · What WOLM is

An AI assistant that does the sales admin for you

Sales people spend hours after every customer meeting writing notes, sending emails and updating records. WOLM does that part automatically, before, during and after each meeting.

Before

You walk in prepared

WOLM builds a one-page brief on the customer.

  • Company news and positioning
  • Risk and deal-stage scoresHow likely the deal is, and where it stands
During

It listens, any way you like

Three ways to capture the conversation:

  • A bot joins the video call
  • A Chrome browser extension
  • A photo of your notebook or a voice noteFor in-person meetings, or when the client says no to bots
After

The work gets done

Minutes after the meeting ends:

  • Tasks for each team memberEveryone knows what to do next
  • Follow-up email draftedReady to send in Gmail or Outlook
  • Sales records updatedTwo-way sync with Salesforce
In one sentence

The AI sales assistant that turns every customer conversation into work that gets done.

Who it's for
  • Small B2B sales teamsBusinesses that sell to other businesses
  • Field sales repsPeople who meet customers on-site, not at a desk
?
CRM (e.g. Salesforce) is the system where a sales team keeps its customer list and tracks every deal. Keeping it up to date is the chore most reps put off.
