### 1.3 Churn by tier (company disclosures on earnings calls)

- **Platinum: about 0.5% a month (6–7% a year). Gold: about 1% a month (12–14% a year). Silver monthly: 7–8% a month. Silver annual: about 40% a year.** These are historical management statements. Source: https://www.medianama.com/2024/05/223-indiamart-looks-to-grow-customer-addition-earnings-call-q4fy23-24/ ; https://www.indiainfoline.com/article/news-top-story/q-fy-review-indiamart-no-respite-on-churn-rates-as-yet--1705648421413_1.html
- More recent statement: Silver monthly churn is about 7%, up from a historical 5–6%. Silver annual is about 4% a month, up from 2–2.5%. Gold and Platinum are about 1% a month or less. Source: same search summary, via medianama and alphastreet. Check against the transcript.
- Q1 FY27 call (July 2026), CEO Dinesh Agarwal: "From that 7% silver monthly, I think nothing has changed, and that's why we are continuing to be negative or flattish total number of customers." Most churn happens in the first 12 months. He rejected the idea that churn is structural, saying the CAC/LTV economics fail unless retention improves. Source: https://www.medianama.com/2026/07/223-indiamart-msme-lending-arm-q1-fy2027-earnings/
- **Gold and Platinum make up about 50% of paying customers and more than 75% of revenue.** Source: same as above, and https://www.gurufocus.com/news/8836186/indiamart-intermesh-ltd-bom542726-q4-2026-earnings-call-highlights-strong-revenue-growth-amidst-supplier-challenges?mobile=true

### 1.5 Lead model

- **Enquiries** are buyer messages or calls sent directly to a supplier's catalog. **BuyLeads** are buyer RFQs that IndiaMART posts and allocates to multiple suppliers, who then "consume" them to unlock buyer contact details.
- Weekly BuyLead allocation by package is listed in the table above. Leads are allocated on Sunday at 00:00 and lapse on Saturday at 23:59. Sellers marked as "PNS defaulter" or "email defaulter" (for not answering calls) are cut to 7 BuyLeads a week. Source: https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/buy-lead-allocation ; https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/daily-bonus-buy-lead-allocation-pns-defaulter-or-email-defaulter/ ; https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/weekly-leads-allocation-time/
- Extra BuyLeads can be bought. Snippets give "lead price starts at Rs 50" and IndiaMART Advantage Program "1 BuyLead = 200 points". The exact current top-up price list was not found. Source: https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/buylead-cost ; https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/indiamart-advantage-program
- **PNS (Preferred Number Service)** is a virtual number shown to buyers that rings all of a seller's linked numbers at once. It needs at least 3 numbers, with up to 5 linked (another page says up to 8), and supports office or non-office routing. Calls show as +91 80-7199-7199 or +91 80-4299-4299. Source: https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/preferred-number-service ; https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/add-update-view-pns-number ; https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/pns-office-hours-of-indiamart
- The structural point that matters for XELOR: **one BuyLead goes to many sellers.** Sellers complain that a lead is "sold to 10+ sellers" (see section 2). PNS also lets IndiaMART meter and penalise how responsive a seller is.
- AI (June 2026): IndiaMART plans to double AI spend every six months. Uses include detecting proxy or fake accounts through pattern matching, and real-time voice-to-text for buyer requirement capture, which call-centre staff used to do. IndiaMART was on the USTR 2022 "Notorious Markets" list for counterfeits. Source: https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/06/25/indiamart-doubles-down-on-ai-to-curb-fake-listings-improve-buyer-interaction ; https://letsdatascience.com/news/indiamart-boosts-ai-spending-to-fight-fake-listings-084102c1
- New: the board approved **IndiaMART Finance Ltd** (lending subsidiary for working capital to sellers), subject to RBI approval (July 2026). Source: https://inc42.com/buzz/indiamart-q1-profit-rises-12-yoy-to-₹172-cr-plans-lending-foray/ ; https://www.whalesbook.com/corporate-news/English/technology/IndiaMART-Q1-FY27-Profit-indian-rupee172-Cr-New-Finance-Subsidiary-Approved/6a64d38198a9268fb175a927

## 5. Price point and the features sellers value (analysis, grounded in the facts above)

Anchors (all cited above):
- IndiaMART's cheapest real tier (Silver/MDC) costs **₹32,000–₹35,000/yr + GST**, or **₹4,000/month**. After the September 2025 hike, about 7% of Silver monthly sellers churn every month.
- IndiaMART's average paying seller spends **₹69K/yr** (ARPU).
- Busy accounting starts at **about ₹3,600/yr**, so MSMEs already pay roughly this much for core software.
- ONDC, GeM, Google Business Profile and WhatsApp catalogs are **free**.

Analysis (not sourced fact):
- To be credibly "far lower", a price needs to be **at least 10x below IndiaMART Silver**. That means about **₹2,000–3,500/yr (₹199–299/month)**, which sits within the range MSMEs already pay for accounting or SaaS. A free tier is needed to compete with the free government rails. A price of ₹9,999+/yr would read as "IndiaMART-lite" rather than "far lower".
- Sustainability depends on cost structure. IndiaMART's churn is concentrated among Silver sellers who were sold by a field or tele-sales force. A no-sales-team, self-serve model (onboarding via the XELOR ERP and cluster associations in Peenya) avoids CAC of the order of a year's fee.
- Features sellers ask for, read off the complaints: (1) **exclusive or limited-share leads** rather than one lead sent to 10+ sellers; (2) **verified buyers** (GSTIN, Udyam, confirmed requirement); (3) **no spam calls**, with masked contact details and consent-based reveal; (4) **no auto-debit traps**, so easy cancellation and no e-NACH lock-in; (5) **transparent ROI**, meaning leads, responses and won orders tracked; (6) honest sales promises.

## 6. Gap: verified, transaction-based supplier performance

- **What IndiaMART TrustSEAL verifies is identity and existence, not performance.**
  - Checks: GST validation via OTP, bank account name match, a video-KYC call confirming proof of identity, products and location, then certification.
  - Displayed fields: proprietor or director name, GSTIN, address, IEC, mobile and email.
  - Documents required: GST certificate, PAN, and registration proof (Udyam, incorporation certificate or shop licence).
  - Sources: https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/trustseal-pro/ ; https://trustseal.indiamart.com/members/sunshineindustries-indore ; https://trustseal.indiamart.com/members/jptradingghaziabad
- TrustSEAL is a paid product (about ₹50K/yr, above) and is bundled in higher tiers. **The badge is bought, not earned through delivery performance.** Buyers report being scammed by "verified" 4.9★ sellers (https://www.smartcustomer.com/reviews/indiamart.com?page=3).
- No evidence was found, within the searches made, of any Indian B2B directory (IndiaMART, TradeIndia, ExportersIndia, JD Mart) publishing **transaction-verified on-time delivery or rejection rates**. These directories do not see the transaction, by design.
- **UNVERIFIED background:** Alibaba.com shows metrics such as "on-time delivery rate" and "response time" on supplier profiles, based on orders placed through its Trade Assurance payment system. That data exists only because Alibaba handles the money. A non-payment marketplace like XELOR could instead source performance data from the **buyer's ERP**: GRN dates against PO due dates, and QC rejection quantities, recorded in XELOR/ONYX by buyer factories. This is a possible structural differentiator that needs no escrow. It must be opt-in and consented, scoped per buyer–tenant, and labelled with sample size. Synthetic fixtures must never be presented as real performance, as the workspace rules already require.
