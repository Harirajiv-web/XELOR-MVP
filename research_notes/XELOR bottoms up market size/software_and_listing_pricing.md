Research date: 2026-10-03. Scope is India only. XELOR price points being tested: **ERP at ₹6 lakh/year per plant** (factories of about 100+ people), and **XELOR Market listing free, or "Verified" at ₹249/month (₹2,988/year)**.

- Many vendor price figures come from resellers and partners, not official price pages. This is noted where it applies.
- Figures without a source are marked **[UNVERIFIED – background knowledge]**. Figures I calculated are marked "derived".

## 1. ERP and software adoption among Indian MSMEs

### Takeaway
The best recent figure is that **about 12% of Indian MSMEs use ERP software**, from an RIS survey run with Kantar on 2,882 enterprises (2025). Adoption rises sharply with firm size. Weak vendor data suggests medium firms are around 60–70% "digitally adopted" against about 18% for micro firms, but no rigorous 2024–26 survey split by size and manufacturing was found. The 12% is an all-MSME average dominated by micro firms. For XELOR's target of 100+ person factories, which are small and medium manufacturers, current ERP use is likely much higher, and for many the realistic sale is **replacing Tally or Busy plus Excel**, not a first-ever system.

### Cited Findings
- **12% of Indian MSMEs use ERP software** (RIS survey, Kantar fieldwork, 2,882 enterprises, 2025). This came from a search summary that drew on India ERP market-report pages — [Mordor Intelligence, India ERP market](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/india-enterprise-resource-planning-market); [Ken Research, India ERP software market 2026–2031](https://www.kenresearch.com/industry-reports/india-enterprise-resource-planning-software-market.md). The underlying report is the RIS "MSME Digitalisation Report", which names KANTAR as survey partner and 2,882 enterprises surveyed — [RIS MSME Digitalisation Report (PDF)](https://ris.org.in/sites/default/files/Publication/MSME_Digitalisation_Report.pdf). **I could not open the PDF, so the exact wording of the 12% (ERP specifically, or "ERP/business software") and its publication year are unconfirmed.**
- India had **7.61 crore registered MSMEs (Udyam) as of January 2026, of which 1.60 crore were manufacturing**. Same search summary (market-report pages above). Check against Udyam/MoMSME data.
- Cloud took **71.48% of new ERP licences in India in 2025**. SME ERP spend is forecast at **19.2% CAGR for 2026–2031**, against 17.83% for large enterprises — [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/india-enterprise-resource-planning-market). This is a paid-report forecast, not a measured figure.
- Adoption by size, **low-quality source**: "Micro (1–5 employees) 18% digital adoption, about $240/yr software spend; Small (6–50) 42%, about $1,850/yr; Medium (51–250) 67%, about $8,900/yr". The same source says SaaS penetration among Indian SMBs is "below 15%" against 60–70% in the US — [Mewayz, Indian SMB Software Market Report](https://mewayz.com/en/blog/the-indian-smb-software-market-report-opportunities-in-the-worlds-largest-smb-pool). This is a vendor blog citing "platform data" with no stated method. **Use only as a directional indicator.**
- Older baseline: about 5% of MSME companies used computerised accounts (2011 study) — [NDIM Review of Professional Management (PDF)](https://rpm.ndimdelhi.org/doi/pdf/10.1177/rpm.2011.9.2.33.pdf). An IndiaMART-commissioned 2019 release said over 50% of MSME transactions were recorded on apps — [IndiaMART corporate, Nov 2019](https://corporate.indiamart.com/2019/11/11/msme-account-books-go-digital-over-50-transactions-recorded-using-apps-by-small-businesses/).
- The installed base of accounting software is large (paying-customer counts are in section 2). Tally has about 27 lakh customers (FY26), Vyapar over 10 million businesses on its user base, and Busy "over two lakh users" (2022).

### Inferences
- **For an all-MSME TAM, 12% ERP use is the defensible, citable figure.** For a SAM of manufacturers with 100+ employees, apply a much higher current software-use rate. A planning assumption of **about 60–70% using accounting software, and perhaps 25–40% using some ERP or manufacturing module**, is consistent with the weak size-split data but is **not survey-backed**. Label it as an assumption.
- Tally's about 27 lakh customers against about 1.6 crore manufacturing Udyam units (and 7.6 crore MSMEs overall) suggests accounting software reaches a few percent of all MSMEs by count, but likely a majority of GST-filing small and medium firms. Derived and directional only: Tally's customers include traders and service firms, and the counts are not like-for-like.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 survey from SIDBI, CRISIL, KPMG, Zinnov, Google/KPMG or NASSCOM giving ERP adoption by firm size or for manufacturing was found in search. The RIS 12% is the only recent primary-survey figure surfaced.
- The exact definition of "ERP" in the RIS survey, and whether a size breakdown exists, need the PDF.
- [UNVERIFIED – background knowledge] Earlier reports from Zinnov, Google/KPMG and Kalaari/NASSCOM (2017–2022) sized the Indian SMB SaaS market at a few billion USD and called SMB SaaS "early". No specific figure can be cited from this session.

## 2. Pricing and paying-user counts for SME tools

### Takeaway
Indian MSME software sits in three price bands:
- **₹700–₹10K/year**: billing and accounting (Vyapar, Busy, Tally Silver rental, Zoho Books lower tiers).
- **₹60K–₹3.6L/year**: SME manufacturing ERP-lite (TranZact paid plans; Zoho Books Ultimate about ₹1.2L).
- **₹10L–₹1 cr first year**: mid-market ERP with implementation (SAP B1, NetSuite, Dynamics BC, Odoo with a partner).

Revenue per customer at the mass-market leaders is only about **₹3–7K/year**. The vertical manufacturing SME ERP (TranZact) is small: **₹5.9 cr revenue in FY25 despite more than 20,000 SMEs onboarded**, mostly on a free tier.

## 3. What a 100–500 person Indian factory typically pays for ERP (licence + implementation)

### Takeaway
No survey gives "typical ERP spend per Indian factory of 100–500 people". Triangulating vendor and partner price lists gives a **first-year cost of about ₹5–25 L for Odoo or SAP B1 at small-to-medium scale, and ₹20 L–₹1.5 cr for NetSuite or Dynamics**. Annual running cost (licence plus AMC) is roughly **₹3–30 L/year** depending on vendor and user count. Below that, SME ERP-lite (TranZact or Zoho) costs **₹0.6–3.6 L/year**. XELOR's **₹6 L/year all-in per plant** sits in the gap between the two.

### Cited Findings
- SAP B1: medium business (5–50 users) **₹14–75 L** total; small (up to 5 users) ₹8–12 L — [Praxis](https://praxisinfosolutions.com/blog/sap-business-one-price-in-india-a-detailed-breakdown-of-sap-b1-cost/).
- Odoo: mid-sized company **₹3–12 L** implementation; 20–50 users **₹5–10 L**; full SMB rollout ₹5–25 L all-in; licence ₹580–1,150/user/month — [Braincuber](https://www.braincuber.com/blog/odoo-implementation-cost-india-2026-full-breakdown); [OEC.sh](https://oec.sh/odoo-pricing/india).
- NetSuite: 20 users first year **₹50 L–₹1.5 cr**; basic SMB ₹20–30 L — [Aaxonix](https://www.aaxonix.com/resources/netsuite-erp-india-guide/).
- Dynamics BC Premium **US$105/user/month**; implementation US$10–50K+ — [Cloudfy](https://cloudfysystems.com/blog/microsoft-dynamics-365-pricing-india); [ERP Software Blog](https://erpsoftwareblog.com/2025/10/dynamics-365-business-central-pricing-explained/).
- TranZact Medium or Enterprise **₹1.8 L or ₹3.6 L/year** — [TranZact pricing](https://letstranzact.com/pricing).
- Medium enterprises (51–250 employees) reportedly spend about **US$8,900/year** on software — [Mewayz](https://mewayz.com/en/blog/the-indian-smb-software-market-report-opportunities-in-the-worlds-largest-smb-pool). This is a low-quality source, but at about ₹7.5–8 L/year it is close to the ₹6 L XELOR price.

### Inferences
Illustrative annual licence cost for a 100–500 person plant. Derived; assumes **15–50 named ERP users**, an assumption since most shop-floor staff are not licensed users:

| Option | Annual licence | Implementation / notes | Comparison with XELOR's ₹6 L/year |
|---|---|---|---|
| TranZact Enterprise | ₹3.6 L | — | XELOR is about 1.7x |
| Odoo Custom, 25–50 users | ≈ ₹3.5–6.9 L at list ₹1,150/user/month | plus ₹5–20 L one-time | Comparable licence cost, but Odoo also has a large implementation fee |
| Dynamics BC Premium, 25 users | US$31.5K ≈ **₹27–28 L** at an assumed ₹86–88/US$ (exchange rate is an assumption) | plus implementation | Far above |
| SAP B1, 25 users (perpetual) | ≈ ₹34 L licence (25 × ₹1.36 L) plus about 20% AMC ≈ ₹6.8 L/year from year 2 | plus implementation | Year-2+ AMC alone is about XELOR's full price |

- **Year-1 TCO for SAP B1, NetSuite or BC at this scale is commonly ₹15 L to over ₹75 L.** Positioning XELOR at ₹6 L/year with no large implementation fee is defensible as **about 25–50% of mid-market year-1 TCO**, while being 1.7x the most expensive SME ERP-lite plan.
- For a SOM pricing sensitivity, use a range of **₹3.6 L (TranZact anchor) to ₹6 L (XELOR list) to ₹10 L+ (mid-market licence)**.

### Gaps
- No primary survey of actual ERP spend by Indian factories of 100–500 employees was found. All figures are vendor or partner list estimates, which tend to overstate the high end.
- Real negotiated discounts, and the share of factories on pirated or perpetual Tally with no recurring spend, are unknown.

## 4. B2B directory and listing benchmarks (IndiaMART, JustDial, TradeIndia)

### Takeaway
Paid B2B listing in India is a **low-penetration, high-ARPU, high-churn** business:
- IndiaMART: **about 2.18 lakh paying suppliers out of about 8.8 mn storefronts (≈2.5%)**, ARPU about **₹69K/year**, entry Silver tier **₹32K/year** (after September 2025), and about 7% monthly churn on Silver.
- Justdial: **639,200 paid campaigns (Q1 FY27)**, implying about **₹19K revenue per campaign per year**.

XELOR's ₹2,988/year Verified tier is **about 1/11 of IndiaMART Silver, about 1/23 of IndiaMART ARPU and about 1/6 of Justdial revenue per campaign**. It is priced like accounting software (Vyapar or Busy, about ₹3.4–3.6K), not like a listing.

## 5. SME SaaS penetration and churn benchmarks (how far a vertical SaaS gets in about 5 years)

### Takeaway
No published benchmark for "share of addressable SMEs a vertical SaaS reaches in 5 years" in India was found. Indian proxies suggest **low single-digit paid penetration of the addressable base, even after many years**:
- IndiaMART: about 2.5% of storefronts paying after about 25 years.
- TranZact: about 330–1,000 paying-equivalents from 20,000+ onboarded after about 7 years (derived), against hundreds of thousands of manufacturing SMEs.
- Tally: about 27 lakh customers after more than 35 years.

Monthly churn on low-price Indian SME plans is **about 7%**; annual plans churn about 40% a year; premium tiers churn about 0.5–1% a month.

### Cited Findings
- IndiaMART churn by tier and flat net customer growth: see section 4 — [Medianama, July 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/07/223-indiamart-msme-lending-arm-q1-fy2027-earnings/). The CEO says most churn happens in the first 12 months.
- TranZact: about 8 years after founding (2017), FY25 revenue ₹5.9 cr with a ₹11.6 cr loss, on 20,000+ SMEs onboarded via a freemium Micro plan — [Inc42 financials](https://inc42.com/company/tranzact/financials/); [I amsterdam startup map](https://startupmap.iamsterdam.com/companies/tranzact_1).
- Vyapar: about 10 mn businesses on its user base, but only ₹69 cr revenue and a ₹63 cr loss in FY25 — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/fintrackr/vyapar-posts-rs-63-cr-loss-in-fy25-cash-reserve-fades-93-10819211). This shows the free-to-paid gap in Indian MSME SaaS.
- "SaaS penetration among Indian SMBs below 15%"; Tier 2/3 cities have "41% lower software adoption" — [Mewayz](https://mewayz.com/en/blog/the-indian-smb-software-market-report-opportunities-in-the-worlds-largest-smb-pool) (low-quality source).
- Frost & Sullivan has a "SaaS Adoption in Indian SMBs" study (paywalled; contents not seen) — [Frost & Sullivan store](https://store.frost.com/directory/currency/switch/currency/MYR/uenc/aHR0cHM6Ly9zdG9yZS5mcm9zdC5jb20vc2Fhcy1hZG9wdGlvbi1pbi1pbmRpYW4tc21icy5odG1s/).

### Inferences
- **SOM planning assumption for the XELOR ERP: 1–3% of SAM in years 1–3, and about 3–7% by year 5.**
  - This is optimistic compared with TranZact, and justified only if the agentic AI and supplier network deliver real differentiation and the sales motion goes through clusters or associations.
  - **Penetration above about 10% of SAM in 5 years has no Indian precedent in the evidence gathered.**
- **Listings SOM: up to 10–20% of target MSMEs listed free; 2–5% of those paying for Verified.** Assume 40–60% annual churn on the ₹249 tier, unless it is bundled with ERP buyer relationships, where IndiaMART's Gold/Platinum retention pattern suggests churn could fall towards 10–15% a year.
- For ERP churn at ₹6 L/year, use IndiaMART Gold/Platinum-like retention of **about 6–14% a year** as an optimistic case, and **20–30% a year** as a conservative case for a new vendor. No Indian-specific ERP churn figure was found.]
