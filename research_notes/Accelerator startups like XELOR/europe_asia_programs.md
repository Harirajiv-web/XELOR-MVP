# Startups similar to XELOR from non-US, non-India-only accelerators (EF, Antler, Sequoia Arc, Seedcamp, Plug and Play, Station F, Techstars intl, Startupbootcamp, EIT Manufacturing, SOSV/HAX, Surge SEA, Iterative, Google for Startups non-India)

Research date: 2 October 2026. Method: about 40 web searches and fetches. Several primary portfolio sites (antler.co, sequoiacap.com, thenextweb.com, finder.techleap.nl) were blocked by the network egress proxy, so some program affiliations rest on search-result summaries and are flagged as such. "Program-verified" means a source ties the company to one of the listed programs. "Not program-verified" means the company is a relevant comparable, but I found no source linking it to a listed program. The report writer should keep these separate.

Overall headline: in the listed programs I found **no company that reproduces XELOR's full loop** (ERP + shortage detection + no-login supplier quoting + award-to-PO + receipt/inspection-driven supplier scorecard + shared capacity + factory "passport"). The closest program-verified matches are AI procurement agents for manufacturers (Magentic, Sequoia Arc 2024; Eluvium, reported Google for Startups MENA-T and backed by Turkey's 212), plus managed factory-matching networks (Inflow, reported Iterative). The strongest functional comparables in Europe, SEA and MENA (Tacto, Valuechain, Kreatize, Laserhub, Spanflug, Bababos, Brkz) are **not** verifiably from the listed programs.

## Q1. Which European startups from these programs do manufacturing procurement, supplier networks, or capacity marketplaces? (verify Partful, Orderful-type, Kausa, Valuechain; find others)

### Takeaway
Only one European company with clear manufacturing-procurement overlap is program-verified: **Magentic** (London; Sequoia Arc 2024), which sells AI procurement agents that work inside a manufacturer's ERP and email. **Eluvium** (London/Berlin/Istanbul) is close, but its Google for Startups tie is unconfirmed. On the names to verify: Partful (aftermarket 3D parts catalogues), Kausa (KPI analytics) and Valuechain (multi-tier supplier collaboration, bootstrapped) all exist, but none is tied to a listed program. Only Valuechain is functionally close. "Orderful" is a US EDI company and out of scope.

### Inferences
- Capacity sharing between peer factories, with live capacity visible because both sides run the same ERP, appears absent from HAX/SOSV, Plug and Play and Startupbootcamp portfolios as far as public sources show. The nearest equivalents are centralised managed marketplaces (Kreatize, Laserhub, Spanflug in Europe; Inflow in Vietnam) that route jobs to partner factories, not peer-to-peer capacity networks.
- Plug and Play's value to a XELOR-like company would be corporate pilots (e.g. Startup Autobahn's manufacturing and supply-chain tracks), not portfolio competition.

### Gaps
- The HAX and Plug and Play portfolio databases could not be queried directly, so obscure portfolio companies (e.g. contract-manufacturing marketplaces in HAX's Shenzhen network) may have been missed.
- No funding, traction or status data was found for Startupbootcamp's Hizeem.
