# Startups from non-YC US accelerators/programs similar to XELOR (a16z speedrun, Techstars, 500 Global, Pioneer, HF0, South Park Commons, Neo, Afore)

Research date: 2 October 2026. Method note: direct page fetches of speedrun.a16z.com, techstars.com, southparkcommons.com, startuphub.ai and 36kr were blocked by the network egress proxy during this session. All findings below come from search-result content (including indexed text of those official pages) plus press coverage. Treat traction figures quoted from company-written profile pages as self-reported. Dates are given as reported by the source.

XELOR reference model: an ERP plus supplier network for SME factories. A shortage triggers an RFQ generated from the item master, BOM and quality plan. Suppliers quote through a no-login, WhatsApp-style link. Quotes are ranked by delivery, landed cost and track record. The award becomes a PO and the owner approves it on a phone. Goods receipt and inspection update the supplier's earned score, suppliers share capacity, and the factory's own record becomes a "passport" it can show customers.

## Which a16z speedrun cohort companies (SR001–SR006, 2024–2026) touch manufacturing, supply chain, procurement or supplier networks?

### Takeaway
Speedrun's recent cohorts, especially SR006 (January–April 2026), contain a handful of industrial and supply-chain companies, but none is a manufacturer-to-supplier RFQ network like XELOR. The closest analogue is Vereda (SR006). It is a WhatsApp-native AI procurement agent for Brazilian farmers. That makes it relevant on channel, on the emerging-market buyer and on agent-led purchasing, but it serves farms buying inputs, not factories sourcing parts. Modern Industrials (SR006) automates quoting and order entry on the distributor (supplier) side.

### Cited Findings
**Program context**
- Speedrun is a 12-week accelerator run by a16z from San Francisco and Los Angeles. It invests up to $1M and gives $10M+ in credits. It began under the a16z GAMES Fund and moved aggressively into AI after 2023. — [Wikipedia / search summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andreessen_Horowitz); [genaiassembling substack](https://genaiassembling.substack.com/p/speedruns-pivot-is-the-playbook-what)
- Deal terms: $500K for 10% on a SAFE up front, plus another $500K in the next round within 18 months. — [speedrun FAQ](https://speedrun.a16z.com/faq)
- SR005 had 60+ companies chosen from more than 14,000 applications (under 0.5% acceptance). SR006 ran January–April 2026 in San Francisco on the same $1M structure. — [startupintros SR006](https://startupintros.com/news/2026-04-14-a16z-speedrun-sr006); [a16z SR005 announcement](https://a16z.com/applications-for-sr005-are-officially-open/)
- Commentary says speedrun now targets "boring" industries such as rail, agriculture, commercial lending and heavy vehicles: very large markets with "near-zero digitization". — [genaiassembling substack](https://genaiassembling.substack.com/p/speedruns-pivot-is-the-playbook-what)

**Vereda (SR006)**
- Vereda is an "AI procurement agent for agriculture" built around a WhatsApp-native AI agent. Brazilian farmers use WhatsApp daily at a rate of about 96%, and they buy through text, audio or documents. — [speedrun company page](https://speedrun.a16z.com/companies/vereda); [genaiassembling substack](https://genaiassembling.substack.com/p/speedruns-pivot-is-the-playbook-what)
- Vereda combines the AI purchasing assistant with embedded financing and a nationwide network of field consultants, aiming to cut out intermediaries. It was founded in 2025 in Rio Verde, Brazil, by João Souza (CEO, formerly BTG Pactual agribusiness) and Pedro Galindo (CPTO). — [Briter](https://www.briter.co/companies/vereda); [speedrun founder page](https://speedrun.a16z.com/companies/vereda/joao-souza)
- Self-reported traction: 800K acres in 45 days, representing $155M GMV and $8M potential revenue. — [Briter](https://www.briter.co/companies/vereda); [speedrun](https://speedrun.a16z.com/companies/vereda)
- Funding: a $4.8M seed round closed on 12 June 2026. Investors beyond speedrun were not found. — [Briter](https://www.briter.co/companies/vereda)

**Modern Industrials (SR006)**
- Modern Industrials is "the AI workforce for distribution", starting with the $450B+ building-materials industry. It was founded in 2025 in New York, had 4 employees, and its founders come from xAI, Google and McKinsey. — [speedrun company page](https://speedrun.a16z.com/companies/modern-industrials)
- Its first product, the "AI Sales Engine", automates quoting, order entry and analytics in the distributor's back office. The stated long-term aim is an "end-to-end OS for distribution". — [speedrun company page](https://speedrun.a16z.com/companies/modern-industrials)

**Agent Astra (speedrun)**
- Agent Astra is an AI-native logistics platform "rebuilding freight from the ground up". It claims the service range of a global logistics provider with one-tenth of the workforce, starting with asset-light freight forwarding. — [speedrun company page](https://speedrun.a16z.com/companies/agent-astra)

**Acaysia (speedrun)**
- Acaysia is a San Francisco company in the Manufacturing/Industrials and Deep Tech space. It builds a real-time AI control layer for industrial reactors. — [speedrun companies index (search snippet)](https://speedrun.a16z.com/companies)

### Inferences
- No speedrun company found matches XELOR's core loop of factory shortage, RFQ, supplier quote, PO, goods receipt and supplier score. Speedrun's industrial bets lean toward "AI workforce for a vertical", sitting on either the buyer side (Vereda) or the seller side (Modern Industrials).
- Vereda supports the XELOR thesis that WhatsApp-native, no-app procurement works in emerging markets. Brazil is a close behavioural analogue to India. It is the strongest emerging-market signal in the speedrun portfolio.
- Modern Industrials is the mirror image of XELOR's supplier response flow. If distributors adopt AI quoting agents, XELOR's no-login quote links may receive machine-generated replies, which affects interoperability planning.

### Gaps
- The full speedrun company index could not be fetched (egress block), so SR001–SR005 were not checked exhaustively. Some industrial or procurement companies may be missing, especially in SR004 and SR005.
- Funding beyond speedrun's standard check was not found for Modern Industrials, Agent Astra or Acaysia. Their cohort numbers (other than SR006 for Vereda and Modern Industrials) were not confirmed.
- a16z American Dynamism portfolio: no American Dynamism company doing supplier networks or RFQ automation was found through a program. The search budget went to the named accelerators.

## Which Techstars companies (Industries of the Future, supply-chain/logistics accelerators, partner programs with manufacturers) do supplier networks, sourcing, procurement or manufacturing marketplaces?

### Takeaway
The most XELOR-like Techstars company found is Laibl (Techstars '25). It runs AI procurement agents that vet suppliers, contact them on several channels and return verified quotes, but it serves CPG brands rather than discrete-manufacturing factories. Other Techstars companies are adjacent: KredFeed finances Mexican manufacturing SMEs, and Pikkolo Assembly offers instant-quote PCB assembly. Techstars' hard-tech programs (Industries of the Future, the Fall 2026 class) mostly fund deep-tech manufacturing, not procurement software.

### Cited Findings
**Laibl (Techstars '25)**
- Laibl is an "Automated CPG packaging sourcing platform". It connects brands with pre-vetted suppliers "in minutes, not weeks". Its AI procurement agents handle automated due diligence, project creation, multi-channel supplier contact and hands-on quality verification. — [Techstars Notion profile](https://techstars.notion.site/Laibl-24bf180a1252809daaa9ec1280d6f4ec)
- Two-sided value: brands get faster, competitive and verified quotes, and suppliers get a steady flow of qualified opportunities. Laibl is scaling in beauty and personal goods first, then plans supplements, food, pet and fashion. — [Techstars Notion profile](https://techstars.notion.site/Laibl-24bf180a1252809daaa9ec1280d6f4ec)
- Laibl joined a Techstars Fall 2025 accelerator program, where it was described as an "AI sourcing platform that matches brands with vetted suppliers". — [Techstars newsroom, Fall 2025 (search snippet)](https://www.techstars.com/newsroom/meet-the-startups-joining-techstars-fall-2025-accelerator-programs)

**KredFeed (Techstars 2023)**
- KredFeed is a Mexican fintech founded in 2019 that finances manufacturing SMEs by advancing up to 95% of invoice value. It closed a $1.5M seed round with Carabela, Techstars, 2045 Ventures, The Pitch Fund, AngelHub, Newlin Ventures and Cacao Capital. — [LatamList](https://latamlist.com/kredfeed-raises-1-5m/); [LatamFintech](https://www.latamfintech.co/articles/kredfeed-fintech-especializada-en-financiamiento-a-pymes-recauda-us-1-5m-para-fortalecer-su-tecnologia-y-expandir-su-capacidad-operativa-en-mexico)
- The round is dated March 2025. — [Seedtable](https://seedtable.com/companies/kredfeed/funding-rounds/seed-2025-03)
- Traction: more than MXN 400M in receivables financed for Mexican SMEs. Plans include US expansion around nearshoring and partnerships with B2B platforms. — [LatamList](https://latamlist.com/kredfeed-raises-1-5m/)

**Techstars Fall 2026 class (kicked off 8 September 2026)**
- Pikkolo Assembly brings automated, low-volume PCB assembly to the US, turning uploaded files into instant quotes with fast turnaround. — [Techstars newsroom, Fall 2026 (search snippet)](https://www.techstars.com/newsroom/introducing-techstars-fall-2026-accelerator-class)
- 2DaLoop (Techstars Future of Food Powered by Ecolab) automates downstream supply-chain data collection and triage. — [same](https://www.techstars.com/newsroom/introducing-techstars-fall-2026-accelerator-class)

**Techstars Industries of the Future**
- The program is based in Knoxville, Tennessee, and run with Oak Ridge National Laboratory, TVA and the University of Tennessee. It funds about 10 hard-tech startups per class (AI, advanced manufacturing, quantum, 5G, biotech, clean energy) for 13 weeks, with up to $120K each. — [ORNL](https://www.ornl.gov/news/application-techstars-industries-future-accelerator-opens); [fundsforNGOs](https://www2.fundsforngos.org/environment/techstars-industries-of-the-future-accelerator/)
- The 2024 class included AutoMetrics (industrial automation and welding inspection) and Dig Robotics (excavator guidance). These are manufacturing technology, not procurement. — [Techstars newsroom (search snippet)](https://www.techstars.com/newsroom/techstars-industries-of-the-future-accelerator-announces-class-of-2024)

### Inferences
- Laibl validates the "AI writes the RFQ and contacts suppliers on several channels" part of XELOR. It is marketplace-led, though: Laibl brings the supplier pool. XELOR is ERP-led, triggered by the factory's own BOM and shortage. Laibl does not appear to build an earned supplier record from goods receipt and inspection.
- KredFeed is a possible partner pattern for XELOR's supplier side rather than a competitor. Supplier-performance passports could feed invoice-financing risk scoring.

### Gaps
- Techstars portfolio pages could not be fetched, so legacy Techstars logistics and supply-chain programs were not checked exhaustively. These include Techstars Logistics (Atlanta), Techstars Detroit (mobility/manufacturing) and Techstars Farm to Fork, plus older alumni in manufacturing marketplaces.
- Laibl's funding, location and Techstars program city were not found. Pikkolo Assembly's funding and location were not found.
- No Techstars company running a manufacturer supplier network with live capacity sharing was found.

## Which 500 Global, Pioneer, HF0, South Park Commons and Neo portfolio companies are relevant?

### Takeaway
The closest match to XELOR across all programs in scope is Tenkara (HF0-backed, March 2026). It builds AI ops agents that automate supplier discovery, procurement, compliance and freight for US manufacturers. Factor (South Park Commons and Afore at seed, 2021) also overlaps: it covers ordering, supplier selection, order tracking and supplier payment for hardware manufacturers. Suchama AI (South Park Commons) is an India-focused factory-planning AI. 500 Global's relevant bets are emerging-market B2B procurement marketplaces (Proglix in Indonesia, BetaStore in West Africa). No relevant Pioneer or Neo company was found.
